## Overview

The AI [[concepts/video-generation]] market consolidated into four distinct tiers after [[entities/sora]]'s March 2026 shutdown. Each platform occupies a different niche defined by quality, cost, ecosystem integration, and creative tools. The choice depends on use case, budget, and workflow requirements.

## Analysis

### Professional Production: Runway Gen-4

[[entities/runway]] is the only platform designed for professional post-production. Character persistence via reference imagery, Act-Two motion capture, and the Aleph in-video editing interface integrate into existing creative workflows. The premium pricing (~$0.12/sec) is justified for advertising, narrative content, and broadcast-quality work.

### High-Volume Production: Kling 3.0

[[entities/kling]] 3.0's $0.07/sec pricing and multi-shot capability make it ideal for marketing agencies and social media teams. Multi-shot sequences with automatic transitions and camera logic enable rapid production of longer-form content (up to 3 minutes). Native audio with lip sync in 5 languages reduces post-production overhead.

### Audio-First and Ecosystem: Veo 3

[[entities/veo]] 3's breakthrough native [[concepts/audio-visual-generation]] eliminates separate sound design workflows. At $0.05/sec (Lite tier), it is the most affordable option. Deep Google ecosystem integration (YouTube, Vertex AI) makes it the natural choice for Google Cloud users and YouTube creators.

### Viral Content: Pika 2.5

Pika specializes in expressive, trend-driven content with Pikaformance (voice/image to performance) and Pikaswaps for creative effects. Optimized for TikTok/Reels format and viral content patterns.

## When to Use Each

| Use Case | Best Platform |
|----------|--------------|

| Long-form (1+ minute) | Kling 3.0 |
| Google ecosystem integration | Veo 3 |
| Maximum character consistency | Runway Gen-4 or Kling 3.0 |
