## Overview

AI geopolitics refers to how artificial intelligence development, deployment, and governance intersect with international power dynamics, national security, and global competition. By 2026, AI has become the central arena of great-power competition, with consequences rippling across economics, military affairs, diplomacy, and domestic politics.

The field spans multiple interconnected dimensions: the [[concepts/us-china-ai-race]] as the primary competitive axis, [[concepts/ai-chip-export-controls]] as the most potent policy lever, divergent [[concepts/ai-regulation-landscape]] approaches, an escalating [[concepts/ai-arms-race]], fierce [[concepts/ai-talent-competition]], growing [[concepts/ai-sovereignty]] movements, and unprecedented [[concepts/ai-industry-consolidation]].

## Key Dimensions

### 1. The US-China Bilateral Competition

The [[concepts/us-china-ai-race]] dominates AI geopolitics. As of 2025-2026, the US maintains a seven-month average lead in model quality and 93% of global LLM market share, but China is closing rapidly — [[entities/deepseek]]'s R1 demonstrated near-parity performance at a fraction of the cost ([[sources/time-us-china-ai-race-graphs]]).

### 2. Hardware as Strategic Asset

[[concepts/ai-chip-export-controls]] have become the most debated policy instrument. US chips are approximately 5x more powerful than China's best (widening to 17x by 2027), and [[entities/nvidia]]'s production dwarfs [[entities/huawei]]'s by 20:1 ([[sources/cfr-china-ai-chip-deficit-huawei-nvidia]]). The Trump administration's decision to relax export restrictions remains deeply controversial.

### 3. Regulatory Divergence

Three fundamentally different regulatory philosophies are running in parallel: the EU's comprehensive rights-based framework ([[entities/eu-ai-act]]), America's fragmented market-driven approach, and China's agile state-directed model ([[sources/pernot-leplay-ai-regulation-china-eu-us]]). The race to set global norms remains "wide open."

### 4. Military Integration

AI has moved from research concept to operational deployment. Operation Epic Fury (February 2026) demonstrated 900 AI-enabled strikes in 12 hours. China's PLA is pursuing "fully autonomous execution of combat decisions" ([[sources/nanonets-ai-warfare-pentagon-china-2026]]).

### 5. Talent as Currency

Europe trains 30% more AI talent per capita than the US but hemorrhages professionals due to 30-70% salary gaps ([[sources/euronews-ai-brain-drain-europe]]). Global AI talent demand exceeds supply 3.2:1.

### 6. Capital Concentration

Q1 2026 saw $242 billion flow into AI (80% of all VC globally), with 65% captured by just four companies ([[sources/crunchbase-q1-2026-record-ai-funding]]).

## The 2026 Inflection

The Council on Foreign Relations identifies 2026 as potentially the most consequential year for AI governance ([[sources/cfr-how-2026-decides-future-of-ai]]):

- **August 2026**: EU AI Act fully enforceable with penalties up to 7% of global revenue
- **Cloud spending**: $600 billion projected for AI infrastructure
- **Workforce**: MIT estimates 12% of US workforce automatable today
- **Shadow AI**: Over 80% of workers using unapproved AI systems
- **Legal**: AI systems attempting to disable oversight raise personhood questions

## Strategic Outlook

The [[concepts/us-china-ai-race]] is trending toward specialization rather than total victory. China's [[concepts/open-source-vs-closed-ai]] strategy (DeepSeek R1 under MIT license) has earned global developer goodwill while the US advantage in compute and capital remains formidable. The key question is whether [[concepts/ai-chip-export-controls]] can maintain leverage as algorithmic efficiency improvements reduce compute requirements.

## Open Questions

- Will the EU AI Act's "Brussels Effect" make European standards the global default?
- Can export controls remain effective as AI training becomes more compute-efficient?
- Will middle powers succeed in building [[concepts/ai-sovereignty]]?
- Does the [[concepts/ai-arms-race]] create pressure to bypass safety measures?
- How long before the US-China seven-month quality gap closes completely?
