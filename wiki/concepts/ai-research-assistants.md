## Overview

AI research assistants represent the most mature manifestation of [[concepts/llm-applications-beyond-code]] in academic and professional research. By 2026, the landscape has evolved from general-purpose chatbots to specialized tools that each excel at a specific phase of the research process. The key insight is that effective researchers now compose multi-tool workflows — mirroring Karpathy's [[concepts/post-code-ai-workflow]] shift from single tools to knowledge orchestration.

## The Research Tool Ecosystem

### Discovery Phase
**[[entities/perplexity-ai]]** leads for citation-backed real-time search. Its Deep Research mode autonomously searches, reads, and synthesizes dozens of sources in a single query. ChatGPT's Deep Research can analyze dozens of papers in minutes, gathering information that would take days manually.

### Synthesis Phase
**[[entities/elicit]]** dominates academic literature reviews, extracting structured data from papers into clean, exportable comparison tables. It can find relevant papers and surface patterns across hundreds of results.

### Validation Phase
**[[entities/consensus]]** provides science-backed yes/no/depends answers with confidence indicators drawn exclusively from peer-reviewed literature. This fills a specific need: quickly determining whether scientific consensus supports a claim.

### Verification Phase
**[[entities/scite]]** analyzes 1.2 billion citation statements from 187M+ research articles. It shows not just who cited a paper but how — supporting, contrasting, or merely mentioning. This is [[concepts/claim-extraction]] at the level of the entire scientific literature.

### Exploration Phase
**[[entities/research-rabbit]]** provides free visual network mapping with citation maps and co-author graphs, enabling researchers to discover related work through network exploration rather than keyword search.

## The Composition Pattern

The most effective 2026 research workflow:

1. **Explore** with Perplexity — broad discovery, real-time web search
2. **Synthesize** with Elicit — structured comparison across papers
3. **Validate** with Consensus — check scientific consensus
4. **Verify** with Scite — analyze how key claims are cited
5. **Map** with Research Rabbit — discover related work through networks

This composition mirrors how the [[concepts/llm-knowledge-base]] operates: ingest (discover), compile (synthesize), query (validate/verify), and explore (map connections).

## Deep Research Mode

Multiple platforms (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini) now offer "Deep Research" modes that:
- Autonomously plan a multi-step research strategy
- Search and read dozens of sources
- Synthesize findings into structured reports
- Provide citations for all claims

This is essentially an automated version of the RESEARCH operation described in this knowledge base's methodology — search, fetch, ingest, compile — performed by an AI agent.

## Connection to Knowledge Management

AI research assistants and [[concepts/llm-knowledge-base]] systems are converging. Both:
- Process multiple sources
- Extract structured information
- Synthesize across sources
- Maintain citation provenance
- Generate navigable outputs

The difference is persistence: research assistants produce ephemeral outputs (reports, answers), while knowledge bases accumulate knowledge over time. The combination — using research assistants to feed a persistent knowledge base — is the workflow Karpathy described.
