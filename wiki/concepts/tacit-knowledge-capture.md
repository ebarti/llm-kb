## Overview

Tacit knowledge is the experiential, intuitive, undocumented knowledge that resides in people's heads -- the "know-how" that experts develop through years of practice but rarely write down. It includes judgment calls, contextual understanding, relationship knowledge, and procedural shortcuts that formal documentation misses.

Tacit knowledge capture has historically been the hardest challenge in [[concepts/enterprise-knowledge-management]]. Per [[sources/helpjuice-km-challenges]], employee expertise disappears when staff depart, and per [[sources/glean-knowledge-silos-unified-search]], knowledge trapped in employees' heads is a primary form of [[concepts/knowledge-silos]].

## The AI Breakthrough

Per [[sources/ek-km-trends-2026]], AI has made enterprise-scale tacit knowledge capture feasible for the first time:

- **AI note-taking tools**: Automatically document meeting discussions, decisions, and action items
- **Automated transcription services**: Convert meetings, presentations, and conversations into searchable text
- **Digital meeting platforms**: Provide the recording infrastructure for capture at scale

KM professionals guide dialogue structure and output validation while automation handles the bulk work. [[concepts/semantic-layer]] implementations identify knowledge gaps and measure program impact.

## Why It Matters

The business case for tacit knowledge capture is driven by:
- **Employee turnover**: When an expert leaves, their tacit knowledge leaves with them
- **Onboarding time**: New employees take months to acquire tacit knowledge that could be documented
- **Decision quality**: Tacit knowledge often contains the context that turns data into good decisions
- **Organizational resilience**: Companies dependent on key individuals are vulnerable to disruption

## Challenges

Even with AI assistance, tacit knowledge capture faces obstacles:
- **Willingness to share**: Experts may view their knowledge as job security
- **Contextual richness**: Tacit knowledge is often situation-dependent and hard to generalize
- **Validation**: How do you verify that captured tacit knowledge is accurate?
- **Knowledge generation flattening**: Per [[sources/ek-km-trends-2026]], as AI automates analysis, senior leaders gain direct access to insights but lose the expert analysis layer -- organizations must deliberately capture and encode expert judgment
