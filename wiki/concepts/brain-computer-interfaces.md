## Overview

Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) are systems that establish a direct communication pathway between the brain's electrical activity and an external device. They read neural signals, decode the user's intentions, and translate them into commands for computers, robotic limbs, or other systems. In 2026, BCIs are transitioning from research devices to commercial medical products, with profound implications for both medicine and the long-term relationship between biological and artificial intelligence.

## Key Ideas

### Types of BCIs

| Type | Invasiveness | Signal Quality | Applications |
|------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| Non-invasive (EEG) | None | Low bandwidth | Consumer devices, basic control |
| Semi-invasive (ECoG) | Moderate | Medium | Research, some clinical |
| Invasive (intracortical) | High | Highest bandwidth | Paralysis, ALS, precision control |

### Current State (2026)

- [[entities/neuralink]]: 12 patients implanted with The Link (23mm, 8mm thick), enabling thought-controlled typing and device interaction
- Neuralink planning high-volume automated production in 2026
- China's Neuracle Technology: first commercially approved invasive BCI for spinal cord injury
- Focus remains primarily medical: ALS, quadriplegia, severe paralysis

### The Technology

Modern invasive BCIs:
1. **Record** electrical signals from thousands of neurons simultaneously
2. **Decode** intended movements or communications using ML algorithms
3. **Translate** decoded intentions into digital commands
4. **Feed back** information through visual, auditory, or (eventually) neural stimulation

Neural decoding increasingly uses deep learning — the same transformer architectures used for language modeling are being adapted for neural signal processing.

### Medical vs Enhancement

A fundamental tension exists in the BCI field:
- **Medical device path**: Restoring function to disabled individuals — regulatory approval, insurance coverage, clinical evidence standards
- **Enhancement/transhumanist path**: Augmenting healthy individuals — cognitive enhancement, brain-AI symbiosis, telepathic communication
- These two paths have different regulatory, ethical, and commercial implications
- Neuralink's dual messaging has been criticized for potentially undermining the medical device industry

### Implications for AI

BCIs are relevant to the neuroscience-AI intersection in several ways:
- **Neural decoding** advances require and advance our understanding of neural coding
- **Closed-loop systems** implement real-time brain-AI interaction (a form of [[concepts/active-inference]])
- **Brain-AI symbiosis** could represent a middle path between fully biological and fully artificial intelligence
- **Data from BCIs** provides unprecedented access to neural activity, accelerating neuroscience research

## How It Connects

BCIs connect to [[concepts/neurotechnology]] (the broader field), [[concepts/neuroai]] (bidirectional brain-AI research), and [[concepts/ai-consciousness]] (if AI becomes conscious, BCIs might bridge biological and artificial minds). ## Open Questions

- When will BCIs move from medical restoration to cognitive enhancement?
- What are the security implications of brain-connected devices? (Neural privacy, hacking)
- How will regulation keep pace with rapidly advancing BCI capabilities?
- Could BCIs enable a form of human-AI intelligence that transcends both?
