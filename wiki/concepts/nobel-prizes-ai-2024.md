## Overview

The 2024 Nobel Prizes marked a watershed for AI in science. For the first time, both the Physics and Chemistry prizes were awarded for work centrally involving artificial intelligence. This simultaneous recognition established AI not as a supporting tool but as a fundamental driver of scientific progress.

## Physics Prize: Foundations of Machine Learning

**Recipients**: John J. Hopfield and Geoffrey E. Hinton

**Hopfield** (Princeton): Created Hopfield networks (1982), an associative memory model inspired by spin glass physics. The network stores patterns as energy minima and retrieves them through relaxation — a direct bridge between statistical physics and neural computation.

**[[entities/geoffrey-hinton]]** (University of Toronto): Extended Hopfield's work to Boltzmann machines, popularized backpropagation for training deep networks, and laid the foundations for modern deep learning. Known as the "Godfather of AI." Notably, Hinton has become one of the most prominent voices warning about AI existential risk.

## Chemistry Prize: Protein Science

**Recipients**: David Baker (one half); Demis Hassabis and John M. Jumper (other half)

**[[entities/david-baker]]** (University of Washington): Over 20 years of computational protein design, creating the Rosetta software suite and ultimately [[entities/rfdiffusion]], enabling [[concepts/ai-protein-design|de novo protein design]].

**[[entities/demis-hassabis]]** and Jumper ([[entities/google-deepmind]]): Developed [[entities/alphafold]] 2, solving the 50-year protein structure prediction challenge at CASP 14 (2020). This enabled [[concepts/ai-protein-structure-prediction]] at scale, with 200M+ structures released.

## Significance

### Intellectual Lineage
The prizes tell a connected story: AlphaFold 2 was made possible only through the foundational neural network work recognized by the Physics prize. Hopfield networks and Hinton's deep learning enabled the transformer architectures that underpin modern AI systems.

### Disciplinary Debate
The awards sparked debate: Is AI physics? Chemistry? Computer science? The Nobel Committee's decision to recognize AI work under traditional scientific disciplines reflects how deeply AI has become integrated into fundamental research.

### Validation of AI for Science
The prizes validated the broader [[concepts/ai-for-scientific-discovery]] movement, signaling to the scientific community that AI-driven discoveries merit the highest recognition.
