## Overview

Neurotechnology encompasses all technologies that interface with, monitor, or are inspired by the nervous system. This includes [[concepts/brain-computer-interfaces]] (direct neural interfaces), [[concepts/neuromorphic-computing]] (brain-inspired hardware), neural decoding (interpreting brain signals), brain stimulation (modulating neural activity), and neurodiagnostics (detecting neurological conditions).

## Key Ideas

### Major Branches

| Branch | Focus | Examples |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Brain-computer interfaces | Direct brain-device communication | [[entities/neuralink]], BrainGate, Synchron |
| Neuromorphic computing | Brain-inspired hardware | [[entities/intel-loihi]], [[entities/ibm-northpole]] |
| Neural decoding | Interpreting brain activity | fMRI decoding, EEG classification |
| Brain stimulation | Modulating neural activity | TMS, tDCS, deep brain stimulation |
| Neurodiagnostics | Disease detection | AI-enhanced EEG, biomarker detection |
| Organoids | Lab-grown brain tissue | Brain organoid computing |

### 2026 Milestones

- Neuralink planning high-volume BCI production
- Intel Loihi 3 entering commercial market
- IBM NorthPole in full-scale production
- China approving first commercial invasive BCI
- Neural decoding achieving real-time language reconstruction

## How It Connects

Neurotechnology is the practical application domain for [[concepts/neuroai]] research. [[concepts/brain-computer-interfaces]] connect brains to machines directly, while [[concepts/neuromorphic-computing]] builds machines that work like brains. Both inform and are informed by neuroscience research.
