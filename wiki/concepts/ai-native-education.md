## Overview

AI-native education is the principle that educational systems should be designed around AI capabilities from the start, rather than bolting AI onto traditional formats. The concept is most fully articulated by [[entities/andrej-karpathy]]'s [[entities/eureka-labs]] company (founded July 2024) and embodies his decade-long evolution as an educator.

## Key Ideas

- **Teacher + AI symbiosis**: Human experts design course materials and set the pedagogical vision. AI Teaching Assistants provide personalized, always-available guidance to students at scale. Neither replaces the other.
- **"Learn by building" philosophy**: Students construct working systems from scratch (autograd engines, language models, tokenizers) rather than studying theory in isolation. Every concept is validated through implementation.
- **Open materials**: Following the pattern of CS231n and Zero to Hero, course materials are freely available online, with optional cohort-based experiences (digital and physical) for structured learning.
- **Scaling the best teachers**: A single world-class instructor could theoretically reach millions through their AI-amplified course, maintaining quality that currently requires 1:1 tutoring.

## Criticisms

- Data privacy concerns with AI monitoring student learning
- Risk of reducing the student-teacher relationship to a transactional exchange
- Dependency on LLM capabilities that may not be reliable enough for education
