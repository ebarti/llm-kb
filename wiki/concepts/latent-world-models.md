## Overview

Latent world models compress high-dimensional observations (images, video, sensor data) into compact representations and learn environment dynamics in that compressed space. Rather than predicting future images directly (computationally expensive and error-prone), these models predict future *latent states* — enabling efficient imagination, planning, and counterfactual reasoning.

This approach is central to modern [[concepts/world-models]]: from Ha and Schmidhuber's 32-dimensional VAE latent space (2018) to [[entities/dreamerv3]]'s Recurrent State-Space Model (2025) to V-JEPA 2's 1.2B-parameter vision transformer representations (2025).

## Key Ideas

### Why Latent Space?

Raw observations are high-dimensional and redundant. A 64x64 RGB image has 12,288 dimensions; a 720p video frame has 2.7 million. Most of this information is irrelevant for decision-making. Latent world models solve this by:

1. **Encoding**: Compress observations into compact vectors (32-dim in Ha & Schmidhuber, learned categorical in DreamerV3)
2. **Predicting**: Model state transitions in latent space — p(z_{t+1} | z_t, a_t)
3. **Planning**: Simulate action sequences entirely within the latent model ("dreaming")
4. **Acting**: Execute the best plan found through imagination

### Planning Methods

Latent world models enable multiple planning strategies:

- **Forward rollout**: Simulate sequences, select highest cumulative reward
- **Model Predictive Control (MPC)**: Re-plan at every step with latest state
- **Tree search (MCTS)**: Explore branching futures in latent space
- **Gradient-based latent planning**: Optimize action sequences via backpropagation through the model
- **Cross Entropy Method**: Sample and refine action distributions (used by V-JEPA 2)

### Evolution of Latent Representations

| System | Year | Latent Space | Key Innovation |
|--------|------|-------------|----------------|
| Ha & Schmidhuber | 2018 | 32-64 dim VAE | "Dream" training with temperature control |
| MuZero | 2020 | Implicit learned dynamics | No observation reconstruction needed |
| DreamerV3 | 2023/2025 | Categorical RSSM (det + stoch) | Single config across 150+ tasks |
| V-JEPA 2 | 2025 | ViT-g/16 (1B params) | Self-supervised from internet video |
| Genie 2 | 2024 | Autoencoder latents | Interactive world generation |

### The Dream Training Paradigm

Ha and Schmidhuber showed that agents can be trained entirely within the world model's "dreams" — synthesized environments generated by the MDN-RNN. The temperature parameter controls stochasticity: higher temperature creates more uncertain dreams that prevent agents from exploiting model imperfections (adversarial policies). DreamerV3 extended this to training actor-critic networks entirely within imagined trajectories across 150+ diverse tasks.

## How It Connects

Latent world models are the implementation mechanism for [[concepts/world-models]] in practice. [[concepts/jepa]] operates entirely in latent space by design — predicting representations rather than pixels. [[concepts/model-based-reinforcement-learning]] depends on latent models for sample-efficient learning. The transition from small VAE latent spaces to billion-parameter ViT representations marks the convergence of latent world models with foundation model scale.
