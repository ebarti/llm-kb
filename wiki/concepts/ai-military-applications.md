## Overview

AI military applications encompass the use of artificial intelligence in defense contexts, from intelligence analysis and targeting to autonomous weapons systems and decision support. By 2026, these applications have moved from experimental to operational, with AI-enabled targeting demonstrated in active combat during Operation Epic Fury.

## Deployed Systems (US)

| System | Function | Status |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Project Maven (Smart System) | Multi-source intelligence fusion (satellite, geolocation, comms) | Deployed across 5 combatant commands |
| Gospel | Generate target building lists | Operational |
| Lavender | Identify individual combatants across hierarchy levels | Operational |
| "Where's Daddy" | Track targets via phone geolocation | Operational |
| GenAI.mil | Pentagon-wide LLM platform | Deployed Dec 2025 (classified Grok) |
| Project ARIA | Army-wide AI modernization initiative | Announced 2026 |
| Phantom | Humanoid robot for defense applications | In development ($24M contracts) |

## Operational Demonstration: Operation Epic Fury

February 28 - March 5, 2026 ([[sources/nanonets-ai-warfare-pentagon-china-2026]]):
- 900 strikes executed in 12 hours (previously required days/weeks of planning)
- 24 of Iran's 31 provinces targeted
- AI compressed the military decision cycle from weeks to hours
- Demonstrated the speed advantage of integrated AI systems

## China's Military AI

- PLA doctrinal framework: "intelligentized warfare" — AI as organizing principle for the entire military
- AI decision-support systems to compensate for perceived command weaknesses
- Goal: "fully autonomous execution of combat decisions" (March 2025)
- Integration of domestic [[entities/huawei]] chips for "algorithmic sovereignty"
- Critical limitation: zero combat testing since 1979

## Other Nations

- **India**: Project Anumaan — AI meteorological system for artillery targeting (200km range) during 2025 India-Pakistan conflict
- **Israel**: "Operational Data and AI Factory" for drone strikes across multiple theaters (Gaza, Lebanon, Iran)

## Ethical Concerns

The deployment of AI in military targeting raises profound ethical questions that intersect with [[concepts/ai-safety]]:
- Systems like Lavender identify individuals for strikes with limited human oversight
- Anthropic was removed from Pentagon contracts during active strikes, exposing private-company dependency
- No international treaty governs AI weapons (unlike nuclear, chemical, biological weapons)
- Speed compression may reduce time for human moral judgment
