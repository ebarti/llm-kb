## Overview

Audio-visual generation refers to the synthesis of synchronized video and audio from a single input (typically text), eliminating the traditional separation between visual and audio production. This convergence represents one of the most significant developments in generative AI in 2026, transforming post-production workflows.

## Key Ideas

### Unified Generation

Rather than generating video and audio separately, new systems produce both simultaneously:
- **[[entities/veo]] 3**: Generates synchronized sound effects, ambient audio, and character dialogue alongside video from a single text prompt
- **[[entities/kling]] 3.0**: Produces native audio with character dialogue and precise lip synchronization in 5 languages

### Post-Production Disruption

Traditional video production requires separate workflows for video, sound effects, music, dialogue, and ambient audio. Audio-visual generation collapses these into a single generation step, which according to the Artlist AI Trend Report 2026, has led teams to report 5-10x content production with the same resources.

### Technical Challenges

Audio-visual synchronization requires:
- Temporal alignment of audio and visual events
- Physical plausibility (sounds matching visible actions)
- Lip synchronization for dialogue
- Spatial audio matching visual scene composition
- Consistent emotional tone across modalities

## How It Connects

Audio-visual generation represents the convergence of [[concepts/video-generation]] and [[concepts/audio-generation]], enabled by the same [[concepts/diffusion-transformer]] architectures that handle both modalities. It builds on advances in both [[concepts/image-generation]] (visual quality) and speech/music synthesis (audio quality).
