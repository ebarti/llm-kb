## Overview

PARA is [[entities/tiago-forte]]'s system for organizing all digital information into four categories based on **actionability** rather than subject matter:

- **Projects**: Short-term efforts with a specific goal and deadline (e.g., "Launch website redesign," "Write quarterly report")
- **Areas**: Long-term responsibilities with standards to maintain but no end date (e.g., "Health," "Finances," "Team management")
- **Resources**: Topics of general interest for future reference (e.g., "Machine learning," "Photography techniques")
- **Archive**: Inactive items from any of the three above categories

## Why Actionability Over Topic

Traditional knowledge organization uses subject-based taxonomies (like a library). Forte argues this fails for personal knowledge because:

1. **Topics are ambiguous**: Does a note about "ML in healthcare" go in "Machine Learning" or "Healthcare"?
2. **Taxonomies require maintenance**: Categories need constant reorganization as interests evolve
3. **Filing is cognitively expensive**: Every save requires a classification decision
4. **Retrieval is topic-dependent**: You can only find notes if you remember which category you filed them in

PARA instead mirrors how work actually flows: information enters as part of active projects, transitions to ongoing areas of responsibility, relaxes into general resources, and eventually moves to the archive when inactive. The sorting question becomes simple: "What project is this for?" and if none, "What area?" and so on.

## Relationship to Zettelkasten

PARA and [[concepts/zettelkasten]] represent fundamentally different organizational philosophies:

| Dimension | PARA | Zettelkasten |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| Organizing principle | Actionability | Network position |
| Structure | Hierarchical folders | Flat linked notes |
| Maintenance | Periodic review/sort | Continuous linking |
| Optimized for | Getting things done | Generating insights |
| Note granularity | Variable (could be entire documents) | Atomic (one idea per note) |

The two can be complementary: PARA organizes your *projects and responsibilities*, while Zettelkasten develops your *ideas and understanding*. Some practitioners use PARA for project management and Zettelkasten for intellectual development within the same PKM system.
