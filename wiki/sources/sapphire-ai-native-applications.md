## Key Points
- AI-native means AI is fundamental, not supplementary — built from day one with models, data pipelines, and learning systems as core components
- Five evaluation dimensions: Design, Data, Domain Expertise, Dynamism, Distribution
- "Gone are the days where having the most data as an incumbent platform drove the greatest technical moat"
- 47+ AI-native apps now generating $25M+ ARR
- Winners differentiate across multiple dimensions simultaneously, not through incremental feature additions
- New pricing models: seat-based + consumption-based + outcome-based

## Detailed Summary

Sapphire Ventures (enterprise VC) provides the most rigorous investment-grade framework for evaluating [[concepts/ai-native-design]] applications. Their core distinction: AI-native is not "existing product + AI features" but "product that could not exist without AI."

The **Design** dimension explicitly calls for moving beyond chat interfaces to multi-modal interaction models with accelerated feedback loops — aligning with [[entities/julie-zhuo]]'s critique of conversational UI as insufficient.

The **Data** dimension argues that data moats have shifted: it is no longer about having the most data but about having *proprietary* data that competitors cannot replicate. This connects to [[concepts/data-quality-bottleneck]] — quality and uniqueness matter more than volume.

**Domain Expertise** is the dimension most relevant to [[concepts/knowledge-base-product-gap]]: the ability to marry foundational model knowledge with organization-specific insights. This is exactly what Karpathy's [[concepts/llm-knowledge-base]] does — combining LLM general knowledge with personal raw source material.

**Dynamism** covers [[concepts/personalization-in-ai]] at the product level — hyper-personalization across users, teams, and departments, with real-time performance/cost optimization.

**Distribution** signals a pricing revolution: pure seat-based pricing is giving way to consumption and outcome-based models, enabling new business models like software-enabled services.
