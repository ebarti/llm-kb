## Key Points
- Independent evaluation of leading AI companies on safety practices, not just model outputs
- Anthropic scored highest overall (C+), followed by OpenAI (C), Google DeepMind (C-)
- No company scored above D in Existential Safety planning — a critical gap given AGI timelines
- Uses benchmarks including HarmBench, BBQ, SimpleSafetyTest, XSTest, AnthropicRedTeam

## Detailed Summary

The Future of Life Institute's Summer 2025 AI Safety Index evaluates seven leading AI companies across 33 indicators spanning six critical domains: Risk Assessment, Current Harms, Safety Frameworks, Existential Safety, Governance & Accountability, and Information Sharing.

The report reveals a fundamental disconnect: companies pursuing AGI within a decade have virtually no plans for existential-level safety. Anthropic leads through human participant bio-risk trials and privacy protections. OpenAI is the sole company publishing whistleblowing policies.

Safety benchmarks referenced include HELM Safety, AIR-Bench, TrustLLM scoring, and Gray Swan adversarial testing. Methodology relied on desk research, company surveys, and an independent six-expert panel assigning letter grades per domain.
