## Key Points

- First fully autonomous AI software engineer (Cognition Labs, March 2024)
- SWE-bench: 13.86% at launch, 7x previous SOTA of 1.96%
- Equipped with shell, code editor, browser in sandboxed environment
- Devin 2.0 (April 2025): price drop from $500 to $20/month, added Wiki and Search
- By 2026, leading agents score 75%+ on SWE-bench Verified (Claude Opus 4.5 at 80.9%)

## Detailed Summary

Devin represents a milestone in [[concepts/agentic-coding]]: the first AI system marketed as a fully autonomous software engineer. Created by Cognition Labs (10-person team, Founders Fund backing), Devin operates with the same tools as human developers — shell, editor, and browser — in a sandboxed compute environment.

The SWE-bench progression tells the story of rapid improvement in the field: from Devin's 13.86% in March 2024 to 75%+ by 2025-2026, with [[entities/claude-code]] (Claude Opus 4.5) leading at 80.9% on the Verified subset. Devin's pivot to $20/month pricing with 2.0 signals the transition from research demo to accessible product.
