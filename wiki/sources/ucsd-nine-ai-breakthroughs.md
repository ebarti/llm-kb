## Key Points

- PHGDH gene identified as Alzheimer's cause (not just biomarker) via AI 3D protein structure modeling.
- MycoBCP: first ML application to bacterial cytological profiling for TB drug discovery.
- Non-invasive heart cell monitoring using AI signal reconstruction.
- Spherical DYffusion: 100-year climate projections in 25 hours (25x faster than current methods).
- ALERTCalifornia + FIRIS: AI-powered wildfire detection and response system.

## Detailed Summary

This UC San Diego feature article demonstrates the breadth of [[concepts/ai-for-scientific-discovery]] across health, climate, and the arts. The most significant discoveries include:

In Alzheimer's research, AI-powered 3D protein structure modeling revealed that the PHGDH gene acts as a cause of the disease (not merely a biomarker), by showing how its "moonlighting" role disrupts brain cell gene regulation. This exemplifies how [[concepts/ai-protein-structure-prediction]] can unlock disease mechanisms.

The Spherical DYffusion model (developed with the Allen Institute for AI) projects 100 years of climate patterns in 25 hours — 25x faster than conventional methods — without massive supercomputers. This complements [[entities/gencast]]'s short-term weather forecasting with long-term [[concepts/ai-weather-climate|climate modeling]] capabilities.

The wildfire detection work (ALERTCalifornia + FIRIS) demonstrates real-time AI application: AI-powered cameras detect smoke, predict fire spread, and share information with emergency responders within minutes.
