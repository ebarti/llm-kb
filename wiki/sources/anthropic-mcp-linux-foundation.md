- AAIF co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI
- Supporting organizations: Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, Bloomberg
- Mission: ensure agentic AI "evolves transparently, collaboratively, and in the public interest"

- Founding AAIF projects: MCP, goose (Block), AGENTS.md (OpenAI)
- Governance structure unchanged — community-driven maintainers continue

## Detailed Summary

In December 2025, [[entities/anthropic]] donated the [[concepts/model-context-protocol]] to the newly created [[entities/agentic-ai-foundation]], a directed fund under the Linux Foundation. This was co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI — notably bringing together competitors under shared governance. Supporting members include Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, and Bloomberg.

The donation came with impressive metrics: over 10,000 active public MCP servers, 97 million monthly SDK downloads, integration with all major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini, Copilot, VS Code), and enterprise deployment support from AWS, Cloudflare, Google Cloud, and Azure.

MCP joins two other founding projects: goose (Block's open-source AI agent) and AGENTS.md (OpenAI's agent specification). The move signals MCP's maturation from a single-vendor project to a genuine industry standard.
