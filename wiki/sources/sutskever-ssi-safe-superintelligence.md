## Key Points

- "We're moving from the age of scaling to the age of research" — breakthroughs now depend on new learning methods, not more GPUs
- Current AI fails at generalization because it lacks evolutionary priors and internal value systems that guide human learning
- Envisions AGI as a "superintelligent fifteen-year-old" that learns any job rapidly, not an omniscient entity
- SSI: $30B valuation, ~50 employees, pre-revenue, no product — pure research focused on safe superintelligence
- Timeline estimate: human-level learning systems within 5-20 years
- His credibility as the architect of the scaling paradigm makes his pivot away from "just scale" extraordinarily significant

## Detailed Summary

[[entities/ilya-sutskever]], co-founder of OpenAI and architect of the scaling approach that produced GPT-2 through GPT-4, departed OpenAI in mid-2024 to co-found [[entities/safe-superintelligence-inc]] with Daniel Gross and Daniel Levy.

### The End of Scaling

Sutskever's November 2025 declaration that "the age of scaling is ending" carries unique weight given his role in establishing scaling as the dominant paradigm. Key observations:
- Pre-training has exhausted most high-quality internet text
- Diminishing returns from added compute in reinforcement learning
- Model size increases no longer fundamentally reshape capabilities
- "It is back to the age of research again, just with big computers"

This aligns with the broader [[concepts/compute-scaling]] wall documented by Epoch AI and others, but Sutskever's framing emphasizes the opportunity in research rather than the constraint.

### The Generalization Problem

Identifies two fundamental gaps in current AI:
1. **Evolutionary priors**: Humans have built-in structure for vision, physics, social reasoning. Models start from random initialization.
2. **Internal value systems**: Emotions provide dense, continuous reward signals. AI receives only sparse training feedback.

This explains why AI excels at benchmarks but fails at basic generalization — a crucial insight for [[concepts/path-to-agi]].

### SSI's Approach

The company's structure is unprecedented: $30B valuation with ~50 employees, no products, no revenue, no timeline to liquidity. Operations split between Palo Alto and Tel Aviv. All staff are researchers or engineers. No sales, marketing, or product managers.

After co-founder Daniel Gross departed to join Meta Superintelligence Labs (July 2025), Sutskever assumed CEO role. The departure stemmed from disagreements over whether real-world deployment feedback was necessary.
