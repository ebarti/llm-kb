## Key Points

- US models captured 93% of global LLM site visits as of August 2025
- China-based LLM visits increased 460% in two months, surging from 3% to 13% market share, driven primarily by [[entities/deepseek]]
- Chinese models trail US models by seven months on average in quality benchmarks since 2023
- Trump administration export rules could give China access to 890,000 Nvidia H200 chips — more than double domestic production capacity for 2026
- China holds a significant advantage in energy availability for AI data centers
- The race shows signs of specialization rather than total victory for either side

## Detailed Summary

This TIME data analysis uses six visualizations to assess the state of the [[concepts/us-china-ai-race]]. The US maintains overwhelming market dominance in LLM usage globally, but the trend line is shifting rapidly. DeepSeek's entry catalyzed a surge in Chinese model adoption that took global market share from negligible to significant within weeks.

The chip dimension reveals a complex picture: while the US leads in AI chip production, policy decisions around [[concepts/ai-chip-export-controls]] could inadvertently accelerate China's capabilities. The energy dimension often overlooked in the AI race gives China a structural advantage for scaling data center infrastructure.
