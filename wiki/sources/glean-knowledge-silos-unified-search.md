## Key Points

- 79% of employees agree information is siloed in their organization; 68% report negative consequences
- Employees spend ~2 hours on redundant tasks and 1.7 hours answering repeated questions due to silos -- nearly half a workday lost
- Fragmented information access can reduce organizational revenue by up to 30%
- Unified enterprise search is the primary technical solution: consolidated indices, NLP for intuitive queries, conversational search assistants, and analytics for identifying content gaps
- Breaking silos produces measurable improvements: reduced redundancy, enhanced cross-department collaboration, faster onboarding, better customer-facing performance
- Cultural change is required alongside technology: recognize teams that dismantle silos, establish comprehensive KM systems, implement user-friendly tools

## Detailed Summary

[[entities/glean]] makes the business case for unified [[concepts/enterprise-search]] by quantifying the cost of [[concepts/knowledge-silos]]. The data is stark: nearly 4 hours per employee per day are wasted due to siloed information, translating to massive productivity losses and up to 30% revenue reduction.

The solution framework combines technical and cultural interventions. Technically, unified search indices consolidate data from multiple repositories, NLP enables intuitive querying, and search analytics identify content gaps. Culturally, organizations must recognize and reward silo-breaking behavior, not just deploy new tools.
