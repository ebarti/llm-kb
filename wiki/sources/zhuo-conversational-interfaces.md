## Key Points
- ChatGPT succeeded because of **obviousness** — leveraging existing SMS/chat mental models — not because chat is the optimal AI interface
- The Blank Page Problem: an empty text box provides no affordance for what the system can do
- The Iteration Problem: chat gets you to 70% but is terrible for refinement — WYSIWYG and direct manipulation are superior for the last 30%
- The Input-Output Problem: input modality (voice, text, image) should not have to match output modality
- The Scoping Problem: AI must acknowledge uncertainty and capability boundaries
- The Personalization Problem (the opportunity): AI should adapt *how* it presents information — visual learners get diagrams, metaphor-lovers get analogies

## Detailed Summary

[[entities/julie-zhuo]], former VP Design at Meta, provides the sharpest critique of chat-as-default-AI-interface. Her core insight: ChatGPT's breakthrough was **the interface** (a familiar text box), not the model. But familiarity is not optimality.

She identifies five problems that make chat interfaces insufficient for "the incredible new product" [[entities/andrej-karpathy]] envisions:

The **Blank Page Problem** ([[concepts/blank-page-problem]]) is structural: a blank chat box puts the burden on users to discover capabilities. Social media learned this lesson — starting with content to react to works far better than empty feeds.

The **Iteration Problem** is the deepest architectural challenge for [[concepts/conversational-ui-vs-structured-ui]]. Natural language is excellent for high-level intent ("write me a blog post about X") but terrible for fine-grained refinement ("move that heading up, make this paragraph shorter, change the tone of the third sentence"). This is why the best AI products will be **hybrid** — conversation for ideation, structured UI for refinement.

The **Input-Output Problem** challenges the assumption that if you type a request, you should get text back. AI understanding should inform modality: sometimes a prototype is better than an essay, a moodboard better than a description.

The **Personalization Problem** is Zhuo's billion-dollar opportunity. Current AI adapts *what* it says but not *how*. Adapting presentation to learning styles, communication preferences, and expertise level is the next step-function in product design.
