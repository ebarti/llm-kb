## Key Points

- Global venture funding: $300 billion in Q1 2026 across 6,000 startups (150%+ YoY growth)
- AI captured $242 billion (80% of total venture funding)
- Top 4 rounds: OpenAI ($122B), Anthropic ($30B), xAI ($20B), Waymo ($16B) = $188B (65% of all Q1 funding)
- US captured $250 billion (83% of global VC), up from 71% in Q1 2025
- China: $16.1B (second), UK: $7.4B (third)
- February 2026: largest single month of startup funding ever ($189B)
- Late-stage: $246.6B across 584 deals (205% YoY)
- 21 venture-backed IPOs above $1B globally

## Detailed Summary

Crunchbase's Q1 2026 report reveals unprecedented concentration of capital in frontier AI. The numbers are extraordinary: a single quarter saw more funding than most entire years in venture history. The concentration is extreme — four companies captured 65% of all global startup funding.

This data crystallizes the [[concepts/ai-industry-consolidation]] dynamic: frontier AI development has become a capital-intensive infrastructure race where only the best-funded survive. The geographic concentration (83% US) reinforces American dominance in the [[concepts/us-china-ai-race]] funding dimension, though China's $16.1B is substantial.
