## Key Points

- AGI by 2027 is "strikingly plausible" based on extrapolating compute, algorithmic efficiency, and unhobbling trends
- Intelligence explosion: millions of AGIs could compress a decade of AI research into under one year
- Infrastructure requires trillions in GPUs, data centers, and power — energy becomes the bottleneck
- AI lab security is catastrophically inadequate against state actors
- Superintelligence provides decisive military and economic advantage — framed as national security issue
- Predicts US government "Manhattan Project" for AGI by 2026-2027

## Detailed Summary

[[entities/leopold-aschenbrenner]], a former member of OpenAI's Superalignment team, published this multi-chapter essay series in June 2024. It quickly became one of the most influential pieces of AI forecasting, shaping policy discourse and contributing to the national security framing of AI development.

### From GPT-4 to AGI (Chapter I)

Decomposes AI progress into three components:
- **Compute scaling**: GPT-2 (4e21 FLOP) to GPT-4 (8e24-4e25 FLOP) — 3-4 orders of magnitude
- **Algorithmic efficiency**: ~0.5 OOMs/year improvement
- **Unhobbling**: Chain-of-thought, tool use, agentic capabilities — unlocking latent potential

GPT-2 to GPT-4 went from "preschooler to smart high-schooler" in 4 years. Another equivalent jump reaches AGI by 2027.

### The Intelligence Explosion (Chapter II)

Once AGI exists, hundreds of millions of copies running faster than human thought could automate AI research itself, compressing a decade of algorithmic progress into under one year. This produces "vastly superhuman" [[concepts/intelligence-explosion]] with decisive military and economic implications.

### Infrastructure and Challenges (Chapter III)

Training runs could require $1 trillion and 100+ GW by 2030. American electricity production must expand "tens of percent." Energy, not capital, is the binding constraint. Current [[concepts/compute-scaling]] approaches face real physical limits.

On security: labs "basically hand key secrets to the CCP on a silver platter." Google DeepMind admits being at "level 0" on a 0-4 security scale. The [[concepts/ai-alignment]] problem remains unsolved but tractable.

### The Project (Chapter IV)

Predicts the US government will nationalize AGI development around 2026-2027 through a Manhattan Project structure. Current governance focused on company regulation misses this inevitable transition.
