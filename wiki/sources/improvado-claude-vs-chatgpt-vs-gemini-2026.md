## Key Points

- No single best AI model in 2026 -- each has clear specialization domains
- Claude Opus 4.6: leads coding (HumanEval+, SWE-bench), instruction-following, long-form writing
- GPT-5.4: leads general reasoning (ARC-AGI 2), strong across all categories
- Gemini 3.1 Deep Think: dominates mathematical reasoning, 1M token context
- DeepSeek: highest actionable recommendation density
- Ideal approach combines multiple models matched to specific tasks
- Claude wins 4 of 7 marketing use cases tested

## Detailed Summary

The Improvado comparison tested four frontier models across seven marketing use cases. Claude Opus 4.6 won content marketing (best tone, headlines), social media management (strategic planning, natural voice), conversion optimization (runner-up to DeepSeek), and landing pages (implementation-ready HTML). GPT-5.4 came closest on analytics but none excelled. The article's key insight is that the three-way rivalry has evolved from "which is best" to a nuanced discussion about specialization -- each model has domains where it clearly dominates.
