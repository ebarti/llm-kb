## Key Points

- Claude is named after Claude Shannon, the founder of information theory
- Model family spans from Claude 1 (March 2023) through Claude 4.6 (February 2026) with 13+ distinct releases
- The constitutional AI constitution grew from initial principles (2022) to 75 guidelines (2023) to 23,000 words (2026)
- Pricing evolved from $15/$75 per MTok (Opus 4) down to $5/$25 (Opus 4.6) -- a 3x cost reduction while improving capability
- Context windows grew from 9K (early Claude) to 100K to 200K to 1M tokens
- Claude Opus 4.6 agents wrote a complete C compiler in Rust from scratch (~$20,000 cost)
- Military use through Palantir/AWS partnership, with "Claude Gov" for classified missions since June 2025
- Anthropic prohibits use for domestic mass surveillance and lethal autonomous weapons

## Detailed Summary

The Wikipedia article provides the most complete public record of Claude's evolution. It documents the three-tier model system (Haiku/Sonnet/Opus) introduced with Claude 3 in March 2024, and tracks each subsequent release through Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 in early 2026. Key product milestones include Artifacts (June 2024), computer use (October 2024), Claude Code (February 2025), and persistent memory (March 2026). The Claude Max subscription tier launched in April 2025 at $100-$200/month. The article also covers Anthropic's government partnerships and the Pentagon supply-chain risk controversy of February 2026.
