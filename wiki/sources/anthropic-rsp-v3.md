## Key Points

- RSP evolved through 6 versions from September 2023 to April 2026
- AI Safety Levels (ASL) modeled after biosafety levels (BSL)
- ASL-3 activated May 2025 with defense-in-depth: access controls, real-time classifiers, async monitoring, post-hoc jailbreak detection
- ASL-3 security includes 17 controls from multi-party authorization to honeypot deception
- v3.0 acknowledges higher ASL safeguards may be impossible without collective industry action
- Influenced OpenAI, Google DeepMind, California SB 53, New York RAISE Act, EU AI Act
- Evaluation interval extended from 3 to 6 months for higher-quality assessments
- Competitive stance: pausing while others race ahead "could result in a less safe world"

## Detailed Summary

The RSP v3.0 represents a significant maturation of Anthropic's safety governance. The key innovation is separating commitments Anthropic can implement unilaterally from those requiring industry coordination. New Frontier Safety Roadmaps require public documentation of security, alignment, safeguard, and policy plans. Risk Reports provide quarterly-to-semiannual transparency on model safety profiles. The document honestly acknowledges that pre-set capability thresholds proved "far more ambiguous than anticipated" and that a RAND report found SL5 security standards "currently not possible."

## Notable Quotes

> "Robust mitigations we laid out in the prior RSP might prove outright impossible to implement without collective action."

> "Having one developer pause while others continued without strong mitigations could result in a less safe world."
