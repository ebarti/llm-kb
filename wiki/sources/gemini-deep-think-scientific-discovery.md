## Key Points

- IMO 2025 Gold: 35/42 points, 5 of 6 problems solved perfectly.
- Autonomously solved 4 open Erdos conjectures (652, 654, 1040, 1051).
- IMO-ProofBench Advanced: 90% (January 2026); FutureMath Basic (PhD-level): 38%.
- Aletheia research agent: natural language verification + iterative revision + web search + failure acknowledgment.
- "Vibe-Proving": human-AI collaboration model using balanced prompting and code-assisted verification.

## Detailed Summary

Gemini Deep Think represents [[entities/google-deepmind]]'s most advanced system for [[concepts/ai-mathematical-reasoning]] and formal scientific reasoning. The Aletheia research agent goes beyond simple problem-solving: it uses natural language verification to identify flaws, iteratively generates and revises solutions, integrates web browsing to prevent hallucinations, and can acknowledge failure rather than producing incorrect outputs.

The IMO gold-medal performance (summer 2025) matched by DeepSeekMath-V2 and followed by ICPC World Finals success demonstrates that AI mathematical reasoning has reached competition-winning levels. More significant are the autonomous discoveries: four open Erdos conjectures solved, with Erdos-1051 leading to a peer-reviewed generalization.

Beyond mathematics, the system made breakthroughs in physics (cosmic string singularity solutions), computer science (settling a 2015 conjecture in submodular optimization), and economic theory (extending the revelation principle). The "Vibe-Proving" collaboration model — where humans guide exploration while AI handles knowledge retrieval and verification — may be the most impactful contribution as a workflow paradigm for [[concepts/ai-for-scientific-discovery]].
