## Key Points

- Born October 23, 1986, in Bratislava, Slovakia; moved to Toronto at 15
- PhD at Stanford (2015) under [[entities/fei-fei-li]], dissertation: "Connecting Images and Natural Language"
- Founding member of [[entities/openai]] (2015-2017), returned 2023-2024 for GPT-4 midtraining
- Director of AI at [[entities/tesla]] (2017-2022), led Autopilot Vision team
- Founded [[entities/eureka-labs]] (July 2024), an AI-native education company
- Created CS 231n at Stanford (grew from 150 to 750 students)
- Named TIME 100 Most Influential People in AI (2024), MIT TR Innovators Under 35 (2020)
- Coined "[[concepts/vibe-coding]]" (February 2025)

## Detailed Summary

Andrej Karpathy's career traces a path through the most consequential AI organizations of the past decade. His PhD work at Stanford's Vision Lab under Fei-Fei Li focused on connecting images and natural language — an intersection that would define much of modern multimodal AI. While at Stanford, he created CS 231n (Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition), which became one of the university's largest and most influential courses, with freely available lectures reaching hundreds of thousands online.

As a founding member of OpenAI in 2015, Karpathy contributed to early generative model and deep RL research. He moved to Tesla in June 2017 as Director of AI, where he led the computer vision team responsible for Autopilot — handling data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip. He reported directly to Elon Musk. He departed Tesla in July 2022, returned to OpenAI in February 2023 to work on GPT-4 midtraining and synthetic data, then left again in February 2024 to found Eureka Labs.

An unusual biographical note: before his AI career, Karpathy ran a popular YouTube channel ("badmephisto") for Rubik's cube tutorials, garnering over 9 million views — an early indicator of his gift for education.
