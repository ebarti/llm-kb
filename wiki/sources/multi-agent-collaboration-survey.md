## Key Points

- Five-dimension taxonomy: actors, types, structures, strategies, coordination protocols
- Three collaboration types: cooperation, competition, coopetition
- Structures range from peer-to-peer to centralized to distributed
- Natural language as universal coordination medium enables emergent behaviors
- Common pattern: planner-worker architecture for task decomposition

## Detailed Summary

This January 2025 survey (arXiv:2501.06322) provides a comprehensive taxonomy of how [[concepts/multi-agent-systems]] collaborate. The five-dimension framework (Actors, Types, Structures, Strategies, Coordination Protocols) enables systematic comparison of different multi-agent approaches.

The three collaboration types — cooperation (shared goals), competition (adversarial improvement), and coopetition (mixed) — capture the full spectrum of agent interactions. The five essential components (Profile, Perception, Self-action, Mutual interaction, Evolution) provide a blueprint for designing new multi-agent systems.

Practical frameworks analyzed include CAMEL (role-playing), AutoGen (flexible communication), and CrewAI (task-oriented orchestration).
