## Key Points
- DeepSeek V3.2: 685B params, MIT license, gold-medal on International Math Olympiad
- Sparse Attention (DSA) reduces inference costs by ~70%
- 32B distilled variant preserves step-by-step reasoning for modest hardware
- V4 (expected): 1T total / 32B active, multimodal, 1M context, trained on Huawei Ascend
- Chinese organizations released 1,500+ open LLMs by mid-2025
- Five families reached frontier simultaneously: DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, Mistral
- Shift from pure scale toward efficiency, multilingual capability, specialization

## Detailed Summary

[[entities/deepseek]]'s impact on the open-source LLM landscape has been transformative. The R1 model (January 2025) demonstrated ChatGPT-level reasoning at dramatically lower training costs, triggering what the industry calls the "DeepSeek moment." V3.2 built on this with MIT licensing and gold-medal math performance.

The hardware independence story is equally significant: DeepSeek V4 is being trained on Huawei Ascend chips rather than NVIDIA, demonstrating that frontier AI development is no longer dependent on a single hardware vendor.

The broader impact: DeepSeek catalyzed a wave of open releases from Chinese labs, making the frontier open-source ecosystem genuinely competitive and global.
