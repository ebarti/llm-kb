## Key Points

- Founded July 16, 2024 by [[entities/andrej-karpathy]]
- Mission: "Building a new kind of school that is AI native"
- Model: Human teacher designs materials, AI Teaching Assistant guides students at scale
- First product: LLM101n — build a Storyteller AI from scratch (Python, C, CUDA)
- ~17 chapters from language model fundamentals to multimodal extensions
- Materials openly available, with both digital and physical cohorts
- Represents formalization of Karpathy's lifelong education mission

## Detailed Summary

Eureka Labs represents the culmination of Karpathy's decade-long trajectory as an educator. From CS 231n at Stanford to the Zero to Hero YouTube series, each project expanded his educational reach. Eureka Labs formalizes this into a company with an ambitious vision: redesigning education around AI from the ground up, not as an afterthought.

The core model is Teacher + AI symbiosis. Human instructors design the curriculum and create the course materials, but an AI Teaching Assistant — optimized for the specific course content — handles the scaling challenge. A single expert teacher could theoretically reach millions of students through their AI-amplified course, maintaining high-quality personalized guidance.

LLM101n, the first course, embodies Karpathy's "learn by building" philosophy. Students construct a complete LLM capable of generating illustrated short stories, progressing through ~17 chapters from foundational concepts to advanced topics like multimodal extensions. The technology stack (Python, C, CUDA) mirrors Karpathy's own projects, ensuring students work at every level of abstraction.
