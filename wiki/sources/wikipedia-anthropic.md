## Key Points

- Founded 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and 5 other ex-OpenAI researchers who left over safety concerns
- Structured as a public benefit corporation with a Long-Term Benefit Trust
- Valuation trajectory: $580M (2022) to $61.5B (March 2025) to $380B (February 2026)
- ~2,500 employees as of 2026
- Major investors: Amazon ($8B total), Google ($2B), Nvidia/Microsoft ($15B)
- Pentagon designated Anthropic a "supply chain risk" in February 2026 after company refused to remove surveillance/weapons restrictions

## Detailed Summary

The article chronicles Anthropic's rise from an AI safety startup to one of the most valuable private companies in the world. The co-founders -- Dario Amodei (CEO), Daniela Amodei (President), Jared Kaplan, Jack Clark, Chris Olah, Ben Mann, Sam McCandlish, and Tom Brown -- left OpenAI in 2020 due to concerns about that organization's commitment to safety. They structured Anthropic as a public benefit corporation that can legally prioritize its safety mission over shareholder profits. The article tracks seven major funding rounds and key partnerships with Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, and Snowflake.
