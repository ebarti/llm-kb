## Key Points

- AlphaFold 2 solved protein structure prediction at CASP 14 (2020), releasing 200M+ predicted structures by 2022 — equivalent to hundreds of millions of years of experimental work.
- 3+ million researchers across 190+ countries have used the database; 1+ million from low- and middle-income countries.
- 35,000+ papers directly cite AlphaFold; 200,000+ incorporate its methodology.
- Over 30% of AlphaFold-related research focuses on disease mechanisms.
- Researchers using AlphaFold 2 showed 40%+ increase in novel protein structure submissions and were 2x more likely to be cited in clinical articles.

## Detailed Summary

The article traces [[entities/alphafold]]'s evolution from a competition entry to a transformative scientific platform. The 2020 CASP 14 breakthrough was followed by the AlphaFold Protein Database launch (2021, with EMBL-EBI), the release of 200M+ structures (2022), and the [[concepts/nobel-prizes-ai-2024|2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry]] for [[entities/demis-hassabis]] and John Jumper.

[[entities/alphafold]]'s impact extends across multiple domains: honeybee conservation (Vitellogenin structure), heart disease (Apolipoprotein B100), and Alzheimer's research. [[entities/isomorphic-labs]] (founded 2021) is building a unified drug design engine on AlphaFold's foundation.

The ecosystem has expanded to include [[entities/alphafold]] 3 (predicting all biomolecular interactions with 76% accuracy on ligand binding), AlphaMissense (mutation impact assessment), [[entities/alphagenome]] (non-coding genome analysis), and AlphaProteo (de novo protein binder design).
