## Detailed Summary

Sebastien Dubois provides the most comprehensive publicly available categorization of Obsidian plugins for 2026. The guide reflects a power-user perspective accumulated over years of vault management and productized through his Obsidian Starter Kit.

The "holy trinity" of power-user plugins is Dataview (query engine), Templater (automation), and QuickAdd (capture). These three plugins together transform Obsidian from a note editor into a programmable knowledge system. Dataview turns the vault into a queryable database; Templater adds dynamic variables, JavaScript execution, and conditional logic to templates; QuickAdd provides rapid capture workflows with macros.

Task management has fragmented into multiple plugins (Tasks, TaskNotes, Kanban, Rollover Daily Todos), each addressing different workflow styles. This reflects Obsidian's philosophy of composability over monolithic features.

Notable newer entries include Journal Bases (leveraging Obsidian's new Bases feature for periodic reviews), Sentinel (automated property management), and Datacore (next-gen Dataview replacement). The backup/versioning category highlights [[entities/obsidian-git]] as a standard practice for version-controlled vaults.
