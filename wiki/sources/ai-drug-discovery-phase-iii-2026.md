## Key Points

- 173+ AI-discovered drug programs in clinical development as of early 2026.
- 15-20 expected to enter pivotal Phase III trials during 2026 — the industry's first major regulatory-stage test.
- AI-discovered compounds show Phase I success rates of 80-90% vs 52% historical average.
- No AI-discovered drug has achieved FDA approval as of December 2025.
- First approval projected 2026-2027 with approximately 60% probability.

## Detailed Summary

The article argues that 2026 is the decisive year for [[concepts/ai-drug-discovery]]. Three leading companies represent distinct AI approaches:

**[[entities/insilico-medicine]]** uses generative chemistry to identify both targets and compounds. Rentosertib (ISM001-055) for pulmonary fibrosis achieved a 30-month target-to-Phase-I timeline (vs traditional 6-8 years). Phase IIa showed lung function improvement of 98.4 mL vs placebo decline of 62.3 mL.

**Schrodinger** employs physics-based AI combining quantum mechanics with ML. Zasocitinib (TAK-279) for psoriasis is already in Phase III.

**[[entities/recursion-pharmaceuticals]]** (merged with Exscientia July 2025) uses phenomics — 2.2M high-throughput biological imaging experiments weekly. Pipeline includes 10+ programs but experienced the setback of REC-994 discontinuation (May 2025).

The FDA published draft guidance (January 2025) for risk-based AI credibility assessment, with joint FDA-EMA principles following in January 2026.
