## Key Points

- Created by Ward Cunningham (inventor of the original wiki), launched 2011/2013
- Fork-based model: users maintain their own copies of pages, preserving individual perspectives
- "A chorus of voices" rather than consensus-based single articles
- Contrasts fundamentally with Wikipedia's single-article-per-topic model
- Wikimedia ecosystem developing parallel federation via Wikibase

## Detailed Summary

[[entities/ward-cunningham]], who invented the original wiki concept, created Federated Wiki as a response to what he saw as a limitation of traditional wikis: their convergence toward a single authoritative version. Instead, Federated Wiki allows users to **fork** any page, modify it, and maintain their own version. Multiple versions of the same topic coexist, each reflecting a different perspective — what Cunningham calls "a chorus of voices."

This represents a fundamentally different model of [[concepts/collaborative-knowledge-building]]. Where [[concepts/wikipedia-knowledge-model]] demands consensus and neutral point of view, Federated Wiki embraces plurality. The tradeoff: richer perspective diversity at the cost of no single authoritative reference.

The Wikimedia ecosystem is independently developing a parallel concept through Wikibase federation: sharing structured data between instances while maintaining authority at each node. Their architecture envisions **underlays** (primary source data), **overlays** (contextual lenses), and **interlace** (connective matrix between knowledge projects). This layered model offers a middle path between Wikipedia's consensus and Federated Wiki's plurality.
