## Overview

Nathan Lambert is an AI researcher who authored the RLHF Book (rlhfbook.com), the most comprehensive publicly available treatment of reinforcement learning from human feedback. Previously at Hugging Face, he has been a leading voice in making RLHF research accessible and transparent.

## Key Contributions

- **RLHF Book**: comprehensive online book covering the full RLHF pipeline from preference data collection through reward modeling to policy optimization
- Advocate for transparency in AI alignment research, particularly around the operational realities of preference data collection
- Detailed documentation of the practical challenges: vendor management, phased delivery, millions wasted on unused data
