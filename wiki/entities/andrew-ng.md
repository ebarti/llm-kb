## Overview

Andrew Ng is a prominent AI researcher, educator, and entrepreneur. He is the founder of DeepLearning.AI and Landing AI, co-founder of Coursera, and former head of Google Brain and Baidu AI. In the context of LLM agents and [[concepts/agentic-workflows]], Ng is the most influential voice advocating for agentic design patterns as a primary driver of AI progress.

## Key Contributions

### Four Agentic Design Patterns
Ng identified and popularized four core patterns that compose [[concepts/agentic-workflows]]:
1. **[[concepts/reflection-pattern]]**: Automated self-critique and iterative improvement
2. **[[concepts/tool-use]]**: Integration with external tools and services
3. **[[concepts/agent-planning]]**: Task decomposition and multi-step reasoning
4. **[[concepts/multi-agent-systems]]**: Specialized agents collaborating on complex tasks

### Architecture > Model Size
Ng's most impactful insight: "GPT-3.5 with an agentic workflow could outperform a more advanced foundational model like GPT-4 using a zero-shot approach." This reframes enterprise AI strategy from model shopping to workflow engineering.

### Enterprise AI Strategy
Consistent advice to enterprises: prioritize building practical applications over chasing model leaderboards. Model costs decreasing ~80% year-over-year make experimentation cheap. "The hardest thing is just building something that works."

### Agentic AI Course (2026)
Announced a DeepLearning.AI course on Agentic AI, calling it "one of the most in-demand skills in the job market."
