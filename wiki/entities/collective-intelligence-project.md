## Overview

The Collective Intelligence Project (CIP) is a nonprofit research and development lab focused on connecting the global public to AI governance decisions. Founded by **Divya Siddarth** and **Saffron Huang**, CIP develops and pilots mechanisms for surfacing, aggregating, and operationalizing collective values in the design and deployment of AI systems.

## Key Contributions

### Collective Constitutional AI
Partnership with [[entities/anthropic]] in which ~1,000 Americans participated in a deliberative process to draft a constitution for Claude. The resulting principles were used to fine-tune the model, demonstrating that [[concepts/ai-alignment-democratic]] is operationally feasible.

### Alignment Assemblies
Since February 2023, CIP has run structured deliberations with AI labs and governments, involving the public in defining what "good" means for AI systems. These assemblies combine representative sampling, facilitated deliberation, and value aggregation.

### Global Dialogues
1,000 participants from 70+ countries convene every two months for real-time conversations about AI's societal role, using Remesh's live conversational platform. This addresses the global scale of AI governance with globally representative input.

### Generative AI and the Digital Commons
Research paper (with arXiv publication) analyzing seven risks AI poses to the [[concepts/knowledge-commons]] and proposing three governance responses: monitoring consortia, commons data contributions, and data-based governance structures.

### Open Evaluation Platform
A collaborative platform for building AI evaluations that test what matters to diverse communities — "democratizing evaluation like Wikipedia democratized knowledge."

## Key Ideas

- **Transformative Technology Trilemma**: Governance typically forces a choice among progress, safety, and participation. CIP argues CI mechanisms enable all three.
- **Value Elicitation Toolkit**: Quadratic voting, liquid democracy, citizens' assemblies, Pol.is, prediction markets
- **CI Corporation**: Proposed organizational model with capped returns, balancing innovation with public benefit

## Partners

Works with RadicalXChange, New Public, Consilience Project, Anthropic, and various government entities.
