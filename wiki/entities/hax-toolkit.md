## Overview

The HAX (Human-AI Experience) Toolkit is [[entities/microsoft]] Research's design library for building AI-powered products. It provides research-backed guidelines and design patterns organized by lifecycle stage.

## Key Guidelines

### First Run
- Make clear what the system can do
- Make clear how well the system can do what it can do (error rates, limitations)

### During Interaction
- Match relevant social norms
- Mitigate social biases

### When Wrong
- Support efficient correction
- Make clear why the system did what it did

### Over Time
- Encourage granular feedback
- Provide global controls

## Significance

The HAX Toolkit grounds Microsoft's copilot UX guidance in empirical research. It bridges the gap between academic HCI research and practical product design, providing actionable patterns rather than abstract principles.
