## Overview

Fei-Fei Li is a professor of computer science at Stanford University, co-director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute, and one of the most influential figures in computer vision. She is best known as the creator of ImageNet, the large-scale visual recognition dataset and challenge that catalyzed the deep learning revolution when AlexNet won the 2012 competition.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: AI researcher, professor
- **Affiliation**: Stanford University
- **Notable for**: ImageNet, Stanford Vision Lab, Human-Centered AI Institute
- **Relationship to KB**: PhD advisor to [[entities/andrej-karpathy]] (2012-2015)

## Role in Knowledge Base

Fei-Fei Li supervised Karpathy's doctoral work on "Connecting Images and Natural Language" — research at the intersection of vision and language that anticipated the multimodal AI explosion. She also created the Stanford Vision Lab where Karpathy developed CS 231n, which became one of the most influential deep learning courses in history.
