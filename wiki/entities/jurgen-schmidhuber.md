## Overview

Jürgen Schmidhuber is an AI researcher at IDSIA and KAUST, known for co-inventing LSTM networks and for decades of work on curiosity-driven learning and world model-based planning. His 2018 collaboration with [[entities/david-ha]] on "World Models" revitalized interest in model-based RL with learned dynamics.
