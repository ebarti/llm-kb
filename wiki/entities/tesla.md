## Overview

Tesla, Inc. is an electric vehicle and clean energy company that, under [[entities/andrej-karpathy]]'s AI leadership (2017-2022), became one of the most ambitious deployments of [[concepts/software-2-0]] in the real world. Tesla's Autopilot system uses a pure vision approach — cameras only, no lidar or radar — powered by massive neural networks trained on fleet data from millions of vehicles.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Technology / automotive company
- **AI Director**: [[entities/andrej-karpathy]] (June 2017 – July 2022)
- **Notable for**: Autopilot, Full Self-Driving (FSD), pure vision approach

## Relevance to Karpathy's Thinking

Tesla was formative for Karpathy's intellectual development:
- **Software 2.0 at scale**: Tesla's camera-only approach is the most extreme real-world application of replacing hand-coded rules with trained neural networks
- **Data quality**: Managing labeling quality at fleet scale informed Karpathy's later emphasis on [[concepts/data-quality-bottleneck]]
- **Custom silicon**: Tesla's FSD chip exemplifies Software 2.0's silicon integration advantage (simplified instruction set for matrix operations)
- **Deployment constraints**: Deploying models to millions of vehicles with over-the-air updates demonstrated real-world ML operations at unprecedented scale
