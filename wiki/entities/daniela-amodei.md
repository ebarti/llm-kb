## Overview

Daniela Amodei is the President and co-founder of [[entities/anthropic]]. She co-founded the company in 2021 alongside her brother [[entities/dario-amodei]] and five other colleagues who left OpenAI over safety concerns. As President, she oversees Anthropic's business operations, partnerships, and organizational scaling.

## Key Facts

- **Role**: President and co-founder of Anthropic
- **Previous role**: VP of Operations at OpenAI
- **Notable for**: Co-leading the fastest-growing AI company ($380B valuation by February 2026)

## Role at Anthropic

While Dario Amodei focuses on research direction and safety strategy, Daniela Amodei has driven Anthropic's operational growth from a small research lab to a ~2,500-employee company. She has managed the company through seven major funding rounds and strategic partnerships with Amazon ($8B), Google ($2B), Microsoft/Nvidia ($15B), and Snowflake ($200M).
