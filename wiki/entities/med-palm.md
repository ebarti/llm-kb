## Overview

Med-PaLM is Google's family of medical large language models, built on the PaLM architecture and fine-tuned for clinical reasoning, medical question answering, and healthcare knowledge tasks. Med-PaLM 2 was the first model to reach expert-level performance on medical licensing examination questions.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Medical large language model
- **Developer**: Google Research / DeepMind
- **Notable for**: First LLM to reach expert-level on medical licensing exams; +19% over baseline on MultiMedQA
- **Successor**: Med-Gemini (built on Gemini architecture)

## Performance

- Med-PaLM 2 exceeded baseline models by 19% on the MultiMedQA suite
- Reached expert-level performance on USMLE-style questions
- Evaluated using both automated metrics and clinician assessments of clinical utility

## Role in Knowledge Base

Med-PaLM exemplifies [[concepts/llm-healthcare-applications]] in its most direct form: a model purpose-built for medical reasoning. It demonstrates the value of domain-specific [[concepts/fine-tuning]] over general-purpose models for high-stakes applications.
