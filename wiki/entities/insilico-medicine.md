## Overview

Insilico Medicine is an AI-driven drug discovery company that uses generative chemistry to both identify disease targets and design novel molecular compounds. It represents one of the most advanced examples of [[concepts/ai-drug-discovery]] in clinical development.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Organization / pharmaceutical company
- **Founded**: 2014
- **CEO**: Alex Zhavoronkov
- **Notable for**: Generative chemistry for both target identification and molecule design

## Lead Program: Rentosertib (ISM001-055)

- **Indication**: Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF).
- **Timeline**: 30-month target-to-Phase-I (vs traditional 6-8 years).
- **Phase IIa results**: Lung function improvement of 98.4 mL vs placebo decline of 62.3 mL.
- **Significance**: One of the first AI-generated drugs to show clinical efficacy.
