## Overview

Wikipedia is a free, multilingual encyclopedia maintained by volunteer editors under the governance of the Wikimedia Foundation. With 60+ million articles in 300+ languages, it represents the most successful implementation of [[concepts/collective-intelligence]] applied to knowledge creation. Its editorial model — open editing, community-governed policies, consensus-driven quality improvement — is analyzed in [[concepts/wikipedia-knowledge-model]].

## Key Facts

- Founded: 2001 by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger
- Articles: 60+ million across 300+ languages
- Editors: Hundreds of thousands of active volunteers
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA)
- Governance: Wikimedia Foundation (nonprofit) + community self-governance

## AI Relationship

Wikipedia occupies a unique position in the AI ecosystem:

**As training data**: Described as "the mother lode for human-generated text available for machine learning." Wikipedia subsets constitute "one of the largest well-curated data sets available for AI training."

**As AI target**: ~5% of new articles showed AI involvement (Princeton 2024). August 2025: speedy deletion for AI articles. March 2026: prohibited AI content generation.

**As AI victim**: 8% visitor decrease in 2025 attributed to AI summaries. Wikimedia Foundation introduced paid API access for cost recovery.

**As AI governance model**: Wikipedia's collaborative editorial model is studied as a blueprint for [[concepts/digital-commons-governance]] in the AI era.
