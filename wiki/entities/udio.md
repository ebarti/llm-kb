## Overview

Udio is an AI music generation platform founded by former Google DeepMind engineers, launched in April 2024. The platform prioritizes audio fidelity and instrumental quality, with output rated "almost indistinguishable from real recordings." Its standout feature is an inpainting tool that allows selective regeneration of specific song sections.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: tool / platform
- **Founders**: Ex-Google DeepMind engineers
- **Notable for**: Superior audio fidelity; inpainting tool
- **Pricing**: $10-30/month (mirrors Suno)
- **Legal**: Settled with UMG (October 2025) and Warner (November 2025)
- **Concern**: Download functionality disabled October 2025, not restored as of March 2026
