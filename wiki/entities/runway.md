## Overview

Runway is an AI video generation platform and the only major tool in the space designed explicitly for professional video editors and post-production workflows. Founded in 2018, the company pioneered AI-powered creative tools and has evolved through multiple model generations from Gen-1 to Gen-4.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: tool / platform
- **Notable for**: Professional-grade video generation with post-production integration
- **Current model**: Gen-4 Turbo
- **Pricing**: ~$0.12/second; $12-35/month subscription tiers
- **Market position**: Quality-first tier

## Capabilities (Gen-4)

- **Character persistence**: Maintains subject identity across scenes using reference imagery
- **Act-Two motion capture**: Converts real-world performance into realistic character animation
- **Aleph interface**: In-video editing capabilities for post-production integration
- **Resolution**: Up to 1080p
- **Duration**: 10-second clips
- **Gen-4 Turbo**: 5 credits/second (vs. 12 for standard Gen-4)

## Market Position

Runway occupies the quality-first tier of the AI [[concepts/video-generation]] market, justifying premium pricing through superior temporal consistency and character persistence. It serves premium advertising, narrative content, and professional creative workflows where [[entities/kling]]'s cost efficiency or [[entities/veo]]'s audio integration are less important than production-quality control.
