## Overview

Melanie Mitchell is a professor at the Santa Fe Institute and a leading voice in the debate over AI understanding. Her "LLMs and World Models" analysis provides one of the most rigorous frameworks for evaluating whether language models develop genuine internal representations of reality.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: person
- **Affiliation**: Santa Fe Institute
- **Notable for**: The Orrery Spectrum, analysis of LLM world understanding

## Key Contributions

- Synthesized three criteria for genuine [[concepts/world-models]]: internal representations, causal structure preservation, algorithmic efficiency
- Introduced the Orrery Spectrum (via Jacob Andreas): lookup tables → maps → orreries → simulators
- Documented pre-LLM cautionary tales of superficial pattern matching
