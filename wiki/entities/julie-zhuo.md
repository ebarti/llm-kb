## Overview

Julie Zhuo served as VP of Product Design at Facebook/Meta, leading the design of products used by billions. She is now an influential voice in product design through her newsletter and advisory work. Her analysis of AI interfaces is among the most cited in the field.

## Key Contributions

### Five Problems with Conversational UI
In [[sources/zhuo-conversational-interfaces]], Zhuo provides the sharpest critique of chat-as-default-AI-interface:
1. **[[concepts/blank-page-problem]]**: Empty text box provides no affordance
2. **Iteration Problem**: Chat achieves 70% but fails at refinement
3. **Input-Output Problem**: Input modality should not force output modality
4. **Scoping Problem**: AI must acknowledge its limits
5. **Personalization Problem**: The billion-dollar opportunity

### Design Philosophy
"Effective translation is the designer's Holy Grail." Obviousness matters more than novelty, minimalism, or simplicity. ChatGPT succeeded because of the **interface** (familiar text box), not just the model.

### The Billion-Dollar Opportunity
Adapting *how* AI presents information (not just *what*): visual learners get diagrams, metaphor-lovers get analogies. This level of [[concepts/personalization-in-ai]] represents the next step-function in product design.
