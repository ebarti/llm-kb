## Overview

Addy Osmani is an engineering lead at Google, known for his work on Chrome and web performance. In early 2026, he published "My LLM Coding Workflow Going into 2026," one of the most widely shared practitioner guides to AI-assisted development.

## Key Contributions

- **10-step LLM workflow:** Spec-first planning, small iterations, extensive context, model rotation, behavior customization, testing integration, human review, frequent commits, agent supervision, continuous learning
- **"Waterfall in 15 minutes":** The concept of rapid structured planning that prevents 80% of AI confusion
- **"Model musical chairs":** The practice of trying the same prompt across multiple LLMs
- **CLAUDE.md/GEMINI.md convention:** Project-level files for customizing AI behavior
- **"Director of the show":** The framing that developers remain in charge while AI accelerates execution
