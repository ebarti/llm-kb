## Overview

Dario Amodei is the CEO and co-founder of [[entities/anthropic]], the AI safety company behind the [[entities/claude]] family of language models. He co-founded Anthropic in 2021 with his sister [[entities/daniela-amodei]] and five other colleagues after leaving OpenAI over concerns about that organization's commitment to safety.

## Key Facts

- **Role**: CEO and co-founder of Anthropic
- **Previous role**: VP of Research at OpenAI
- **Education**: PhD in computational neuroscience from Princeton
- **Notable for**: Leading the development of [[concepts/constitutional-ai]], the [[concepts/responsible-scaling-policy]], and articulating a detailed optimistic vision for AI

## "Machines of Loving Grace" Essay

In October 2024, Amodei published a 50+ page essay titled "Machines of Loving Grace" -- the most detailed public articulation of his vision for AI's positive potential. Key arguments:

- Powerful AI = "a country of geniuses in a datacenter"
- Five domains of transformative impact: biology/health, neuroscience/mental health, economic development, governance, and work/meaning
- AI could compress 50-100 years of biological progress into 5-10 years
- "Entente strategy" for democracies to secure AI advantages
- Framework of "marginal returns to intelligence" analyzing where AI faces physical-world constraints
- Anthropic focuses on risks because they're the obstacles between us and a positive future

Title from Richard Brautigan's poem "All Watched Over By Machines Of Loving Grace."

## Leadership Philosophy

Amodei has articulated that Anthropic's safety focus is motivated by hope rather than fear: addressing AI risks is what enables the radical upside. He structures Anthropic as a public benefit corporation that can legally prioritize its safety mission over shareholder profits. Under his leadership, Anthropic refused Pentagon demands to remove restrictions on domestic surveillance and autonomous weapons, leading to a "supply chain risk" designation in February 2026.

## AGI Timeline and Predictions

Amodei predicts "powerful AI" — defined as systems with Nobel Prize-level intellectual capabilities — could arrive as early as late 2026 to early 2027. This places him among the more aggressive forecasters alongside [[entities/leopold-aschenbrenner]] and Sam Altman. His "compressed 21st century" framework suggests the 5-10 years after powerful AI arrives could see 50-100 years of equivalent human progress.

Key distinction from Aschenbrenner: where Aschenbrenner emphasizes urgency, security threats, and geopolitical competition, Amodei focuses on the positive potential while treating risk management as the prerequisite for unlocking that potential. See [[concepts/path-to-agi]] and [[concepts/ai-optimism-and-abundance]].
