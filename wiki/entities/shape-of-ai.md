## Overview

The Shape of AI (shapeof.ai) is a community-maintained, open catalog of UX design patterns specific to AI-powered products. With 57 named patterns across six categories, it is the most comprehensive public taxonomy of AI interaction patterns available.

## Pattern Categories

1. **Wayfinders** (8 patterns) — Solving the [[concepts/blank-page-problem]]
2. **Prompt Actions** (14 patterns) — What AI can do
3. **Tuners** (10 patterns) — User control over context and parameters
4. **Governors** (13 patterns) — [[concepts/human-in-the-loop]] oversight
5. **Trust Builders** (7 patterns) — Building confidence in AI
6. **Identifiers** (5 patterns) — AI brand and personality

## Significance

Provides a shared vocabulary for designers building AI products, similar to how the Gang of Four patterns standardized software design. Enables teams to discuss AI UX decisions using consistent terminology.
