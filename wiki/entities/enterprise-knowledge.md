## Overview

Enterprise Knowledge is a knowledge management consulting firm that publishes influential annual trend reports on the state of enterprise KM. Led by CEO Zach Wahl, the firm has built credibility through prescient predictions: knowledge graphs in 2019, taxonomy importance in 2020, semantic layers in 2024.

- Annual KM trend reports based on client interviews, RFP analysis, and conference attendance
- Deep expertise in [[concepts/semantic-layer]] implementation

- Enterprise AI advisory with focus on knowledge-first approaches
