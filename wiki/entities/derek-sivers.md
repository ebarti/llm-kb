## Overview

Derek Sivers is a programmer, writer, and entrepreneur (founder of CD Baby) who has become one of the most influential advocates for plain text as a personal productivity and knowledge management system. His essay "Write Plain Text Files" (sive.rs/plaintext) is widely cited in the plain-text and knowledge-management communities.

## Key Contributions

- **35+ years of plain text practice**: Since 1990, Sivers has written four books, 400+ blog posts, decades of journals, and all his code in plain text
- **Articulated the longevity argument**: "You will outlive these companies. Your writing should outlive you."
- **Demonstrated minimalist tooling**: Folders for organization, date/tag fields for metadata, basic text editor — no databases, no apps, no cloud services
- **Influenced the "file over app" movement**: His essay predates and complements [[entities/steph-ango]]'s articulation of the same philosophy
