## Overview

World Labs is an AI startup founded by [[entities/fei-fei-li]] (Stanford, former Google Cloud AI chief). With $230M in funding, the company launched Marble in November 2025 — a platform for generating persistent, downloadable 3D environments from text prompts, photos, videos, or 3D layouts.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: organization (AI startup)
- **Founded**: 2024
- **Funding**: $230M
- **Product**: Marble (3D world generation)
- **Pricing**: Free (4/mo) to Max ($95, 75/mo)
- **Export**: Unreal Engine, Unity formats
- **VR**: Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest 3 support

## Significance

World Labs approaches [[concepts/world-models]] from the creative/spatial intelligence angle, complementing [[entities/ami-labs]]' theoretical approach and [[entities/nvidia-cosmos]]' industrial focus. Marble makes 3D world generation accessible to non-technical users, with applications in gaming, VFX, and robotics training simulation.
