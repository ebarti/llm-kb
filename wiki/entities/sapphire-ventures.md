## Overview

Sapphire Ventures is an enterprise-focused venture capital firm. They developed the most rigorous investment-grade framework for evaluating AI-native applications.

## Key Contributions

### The 5-D Evaluation Framework
Five dimensions for evaluating AI-native enterprise software:
1. **Design** — New interaction models beyond chat
2. **Data** — Proprietary datasets as the new moat
3. **Domain Expertise** — Marrying global model knowledge with local organizational insights
4. **Dynamism** — Hyper-personalization and real-time optimization
5. **Distribution** — Flexible pricing (seat + consumption + outcome-based)

### Market Data
- $8.5B invested in GenAI-native applications through October 2024
- 47+ AI-native apps generating $25M+ ARR
- Tracked companies: Abridge, Glean, Perplexity, Cognition
