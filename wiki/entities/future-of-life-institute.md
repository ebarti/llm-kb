## Overview

The Future of Life Institute (FLI) is a research organization focused on reducing existential and catastrophic risks from advanced technologies, including artificial intelligence. Its most influential contribution to AI safety evaluation is the AI Safety Index.

## AI Safety Index

Published biannually, the AI Safety Index provides independent assessments of leading AI companies' safety practices. The Summer 2025 edition evaluated seven companies across 33 indicators in six domains: Risk Assessment, Current Harms, Safety Frameworks, Existential Safety, Governance & Accountability, and Information Sharing.

Key finding: no evaluated company scored above D in Existential Safety planning, despite many pursuing AGI within a decade ([[sources/fli-ai-safety-index-2025]]).
