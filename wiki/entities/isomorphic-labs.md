## Overview

Isomorphic Labs is a drug discovery company founded by [[entities/demis-hassabis]] in 2021 to apply [[entities/alphafold]]'s structural biology capabilities to pharmaceutical development. It represents the commercial arm of DeepMind's AI-for-science effort in drug discovery.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Organization / pharmaceutical company
- **Founded**: 2021 by Demis Hassabis
- **Parent relationship**: Alphabet subsidiary (separate from Google DeepMind)
- **Notable for**: Developing a unified drug design engine using AlphaFold technology
