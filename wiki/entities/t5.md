## Overview

T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer), introduced by Raffel et al. at Google in 2019, uses the full encoder-decoder [[concepts/transformer-architecture]] and frames every NLP task as a text-to-text problem. Classification becomes "sentiment: positive"; translation becomes text-in, text-out; summarization becomes document-in, summary-out.

## Architecture

- **Encoder-decoder**: Full original Transformer architecture
- **Bidirectional encoder + unidirectional decoder**
- **Relative position biases** (instead of absolute or sinusoidal positional encodings)
- **Tokenization**: SentencePiece (language-agnostic)
- **Training data**: Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus (C4)

## Pre-training Objective

**Span denoising**: Replace random spans of text with sentinel tokens; model predicts the original spans. More efficient than BERT's single-token masking because it processes spans in one prediction.

## Sizes

T5-small (60M), T5-base (220M), T5-large (770M), T5-3B, T5-11B

## Legacy

T5's text-to-text framing influenced the instruction-tuning paradigm (FLAN-T5). The encoder-decoder architecture served as the basis for the [[entities/switch-transformer]] (1.6T parameter MoE variant). While decoder-only models have become dominant for general LLMs, T5-style architectures remain valuable for translation, summarization, and structured generation.
