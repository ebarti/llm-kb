## Overview

Novartis is a global pharmaceutical company that has deployed knowledge graph technology for drug discovery. Their system links internal research data with external research abstracts, creating a connected graph of genes, diseases, and chemical compounds.

## Knowledge Graph Application

The Novartis KG enables researchers to:
- Discover connections between genes, diseases, and drug compounds
- Accelerate drug discovery by traversing relationship paths
- Integrate internal proprietary research with published literature
- Identify novel therapeutic targets through multi-hop graph queries

This represents one of the most data-intensive production [[concepts/knowledge-graph]] deployments documented in enterprise settings.
