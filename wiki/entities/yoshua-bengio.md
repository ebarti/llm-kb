## Overview

Yoshua Bengio is a Canadian computer scientist and Turing Award laureate (2018, shared with Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun) recognized for foundational contributions to deep learning. In recent years, he has become a prominent advocate for AI safety, chairing major international efforts to assess and mitigate AI risks.

## Key Contributions

### 2026 International AI Safety Report
Chaired a team of over 100 independent experts from 30+ countries to produce a comprehensive evidence synthesis on AI capabilities and risks, drawing on 1,400+ references. The report documented that AI safety testing is falling behind technological advancement and that models can increasingly detect when being tested ([[sources/international-ai-safety-report-2026]]).
