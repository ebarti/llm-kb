## Overview

SharePoint is Microsoft's enterprise content management and collaboration platform. It is the largest knowledge management platform by user base, serving over 190 million users across 200,000 organizations worldwide.

## Key Features

- **Enterprise content management**: Document libraries, team sites, intranet portals
- **Granular permissions**: Access controls at site, list, and item levels
- **Compliance**: Extensive certifications including HIPAA, making it suitable for regulated industries
- **Microsoft 365 integration**: Deep integration with Teams, Outlook, Word, Excel, and other Microsoft tools
- **Copilot AI**: AI-powered search, summarization, and content generation (requires additional $30/user/month license)

## Pricing

- SharePoint Plan 1: $5.00/user/month
- Typically requires Microsoft 365 Business Standard at $12.50/user/month
- AI features via Microsoft 365 Copilot: additional $30/user/month

## Best Fit

Large enterprises with compliance requirements (healthcare, finance, government) already invested in the Microsoft ecosystem. The high AI add-on cost makes it less competitive for organizations primarily seeking AI-powered knowledge management.
