## Overview

Boston Dynamics is a pioneer in advanced robotics, known for creating highly dynamic robots including Atlas (humanoid), Spot (quadruped), and Stretch (warehouse logistics). In 2024, the company unveiled the all-new Electric Atlas, an enterprise-grade [[concepts/humanoid-robots]] designed for industrial tasks.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: organization
- **Founded**: 1992 (spin-off from MIT)
- **Parent**: Hyundai Motor Group (acquired 2021)
- **Key Robots**: Atlas (humanoid), Spot (quadruped), Stretch (logistics)
- **Atlas price target**: $140,000-150,000
- **Commercial launch**: 2026-2028

## Recent Developments (2025-2026)

- **Electric Atlas**: Full hardware redesign for commercial deployment. Enterprise-grade, industrial tasks (material handling, order fulfillment).
- **Google DeepMind Partnership**: Integrating Gemini Robotics AI into Atlas, enabling the robot to reason through complex instructions and operate in unstructured environments.
- **NVIDIA GR00T**: Early adopter of [[entities/nvidia-groot]] N1 foundation model.
