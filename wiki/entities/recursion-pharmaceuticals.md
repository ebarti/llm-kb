## Overview

Recursion Pharmaceuticals is an AI-driven drug discovery company that uses phenomics — high-throughput biological imaging — to screen for drug candidates at massive scale. The July 2025 merger with Exscientia created the largest combined AI drug discovery entity by pipeline breadth and proprietary data.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Organization / pharmaceutical company
- **Founded**: 2013, Salt Lake City
- **Notable for**: Phenomics approach to drug discovery; largest AI drug pipeline post-Exscientia merger

## Approach: Phenomics

- Runs 2.2 million high-throughput biological imaging experiments per week.
- AI analyzes cellular phenotype changes to identify molecular effects.
- Screens for unexpected biological activity at massive scale.
- Generates one of the world's largest biological datasets.

## Pipeline (as of 2026)

- 10+ clinical and preclinical programs (combined with Exscientia).
- **REC-394**: C. difficile infection (Phase II readout expected 2026).
- **REC-1245**: Lymphoma degrader (2026 readout).
- **REC-994**: Discontinued May 2025 after disappointing long-term efficacy data.
