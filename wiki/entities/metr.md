## Overview

METR (Model Evaluation & Threat Research) is an AI safety research organization focused on measuring AI capabilities and their real-world impact. Their most influential contribution to the developer tools space is the randomized controlled trial measuring AI's impact on experienced open-source developer productivity.

## Key Contributions

- **Early 2025 RCT:** 16 developers, 246 tasks, finding 19% slowdown with AI tools
- **Perception-reality gap:** Documented that developers believed AI helped (20% faster) while it actually slowed them down
- **Follow-up study (2026):** Updated methodology with estimated speedup of -18% (CI: -38% to +9%)
- **Open research:** Published full methodology and data on GitHub

## Significance

The METR study is the most frequently cited evidence in debates about AI developer productivity. Its RCT design is uniquely rigorous compared to self-reported surveys or benchmark-only evaluations, making it the gold standard for productivity measurement in this domain.
