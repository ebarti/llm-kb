## Overview

Confluence is Atlassian's enterprise wiki and documentation platform, designed for structured knowledge management. It excels at technical documentation and is deeply integrated with the Atlassian ecosystem, particularly Jira.

## Key Features

- **Structured wiki**: Page hierarchy designed for organized technical documentation
- **Rovo AI**: Atlassian Intelligence providing conversational search, 20+ pre-built agents for documentation tasks (summarization, meeting notes, action items, KB search)
- **Jira integration**: 76% of customers ship projects faster when connecting Jira to Confluence
- **AI content maintenance**: AI spots outdated pages and broken links
- **Included at Premium tier**: Rovo AI without per-user add-ons

## Pricing

- Standard: $5.42/user/month
- Premium (with Rovo AI): higher tier pricing

## Best Fit

Teams using Jira with technical documentation needs. Engineering organizations that want structured, searchable documentation tightly connected to their project tracking.
