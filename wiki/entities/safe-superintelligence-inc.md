## Overview

Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI) is an AI research company founded in June 2024 by [[entities/ilya-sutskever]], Daniel Gross, and Daniel Levy. The company's singular mission — building safe superintelligent AI — is pursued with no side products, no API platform, no productivity tools, and no commercial deployment. This makes SSI the purest expression of the "safety-first superintelligence" thesis in the AI landscape.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Organization (AI research lab)
- **Founded**: June 19, 2024
- **CEO**: [[entities/ilya-sutskever]] (since July 2025)
- **Employees**: ~50 (all researchers/engineers, no sales/marketing/product)
- **Locations**: Palo Alto, California and Tel Aviv, Israel
- **Valuation**: $30 billion (Series B, March 2025)
- **Revenue**: Zero
- **Products**: None

## Infrastructure

Partnership with Google Cloud for TPU access (April 2025). Technical approach remains undisclosed.

## Leadership Changes

Daniel Gross departed in July 2025 to join Meta's Superintelligence Labs, following disagreements over whether empirical feedback from real-world deployment was necessary for progress. This is a philosophically significant split: Gross believed deployment feedback was important; Sutskever believed pure research could proceed without it. Meta had also expressed acquisition interest in SSI in early 2025, which was rebuffed.

## Strategic Position

SSI occupies a unique position in the AI landscape:
- **vs. OpenAI**: Sutskever's former company; SSI rejects commercial pressure as corrupting safety research
- **vs. [[entities/anthropic]]**: Both safety-focused, but Anthropic ships products (Claude) while SSI ships nothing
- **vs. DeepMind**: Both research-heavy, but DeepMind operates within Google's commercial ecosystem
- **vs. Meta AI**: Direct competition for talent (Gross defected to Meta's Superintelligence Labs)
