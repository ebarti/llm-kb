## Overview

The Prompt Engineering Guide (promptingguide.ai) is an open-source, community-maintained resource by DAIR.AI that has become the de facto reference for [[concepts/prompt-engineering]] techniques. It covers every major technique (CoT, ToT, few-shot, self-consistency, prompt chaining, RAG, meta-prompting) with research citations, examples, and practical guidance.

## Key Strengths
- Research-grounded: Citations to original papers for every technique
- Comprehensive: Covers the full spectrum from basic to advanced
- Regularly updated: Tracks the latest developments in the field
- Open-source: Community contributions keep it current
