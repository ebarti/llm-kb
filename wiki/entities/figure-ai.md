## Overview

Figure AI is a San Jose-based humanoid robotics company founded in 2022 by Brett Adcock. It has raised over $1.7B across three funding rounds, reaching a $39B valuation by September 2025, with investors including Jeff Bezos, Microsoft, Nvidia, OpenAI, and Qualcomm.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: organization (startup)
- **Founded**: 2022 by Brett Adcock
- **HQ**: San Jose, CA
- **Employees**: ~180
- **Valuation**: $39B (Sep 2025)
- **Key Products**: Figure 01, 02, 03 humanoid robots
- **AI System**: [[entities/helix-vla]] (proprietary)

## Robot Generations

| Generation | Date | Key Specs |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Figure 01 | 2022 | Basic bipedal, external cabling |
| Figure 02 | Aug 2024 | 35 DOF, 16 DOF/hand, 25kg carry, dual GPUs, 6 RGB cameras |
| Figure 03 | Oct 2025 | 3g tactile sensors, 2x camera FPS, 60% wider FOV, wireless charging |

## Strategic Pivot

Partnered with OpenAI in Feb 2024 for language AI, but ended the partnership after ~1 year. Adcock: "large language models had become a smaller problem compared to high rate robot control." Developed proprietary [[entities/helix-vla]] for millisecond-level motor control.

## Manufacturing

BotQ facility targeting 12,000 humanoids/year, using Figure's own robots in production.
