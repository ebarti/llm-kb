## Overview

Neuralink is a neurotechnology company founded by [[entities/elon-musk]] in 2016, developing implantable [[concepts/brain-computer-interfaces]]. The company's device, "The Link," is a coin-sized implant (23mm diameter, 8mm thick) that reads electrical signals from neurons and translates them into digital commands, enabling paralyzed patients to control computers through thought.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: organization (neurotechnology startup)
- **Founded**: 2016
- **Headquarters**: Fremont, California
- **Notable for**: First commercially-oriented invasive BCI with automated surgery
- **URL**: https://neuralink.com/

## Timeline

- **2016**: Founded by Elon Musk
- **2019**: First public demonstration of early prototypes
- **2023**: FDA approval for human clinical trials
- **2024**: First human implant (Noland Arbaugh, quadriplegic)
- **2025**: 12 total patients implanted (ALS, quadriplegia)
- **2026**: Plans for high-volume automated production; commercial launch

## Technology

The Link device:
- Reads electrical signals from thousands of neurons simultaneously
- Translates neural signals into digital commands
- Enables cursor control, typing, and device interaction through thought
- Increasingly automated surgical implantation procedure
- Size of a US quarter (23mm × 8mm)

## Controversy: Medical Device vs Transhumanism

Neuralink occupies a contested space:
- **Medical focus**: Real clinical success helping paralyzed patients communicate
- **Transhumanist rhetoric**: Executives discuss "machine-human symbiosis" and consumer applications
- **Industry concern**: Competitors argue dual messaging undermines regulatory approval and insurance coverage for the broader BCI industry
- **Regulatory questions**: Recruitment of senior FDA official overseeing Neuralink regulation raised eyebrows

## Competition

- **Neuracle Technology (China)**: First commercially approved invasive BCI (March 2026)
- **BrainGate**: Academic research BCI with longer track record
- **Synchron**: Semi-invasive approach (through blood vessels, no open brain surgery)
- **Blackrock Neurotech**: Utah Array-based research systems
