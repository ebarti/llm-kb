## Overview

Ilya Sutskever is one of the most influential figures in the history of deep learning. He co-created AlexNet (2012), which launched the modern deep learning revolution, served as Chief Scientist at OpenAI from its founding in 2015, and was a key architect of the scaling approach that produced GPT-2 through GPT-4. His departure from OpenAI in mid-2024 to found [[entities/safe-superintelligence-inc]] — and his November 2025 declaration that "the age of scaling is ending" — represents one of the most significant intellectual pivots in AI history, given his central role in establishing the very paradigm he now says is exhausted.

## Key Contributions

### AlexNet (2012)
Co-created with Alex Krizhevsky and Geoffrey Hinton. Won the ImageNet competition by a massive margin, demonstrating that deep neural networks with GPU training could dramatically outperform traditional computer vision. This single result launched the modern deep learning era.

### The Scaling Paradigm at OpenAI
As Chief Scientist, Sutskever was instrumental in the decision to scale up language models — leading to GPT-2, GPT-3, and GPT-4. His conviction that "scale is all you need" drove OpenAI's strategy and influenced the entire industry's direction.

### The Superalignment Team
Led OpenAI's Superalignment team focused on ensuring AI systems smarter than humans remain aligned with human values. [[entities/leopold-aschenbrenner]] and Jan Leike worked under him on this team.

### The "End of Scaling" (November 2025)
His declaration carries unique weight:
- "We're moving from the age of scaling to the age of research"
- Pre-training has exhausted most high-quality internet text
- Diminishing returns from added compute in RL
- Model size increases no longer fundamentally reshape capabilities
- "It is back to the age of research again, just with big computers"

### The Generalization Problem
Identifies two fundamental gaps in current AI:
1. **Evolutionary priors**: Humans have built-in structure for vision, physics, social reasoning; models start from random initialization
2. **Internal value systems**: Emotions provide dense reward signals; AI gets only sparse training feedback

Envisions AGI as a "superintelligent fifteen-year-old" — capable of learning any job rapidly, not omniscient.

### NeurIPS 2024 Talk
"Pre-training as we know it will end" — foreshadowed his public pivot away from the scaling paradigm.

## Career Timeline

- **2012**: Co-created AlexNet at University of Toronto
- **2015**: Co-founded OpenAI as Chief Scientist
- **2023**: Led Superalignment team at OpenAI
- **November 2023**: Briefly involved in OpenAI board crisis (Altman removal/reinstatement)
- **May 2024**: Departed OpenAI
- **June 2024**: Co-founded SSI with Daniel Gross and Daniel Levy
- **July 2025**: Assumed CEO role at SSI after Gross's departure to Meta
- **November 2025**: Public declaration of "end of scaling"
