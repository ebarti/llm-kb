## Overview

AlphaFold is DeepMind's AI system for predicting the three-dimensional structure of proteins from their amino acid sequences. AlphaFold-2 achieved near-experimental accuracy in the CASP14 competition and earned a Nobel Prize in Chemistry, making it the landmark example of [[concepts/ai-scientific-discovery]].

## Key Facts

- **Type**: AI protein structure prediction system
- **Developer**: [[entities/deepmind]] (Google)
- **URL**: https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk
- **Notable for**: Nobel Prize in Chemistry; predicting 200M+ protein structures
- **Significance**: Solved a 50-year grand challenge in biology

## Impact

AlphaFold's protein structure database covers over 200 million proteins — virtually every known protein in nature. This has:
- Accelerated drug discovery by providing structural targets
- Enabled understanding of disease mechanisms at molecular level
- Democratized access to structural biology data globally
- Inspired a generation of AI-for-science tools

## Role in Knowledge Base

AlphaFold is the single most important example of AI achieving a scientific breakthrough that eluded human researchers for decades. It validates the "lab-pilot" vision described in [[concepts/ai-scientific-discovery]] and demonstrates that AI can contribute fundamental scientific knowledge, not just process existing information.
