## Overview

The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) is a directed fund under the Linux Foundation established in December 2025 to provide vendor-neutral governance for the [[concepts/model-context-protocol]] and other agentic AI infrastructure projects.

## Key Facts

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Founded | December 2025 |
| Parent org | Linux Foundation |
| Co-founders | [[entities/anthropic]], Block, OpenAI |
| Supporting members | Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, Bloomberg |
| Initial projects | MCP, goose (Block), AGENTS.md (OpenAI) |
| Mission | Ensure agentic AI "evolves transparently, collaboratively, and in the public interest" |

## Significance

The AAIF represents a remarkable instance of direct competitors (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) collaborating on shared infrastructure. By donating MCP to a neutral foundation, Anthropic addressed concerns about single-vendor control of a protocol that had become critical infrastructure for the AI industry.

## Founding Projects

1. **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — The universal tool integration standard (Anthropic)
2. **goose** — An open-source AI agent framework (Block)
3. **AGENTS.md** — A specification for describing agent capabilities (OpenAI)

## Governance

MCP's existing governance structure was preserved through the transition. Community maintainers continue to drive development with transparent, community-input-driven decision-making.
