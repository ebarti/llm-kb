## Overview

Philip Gage published the original Byte Pair Encoding algorithm in 1994 as a method for text compression. The algorithm iteratively replaces the most frequent pair of adjacent bytes with a single new byte, reducing file size. Decades later, a modified version of his algorithm became the foundation of [[concepts/tokenization]] for virtually all modern large language models, making BPE one of the most impactful algorithms in AI history despite its origins in data compression.
