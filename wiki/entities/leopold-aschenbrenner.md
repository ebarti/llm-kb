## Overview

Leopold Aschenbrenner is a former member of OpenAI's Superalignment team and the author of "Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead," arguably the most influential essay on AGI timelines published in 2024. After being terminated from OpenAI in April 2024 (the company cited an information leak; he contends it was retaliation for raising security concerns), he founded Situational Awareness LP, a hedge fund managing over $1.5 billion as of 2025.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Person
- **Born**: ~2002 (graduated Columbia at 19 in 2021)
- **Education**: Valedictorian, Columbia University (economics + math-statistics, 2021)
- **Roles**: Founder & CIO, Situational Awareness LP; formerly OpenAI Superalignment team
- **Notable for**: "Situational Awareness" essay series; "Weak to Strong Generalization" paper (ICML 2024)
- **AGI Timeline**: 2027 (AGI), 2028-2030 (superintelligence)

## Key Contributions

### "Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead" (June 2024)

Multi-chapter essay series arguing:
1. AGI by 2027 is "strikingly plausible" — extrapolating compute, algorithmic efficiency, and unhobbling trends
2. **Intelligence explosion**: Millions of AGIs compress a decade of AI research into <1 year, producing superintelligence
3. **Trillion-dollar infrastructure**: Energy, not capital, is the bottleneck; American electricity must expand "tens of percent"
4. **Security crisis**: AI labs "basically hand key secrets to the CCP on a silver platter"
5. **Manhattan Project**: US government will nationalize AGI development by 2026-2027
6. **National security framing**: "The free world's very survival will be at stake"

See [[sources/aschenbrenner-situational-awareness]] for the full source summary.

### Academic Work

Co-authored "Weak to Strong Generalization" at OpenAI — a key paper in the [[concepts/superalignment]] research program, presented at ICML 2024. This work explored whether weak models could supervise stronger models, a foundational question for scalable oversight.

### Investment Influence

The $1.5B+ hedge fund backed by Patrick and John Collison (Stripe), Daniel Gross, and Nat Friedman represents financial markets taking his thesis seriously. His predictions have contributed to the massive increase in AI infrastructure investment.
