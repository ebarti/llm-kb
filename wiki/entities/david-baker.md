## Overview

David Baker is a biochemist at the University of Washington and director of the Institute for Protein Design. He received one half of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his two-decade pioneering work in computational protein design.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Person
- **Role**: Director, Institute for Protein Design, University of Washington
- **Notable for**: Computational protein design, Rosetta software, RFdiffusion, 2024 Nobel Prize

## Key Contributions

- **Rosetta**: Foundational software suite for protein structure prediction and design.
- **Top7** (2003): First computationally designed protein with a novel fold.
- **[[entities/rfdiffusion]]** (2023): Diffusion-based de novo protein design.
- **RFdiffusion2** (2025): Atomically accurate antibody design.
- **RFdiffusion3** (2025): Foundation model for all-atom biodesign.
