## Overview

Jason Wei is a researcher at Google (Brain/DeepMind) who authored two of the most influential papers in the LLM reasoning space:

1. **Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models** (2022) -- demonstrated that step-by-step reasoning exemplars in prompts dramatically improve LLM reasoning performance. This paper launched the entire field of LLM reasoning research.

2. **Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models** (2022) -- formalized the concept of emergent abilities that appear suddenly at scale, with chain-of-thought reasoning as a key example.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced [[concepts/chain-of-thought|chain-of-thought prompting]] as a practical technique.
- Demonstrated that prompting alone (no fine-tuning) can unlock reasoning in large models.
- Showed the scale-dependence of reasoning: CoT helps only above ~100B parameters.
- Formalized the concept of [[concepts/emergent-abilities|emergent abilities]] in LLMs.
- Identified chain-of-thought reasoning as an emergent ability with a threshold at ~10^22 FLOPs.
