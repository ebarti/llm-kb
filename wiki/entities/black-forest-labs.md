## Overview

Black Forest Labs is the AI company behind the [[entities/flux]] family of image generation models. The company was founded by key researchers who created the original [[entities/stable-diffusion]] at Stability AI, including Robin Rombach and colleagues from the CompVis group at LMU Munich.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: organization
- **Notable for**: Created FLUX, the leading open-weight image generation system
- **Founders**: Original Stable Diffusion creators
- **Product**: FLUX model family (FLUX.1, FLUX.2)
- **Architecture**: Pioneered MMDiT with flow matching for open-source image generation

## Role in Knowledge Base

Black Forest Labs represents the cutting edge of open-source [[concepts/image-generation]]. Their progression from [[entities/stable-diffusion]] (U-Net + DDPM) to [[entities/flux]] (MMDiT + [[concepts/flow-matching]]) mirrors the broader architectural evolution of [[concepts/diffusion-models]].
