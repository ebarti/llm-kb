## Overview

Eureka Labs is an AI education company founded by [[entities/andrej-karpathy]] in July 2024. Its mission is to build "a new kind of school that is AI native," where human instructors design course materials and AI Teaching Assistants provide personalized guidance to students at scale.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: AI education startup
- **Founded**: July 16, 2024
- **Founder**: [[entities/andrej-karpathy]]
- **URL**: https://eurekalabs.ai/
- **First Product**: LLM101n ("Let's Build A Storyteller")

## LLM101n Course

The flagship course guides students through building a complete LLM from scratch:
- **Languages**: Python, C, and CUDA
- **Structure**: ~17 chapters from fundamentals to multimodal extensions
- **Culmination**: A ChatGPT-like web application that generates and illustrates short stories
- **Philosophy**: "Learn by building" — every concept validated through implementation
- **Access**: Materials freely available online, with optional cohort experiences

## Educational Model

The Teacher + AI symbiosis model:
1. Expert human instructor designs the curriculum and course vision
2. AI Teaching Assistant (optimized for the specific course content) guides individual students
3. This combination could run an entire curriculum on a common platform
4. A single world-class teacher could scale to millions of students

## Context in Karpathy's Career

Eureka Labs formalizes Karpathy's decade-long educational trajectory:
- CS 231n at Stanford (2015-2017): First deep learning course
- Neural Networks: Zero to Hero (YouTube, 2022-2024): Millions of views
- micrograd, nanoGPT, minbpe (GitHub): Minimal educational codebases
- Eureka Labs (2024-present): Education as a company
