## Overview

Scite is an AI-powered citation analysis tool that goes beyond simple citation counts to analyze how papers are cited. Its Smart Citations system classifies 1.2 billion citation statements from 187M+ research articles as supporting, contrasting, or mentioning — enabling researchers to assess the credibility and reception of specific claims.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: AI citation analysis tool
- **URL**: https://scite.ai
- **Notable for**: 1.2 billion citation statements; Smart Citations (supporting/contrasting/mentioning classification)
- **Database**: 187M+ research articles

## Role in Knowledge Base

Scite represents the verification phase of [[concepts/ai-research-assistants]]. It performs [[concepts/claim-extraction]] at the scale of the entire scientific literature — a capability that has no human-scale equivalent.
