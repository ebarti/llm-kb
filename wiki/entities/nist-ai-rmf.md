## Overview

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) is a voluntary framework developed by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology in consultation with industry and civil society. While it does not carry the force of law, it is widely referenced by regulators and standards bodies worldwide as a baseline for responsible AI implementation.

## Four Core Functions

1. **Govern**: Establish organizational environment for AI risk management — policies, roles, culture
2. **Map**: Understand context, stakeholders, system limitations, and intended use
3. **Measure**: Analyze risks including bias, uncertainty, performance, and security
4. **Manage**: Respond to identified risks with controls, documentation, and monitoring

## Key Characteristics

- **Voluntary**: No penalties for non-compliance
- **Flexible**: Sector-agnostic; adaptable to different industries
- **Comprehensive**: Covers the full AI lifecycle
- **Complementary**: Designed to work alongside binding regulations like the [[entities/eu-ai-act]]
