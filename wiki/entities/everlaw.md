## Overview

Everlaw is a cloud-based litigation platform that uses AI to help legal teams review, analyze, and classify documents at scale. It represents the document analysis layer of [[concepts/llm-legal-applications]].

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Legal technology platform
- **URL**: https://www.everlaw.com
- **Notable for**: AI-powered e-discovery and document review; rapid summarization of large document volumes

## Role in Knowledge Base

Everlaw exemplifies how LLMs transform professional workflows: paralegals who previously spent weeks reviewing documents can now have AI classify and summarize them, shifting human effort to analysis and strategy.
