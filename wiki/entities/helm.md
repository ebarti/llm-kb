## Overview

HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) is the most comprehensive academic benchmark for LLMs, covering 42 scenarios and 7 evaluation metrics across 16+ models. Developed by Stanford's Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM), it aims to provide standardized, reproducible evaluation.

## Key Properties

- **42 scenarios** spanning diverse tasks
- **7 evaluation metrics** per scenario
- **Public leaderboard** with reproducible results
- **Standardized methodology** enabling fair comparison

## Limitations

- Academic focus may not reflect production use cases
- Snapshot-in-time evaluation (not continuous)
- Generic scenarios rather than domain-specific
