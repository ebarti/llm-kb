## Overview

David Ha is an AI researcher who co-authored the foundational "World Models" paper (2018) with [[entities/jurgen-schmidhuber]], revitalizing the research program of learning environment dynamics for imagination-based planning. The paper's VAE + MDN-RNN + minimal controller architecture and "learning inside a dream" concept inspired the entire modern [[concepts/world-models]] field.
