## Overview

Elicit is an AI research assistant built specifically for the literature review process. It excels at finding relevant papers, extracting structured data, and producing clean exportable comparison tables — making it the standard tool for the synthesis phase of academic research.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: AI literature review tool
- **URL**: https://elicit.com
- **Pricing**: Free / Plus $10/month / Pro $42/month / Team $65/user/month
- **Notable for**: Structured literature review, paper comparison tables, semantic summaries

## Key Features

- Customizable comparison tables across papers
- Semantic summaries of research papers
- Peer-reviewed source focus
- Data extraction into exportable formats
- Saves hours on manual literature reviews
