## Overview

Tesla Optimus (Tesla Bot) is a general-purpose [[concepts/humanoid-robots]] project announced in August 2021. It shares its AI system with Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) autonomous driving technology, representing Tesla's bet that the same perception and planning systems can power both vehicles and humanoid robots.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: product (humanoid robot)
- **Organization**: [[entities/tesla]]
- **Announced**: August 19, 2021
- **Specs**: 5'8" (173 cm), 125 lb (57 kg), 45 lb carry capacity
- **Gen 3 hands**: 22 DOF + 3 DOF wrist/forearm
- **AI**: Tesla FSD neural networks
- **Production**: Summer 2026 (few hundred units for factories)
- **Price target**: Under $20,000 long-term; early models $25-40K

## Criticism

- Rodney Brooks (2025): "pure fantasy thinking"
- Multiple demos relied on teleoperation, not autonomy
- Bloomberg: "mission creep" beyond clean-energy focus
- The Washington Post: Tesla's history of "exaggerating timelines"
