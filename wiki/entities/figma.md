## Overview

Figma is a collaborative interface design tool. Its **Figma Make** feature (out of beta 2025) is cited as a leading example of [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration-design]] done well.

## Key Features (AI)

### Figma Make
Enables designers to generate interactive experiences through natural language prompts while maintaining full creative control. The AI proactively suggests ideas but always waits for the user to accept, modify, or ignore each suggestion — creating moments of collaboration and surprise.

This exemplifies the [[concepts/copilot-pattern]] at its best: conversation for ideation, structured UI for refinement, human approval for commitment.
