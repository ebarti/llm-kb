## Overview

Sebastian Raschka is a machine learning researcher and educator known for his accessible technical writing about deep learning and language models. He is the author of "Build a Large Language Model From Scratch," which includes a detailed walkthrough of [[concepts/byte-pair-encoding]] tokenizer implementation.

## Key Contributions

- Authored one of the most detailed hands-on BPE implementation tutorials, building a `BPETokenizerSimple` class from scratch with training, encoding, decoding, and GPT-2 compatibility
- Makes complex ML concepts accessible through step-by-step implementations
