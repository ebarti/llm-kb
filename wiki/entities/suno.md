## Overview

Suno is the dominant AI music generation platform, with approximately 100 million users and a valuation exceeding $2.4 billion. The company's v5 model represents a significant leap in vocal clarity, arrangement sophistication, and genre accuracy.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: tool / platform
- **Notable for**: Largest AI music platform; most capable vocal synthesis
- **Users**: ~100 million
- **Valuation**: $2.4B+
- **Current model**: v5

## Capabilities

- **Audio quality**: 44.1kHz output
- **Stem separation**: Up to 12 individual tracks
- **MIDI export**: Integration with professional DAWs
- **Suno Studio**: Timeline-based editor functioning as lightweight DAW
- **Vocal synthesis**: Excellent for pop, rock, R&B with complex lyrical phrasing
- **Free tier**: 10 songs/day
- **Pro**: $10/month (500 songs)
- **Premier**: $30/month (2,000 songs)

## Legal Status

Warner Music Group has settled with Suno, establishing a licensing partnership that legitimizes the platform commercially. This mirrors the trajectory of sampling technology, moving from litigation to licensed creative tool.
