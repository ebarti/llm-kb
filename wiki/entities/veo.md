## Overview

Veo is Google DeepMind's AI video generation model family. Veo 3, released in early 2026, introduced a breakthrough capability: native synchronized audio generation that creates sound effects, ambient audio, and character dialogue alongside video from a single text prompt. This [[concepts/audio-visual-generation]] capability eliminates entire post-production workflows.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: tool / model
- **Developer**: Google DeepMind
- **Notable for**: Native audio-visual generation; ecosystem integration
- **Current version**: Veo 3.1

## Variants and Pricing

| Variant | Per-Second | Quality | Use Case |
|---------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Veo 3.1 Lite | $0.05 | Good | High-volume production |
| Veo 3 Standard | $0.35-0.50 | Cinematic | Premium content |
| Veo 3 Ultra | Higher | 4K potential | Top-tier production |

## Key Capabilities

- **Native audio**: Synchronized effects, dialogue, and ambient sound from text
- **Physics realism**: Leads market on physically plausible motion
- **4K output**: Available in ultra tier
- **Ecosystem**: Direct integration with YouTube creator tools and Vertex AI
- **Cost efficiency**: Veo 3.1 Lite at $0.05/second is the most affordable option

## Market Position

Veo 3 occupies the ecosystem tier: its Google Cloud integration and YouTube connection make it the natural choice for creators already in the Google ecosystem. The native audio capability is a competitive moat that other platforms are racing to match.
