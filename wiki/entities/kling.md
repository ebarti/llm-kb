## Overview

Kling is ByteDance's AI video generation model, positioning as the cost-efficiency leader in the [[concepts/video-generation]] market. Kling 3.0, released February 2026, introduced several technical breakthroughs including multi-shot sequences and native audio with lip synchronization.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: tool / model
- **Developer**: ByteDance
- **Notable for**: Cost efficiency ($0.07/sec), multi-shot sequences, native audio
- **Current version**: Kling 3.0

## Capabilities (Kling 3.0)

- **Multi-shot sequences**: 2-6 scenes with automatic transitions and camera logic -- a major technical breakthrough
- **Character lock**: Subject consistency maintained across all scenes and camera angles
- **Native audio**: Character dialogue with precise lip synchronization in 5 languages
- **Native text rendering**: Readable text within video output
- **Duration**: Up to 3 minutes (longest in the market in early 2026)
- **Resolution**: 720p-1080p
- **Cost**: $0.07/second (65% cheaper than Sora was)
- **Pricing**: ~$10-15/month entry, ~$33/month pro

## Market Position

Kling 3.0 occupies the cost-efficiency tier, enabling rapid iteration and A/B testing at minimal cost. Ideal for social media production, marketing agencies, and high-volume workflows where per-unit cost matters more than maximum quality.
