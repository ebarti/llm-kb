## Overview

Demis Hassabis is the co-founder and CEO of [[entities/google-deepmind]] and co-recipient of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for developing [[entities/alphafold]] 2, which solved the 50-year protein structure prediction challenge.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Person
- **Role**: CEO, Google DeepMind; Founder, Isomorphic Labs
- **Notable for**: AlphaFold, 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, founding DeepMind

## Career

- **2010**: Co-founded DeepMind (acquired by Google 2014).
- **2016**: AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol at Go.
- **2020**: AlphaFold 2 solves protein structure prediction.
- **2021**: Founded [[entities/isomorphic-labs]] for AI drug discovery.
- **2023**: DeepMind merged with Google Brain to form Google DeepMind.
- **2024**: Nobel Prize in Chemistry (shared with John Jumper).

## Contributions to AI for Science

Under Hassabis's leadership, DeepMind has produced an expanding ecosystem of AI tools for scientific discovery: AlphaFold (proteins), [[entities/gnome]] (materials), [[entities/gencast]] (weather), [[entities/funsearch]] and [[entities/alphaevolve]] (mathematics), and [[entities/alphagenome]] (genomics).
