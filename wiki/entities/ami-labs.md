## Overview

Advanced Machine Intelligence (AMI) Labs is a Paris-based AI startup co-founded by [[entities/yann-lecun]] in late 2025. The company raised $1.03 billion in seed funding at a $3.5 billion pre-money valuation in March 2026 — the largest seed round in European history. AMI Labs aims to build [[concepts/world-models]] based on [[concepts/jepa]] that understand physical reality, positioning itself as the primary challenger to the LLM paradigm.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: organization (AI startup)
- **Founded**: Late 2025
- **HQ**: Paris, France
- **Executive Chairman**: Yann LeCun (Turing Award winner)
- **CEO**: Alex LeBrun (ex-Nabla medical AI)
- **Funding**: $1.03B seed at $3.5B valuation (March 2026)
- **Relationship with Meta**: Partnership maintained; no direct Meta investment

## Technology

AMI Labs builds on the [[concepts/jepa]] family:
- **I-JEPA**: Image understanding via masked patch prediction in representation space
- **V-JEPA 2**: Video understanding and robotic planning
- **LeWorldModel**: First end-to-end JEPA trained from raw pixels (15M params)
- **Goal**: General world models that enable causal reasoning, persistent memory, and hierarchical planning
