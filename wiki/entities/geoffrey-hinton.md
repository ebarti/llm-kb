## Overview

Geoffrey Hinton is a computer scientist at the University of Toronto, widely known as the "Godfather of AI." He received the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics (shared with John Hopfield) for foundational work on machine learning using artificial neural networks.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Person
- **Role**: Professor Emeritus, University of Toronto; formerly Google
- **Notable for**: Deep learning foundations, Boltzmann machines, backpropagation, 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics

## Key Contributions

- **Boltzmann machines**: Extended Hopfield networks with learning algorithms.
- **Backpropagation**: Co-authored the influential 1986 paper popularizing backprop for training neural networks.
- **Deep belief networks** (2006): Demonstrated effective deep network training.
- **AlexNet** (2012, with students Krizhevsky and Sutskever): Launched the modern deep learning revolution.
- **Capsule networks**: Proposed alternative to convolutional neural networks.

## AI Safety Advocacy

After leaving Google in 2023, Hinton became one of the most prominent voices warning about AI existential risk. His concerns about AI systems potentially becoming more intelligent than humans carry particular weight given his foundational role in creating the technology.
