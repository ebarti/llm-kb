## Overview

Excalidraw is the most-downloaded community plugin for [[entities/obsidian]], providing a full-featured freehand drawing and diagramming tool directly within the vault. It enables visual thinking — sketches, diagrams, flowcharts, and handwritten notes — alongside standard markdown notes.

## Key Features

- Freehand sketching with a natural, hand-drawn aesthetic
- Shapes, arrows, text, and connections for diagrams
- Embedding Obsidian notes and images within drawings
- LaTeX support for mathematical notation
- Export to PNG, SVG, and other formats
- Libraries of reusable components
- Collaboration support

## Comparison with Canvas

While [[concepts/obsidian-canvas]] is for spatial arrangement of existing notes, Excalidraw is for creating new visual content — diagrams, sketches, and freehand drawings. Canvas arranges cards and edges; Excalidraw provides a freeform drawing surface. They serve complementary purposes.
