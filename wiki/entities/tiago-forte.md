## Overview

Tiago Forte is a productivity consultant, author, and educator who created the Building a Second Brain (BASB) methodology — arguably the most widely adopted personal knowledge management framework in the world. His approach focuses on practical, actionable systems for capturing, organizing, distilling, and expressing knowledge.

## Key Contributions

### Building a Second Brain (BASB)
The comprehensive methodology published as a book in 2022, teaching people to create external digital repositories for their knowledge. The core philosophy: "Our brains are for having ideas, not storing them."

### CODE Method
The four-step workflow: **C**apture (save resonant ideas intuitively), **O**rganize (by actionability using PARA), **D**istill (via [[concepts/progressive-summarization]]), **E**xpress (turn knowledge into creative output).

### PARA Method
The organizational framework: **P**rojects, **A**reas, **R**esources, **A**rchive — categorized by actionability rather than topic. This solved the "where does this go?" problem that plagues traditional folder systems.

### Progressive Summarization
A layered distillation technique: bold key passages, highlight the best-of-best, write an executive summary — designing notes for your Future Self to scan at any depth.

### Intermediate Packets
The concept that every work session should produce discrete, reusable work units that can be recombined for future projects — transforming knowledge work from starting-from-scratch to assembling-from-components.

## Influence

Forte's work popularized the term "second brain" (which is now also used in AI contexts — see [[concepts/second-brain]]). His BASB course has been taken by tens of thousands of students. His approach differs from [[concepts/zettelkasten]] in prioritizing practical output over intellectual exploration, and in using hierarchical folders (PARA) rather than flat linked notes.
