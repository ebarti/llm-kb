## Overview

Dean Allemang is a knowledge graph and semantic web practitioner who authored an influential analysis resolving the perceived conflict between property graphs and formal ontologies in the age of LLMs.

## Key Contributions

- **False dichotomy thesis**: Demonstrated that [[concepts/property-graphs]] and OWL ontologies are complementary — ontologies provide conceptual frameworks that property graphs instantiate
- **LLM-OWL compatibility**: Showed that GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 successfully generate accurate Cypher queries from OWL ontology summaries without seeing actual data
- **Practical workflow**: Proposed OWL → R2RML → Property Graph → LLM pipeline for enterprise knowledge management
- **Knowledge-directed RAG**: Advocated for OWL-structured RAG over pure vector approaches for large ontologies
- **Three-level governance**: Identified ontologies as enabling intra-enterprise, inter-institutional, and human-to-LLM communication
