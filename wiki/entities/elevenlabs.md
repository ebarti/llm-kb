## Overview

ElevenLabs is an AI voice synthesis company valued at $11 billion, best known for its text-to-speech and voice cloning technology. In August 2025, the company expanded into music generation with Eleven Music, leveraging its voice modeling expertise to produce vocals described as "unsettlingly realistic."

## Key Facts

- **Type**: tool / platform
- **Valuation**: $11 billion
- **Notable for**: Voice synthesis; expanding into music with realistic vocal generation
- **Music launch**: August 2025
- **Capabilities**: Songs up to 4 minutes, multiple languages, superior vocal realism
- **Limitation**: Fewer editing tools than [[entities/suno]] or [[entities/udio]]
