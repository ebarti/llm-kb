## Overview

Ward Cunningham is a computer programmer who invented the **wiki** concept in 1995 (WikiWikiWeb) and later created **Federated Wiki** (2011/2013). The original wiki concept — any visitor can edit any page — became the foundation for Wikipedia and thousands of other collaborative knowledge systems. Federated Wiki represents Cunningham's evolution of the idea: rather than forcing consensus on a single article, it enables forking and a "chorus of voices."

## Key Contributions

### WikiWikiWeb (1995)
The first wiki, enabling collaborative editing of web pages. This concept directly enabled [[concepts/wikipedia-knowledge-model]] and the broader [[concepts/collaborative-knowledge-building]] movement.

### Federated Wiki (2011-present)
Launched at IndieWebCamp 2011, with initial release December 16, 2013. Key innovation: applying source-control concepts (forking, branching) to wiki pages, enabling multiple perspectives to coexist rather than converging on a single version. See [[concepts/federated-knowledge]] for detailed analysis.

## Significance

Cunningham's two inventions represent the poles of a fundamental tension in [[concepts/collaborative-knowledge-building]]: the original wiki (→ Wikipedia) optimizes for **authoritative consensus**, while Federated Wiki optimizes for **perspective pluralism**. Both are valid responses to the challenge of collective knowledge creation.
