## Overview

Epoch AI is a research organization focused on investigating and forecasting the trajectory of AI development. They maintain the most comprehensive databases of AI compute trends, training data availability, and scaling analysis. Their work is widely cited in both academic and policy contexts.

## Key Facts

- **Type**: Research organization
- **URL**: https://epoch.ai
- **Notable for**: Compute trend tracking, scaling limit analysis, training data forecasts
- **Key publication**: "Can AI Scaling Continue Through 2030?" — the definitive four-constraint analysis

## Key Contributions

### Scaling Limits Analysis
Published the most rigorous analysis of four constraints on AI scaling through 2030:
1. **Power**: Most binding — US data center capacity 40 GW → 90 GW possible
2. **Chips**: 20-400M H100-equivalent GPUs possible by 2030
3. **Data**: Less binding — 400T to 20Q tokens by 2030 with multimodal
4. **Latency**: Least binding

Conclusion: 2e29 FLOP training runs (another GPT-2→GPT-4 jump) are feasible with massive investment.

### Context Window Growth
Tracked context window growth across 123 models, finding ~30x/year growth since mid-2023.

### Data Exhaustion Forecasts
Estimated high-quality language data (~300 trillion tokens) could be exhausted by 2026-2032.
