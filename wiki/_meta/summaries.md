- [[concepts/3d-parallelism]] — Combining data, tensor, and pipeline parallelism in a topology-aware configuration: TP within nodes (NVLink), PP across nodes (InfiniBand), DP across the cluster. The standard approach for frontier LLM training.
- [[concepts/5d-parallelism]] — Extension of 3D parallelism (DP+TP+PP) with Context Parallelism (splitting sequence length) and Expert Parallelism (distributing MoE experts) — the current state-of-the-art for training frontier models like DeepSeek-V3.
