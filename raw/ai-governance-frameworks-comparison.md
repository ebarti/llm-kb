# EU AI Act vs NIST AI RMF vs ISO/IEC 42001

## Three Major Frameworks

**EU AI Act** — Binding European regulation with extraterritorial reach
**NIST AI RMF** — Voluntary U.S. framework for risk management
**ISO/IEC 42001** — Certifiable international management system standard

## EU AI Act Risk Tiers
1. **Unacceptable Risk** — Prohibited (social scoring)
2. **High Risk** — Safety/fundamental rights (medical devices, employment, credit)
3. **Limited Risk** — Transparency obligations
4. **Minimal/No Risk** — Unrestricted

## NIST AI RMF Functions
Govern → Map → Measure → Manage

## Key Comparison

| Dimension | EU AI Act | NIST AI RMF | ISO/IEC 42001 |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Enforcement | Legally binding; Aug 2026 | Voluntary | Certifiable |
| Scope | EU + extraterritorial | U.S.-focused | International |
| Penalties | Up to €35M or 7% revenue | None | N/A |
| Approach | Prescriptive risk tiers | Flexible, sector-agnostic | Structured management |

## EU AI Act Timeline
- Feb 2025: Prohibited practices + AI literacy obligations
- Aug 2025: GPAI provider compliance
- Aug 2026: Full enforcement by Commission
- Aug 2027: High-risk systems in regulated products

## High-Risk Requirements
- Technical documentation
- Data governance and quality standards
- Human oversight mechanisms
- Conformity assessment before deployment
- Post-market monitoring

## Integration Strategy
Organizations can create unified compliance programs: inventory and classify AI systems, map controls across frameworks, establish governance, implement iteratively.
