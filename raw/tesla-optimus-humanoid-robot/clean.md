# Tesla Optimus (Tesla Bot)

General-purpose humanoid robot under development by Tesla, Inc.

## Timeline

- Aug 2021: Announced at AI Day. Musk: would handle "anything humans don't want to do"
- Apr 2022: First prototype at Giga Texas
- Sep 2022: Semi-functional prototypes at second AI Day (one walked, one moved arms)
- Sep 2023: Video of sorting blocks, yoga poses
- Dec 2023: Gen 2 unveiled (slimmer, 11 DOF hands, dancing, cooking)
- May 2024: Factory task video (criticized for teleoperation reliance)
- Oct 2024: Gen 3 at "We, Robot" event (22 DOF hands, $30K target price)
- Summer 2026: Gen 3 production begins

## Specifications

- Height: 5'8" (173 cm), Weight: 125 lb (57 kg)
- Carrying capacity: 45 lb (20 kg)
- Gen 3: 22 DOF hands + 3 DOF wrist/forearm
- Planetary roller screw actuators for shock load resistance

## AI System

Uses same AI as Tesla Full Self-Driving (FSD). Directly connects Optimus development to Autopilot technology.

## Production Plans

- Few hundred units in 2026 (factories + select enterprise partners)
- Thousands to tens of thousands from 2027-2028
- Long-term target: under $20,000/unit; early models $25,000-$40,000
- Musk: 50,000 units planned for 2026

## Leadership

Milan Kovac (program head since 2022) resigned June 2025; replaced by Ashok Elluswamy (head of Autopilot).

## Criticism

- Rodney Brooks (2025): humanoid robot vision "pure fantasy thinking"
- Multiple demonstrations criticized for teleoperation rather than autonomous capability
- Researchers called 2022 demonstrations a "complete and utter scam"
- Bloomberg: "mission creep" outside Tesla's clean-energy focus
- Washington Post: Tesla's history of "exaggerating timelines"
