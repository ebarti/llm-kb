# Ilya Sutskever: The End of Scaling and the Rise of Safe Superintelligence

## Overview

Ilya Sutskever, co-founder of OpenAI and one of the most influential figures in deep learning, departed OpenAI in mid-2024 to co-found Safe Superintelligence Inc. (SSI) with Daniel Gross and Daniel Levy. SSI's singular mission: build safe superintelligent AI with no side products, no API platform, no distractions.

## The End of Scaling

In November 2025, Sutskever declared: "We're moving from the age of scaling to the age of research." Key observations:
- Pretraining has exhausted most high-quality internet text
- Companies see diminishing returns from added compute in reinforcement learning
- Model size increases no longer fundamentally reshape capabilities
- "It is back to the age of research again, just with big computers"
- Breakthrough improvements will come from novel training methods and algorithms, not pure computational increases

## The Generalization Problem

Sutskever identifies the critical gap in current AI:

Evolutionary priors: Humans arrive with built-in structure for vision, physics, and social reasoning — compressed knowledge from evolution. Models start from random initialization.

Internal value systems: Emotions provide dense, continuous reward signals guiding human learning. AI receives only sparse feedback from training pipelines, leaving it brittle outside training domain.

Contemporary AI systems excel at benchmarks but fail at basic generalization tasks humans master easily.

## AGI Redefined

Rather than an omniscient entity, Sutskever envisions AGI as a "superintelligent fifteen-year-old":
- Capable of learning any job rapidly
- Deployment itself becomes part of training
- Different AI instances master distinct domains while sharing knowledge instantly
- Exponential capability growth through real-world experience

Timeline estimate: Human-level learning systems within 5-20 years.

## SSI: Structure and Strategy

Founded: June 19, 2024
Co-founders: Ilya Sutskever, Daniel Gross, Daniel Levy

Team composition (~50 employees as of July 2025):
- All researchers or engineers focused on technical problems
- No sales, marketing, or product managers
- Operations split between Palo Alto and Tel Aviv

Funding:
- Series A (September 2024): $1B from SV Angel, DST Global, Sequoia, a16z
- Series B (March 2025): $30B valuation, led by Greenoaks Capital (6x increase from $5B)
- Pre-revenue, no product, no timeline to liquidity

Infrastructure: Partnership with Google Cloud for TPU access (April 2025)

Leadership change: Daniel Gross departed July 2025 to join Meta Superintelligence Labs after disagreements over whether empirical feedback from real-world deployment was necessary. Sutskever assumed CEO role.

## SSI's Approach vs. Existing Labs

- Research-heavy, no public model deployment
- Betting novel ideas matter more than scale alone
- Safety-first development
- Specifics of technical approach remain undisclosed
- Rejected Meta acquisition interest in early 2025
- Direct competitor with OpenAI (from which Sutskever departed)

## Sutskever's Broader Influence

- Co-created AlexNet (2012) — launching the deep learning revolution
- Key architect of the scaling approach at OpenAI
- Led the Superalignment team at OpenAI before departure
- His shift away from "just scale" carries enormous credibility given his role in establishing it
- November 2024 NeurIPS talk: "Pre-training as we know it will end"
