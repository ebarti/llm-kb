# AlphaFold: Five Years of Impact

## Key Milestones

- **2020**: AlphaFold 2 solved the protein structure prediction problem at CASP 14 competition, achieving astonishing accuracy in predicting protein structures from amino acid sequences alone.
- **2021**: Launch of the AlphaFold Protein Database in partnership with EMBL-EBI, marking a turning point for global adoption.
- **2022**: Released predictions for over 200 million protein structures — equivalent to hundreds of millions of years of experimental work.
- **2024**: Recognition with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (Demis Hassabis and John Jumper).

## Global Reach & Usage

- 3+ million researchers across 190+ countries have utilized the database.
- 1+ million users from low- and middle-income countries.
- 35,000+ papers cite AlphaFold.
- 200,000+ papers incorporated AlphaFold 2 methodology elements.
- AlphaFold 3 research paper directly cited more than 9,000 times (as of Nov 2025).

## Scientific Applications

- Over 30% of AlphaFold-related research focuses on understanding disease mechanisms.
- Honeybee immunity protein (Vitellogenin) structure revealed for conservation efforts.
- Apolipoprotein B100 structure finally determined, enabling heart disease therapies targeting atherosclerosis.
- Researchers using AlphaFold 2 showed over 40% increased submission of novel protein structures and were twice as likely to be cited in clinical articles.

## Drug Discovery & Future Directions

- **Isomorphic Labs** (founded 2021) developed a unified drug design engine leveraging AlphaFold's capabilities.
- **AlphaFold 3**: Predicts structures of all life's molecules — proteins, DNA, RNA, and ligands. Uses a diffusion-based architecture. 76% accuracy on ligand binding poses, double any competing method.
- **AlphaFold Server**: Has generated 8+ million structure predictions for thousands of researchers globally.

## Complementary Tools

- **AlphaMissense**: Assesses genetic mutations underlying disease.
- **AlphaGenome**: Decodes genetics to pinpoint diseases.
- **AlphaProteo**: Designs novel protein binders for cancer and diabetes targets.
