# AI Model Training Costs 2026: Complete Analysis

## Key Training Cost Figures

Frontier Models (175B+ parameters):
- GPT-4: $100M+ with estimates around $150M for a single training run
- Llama 3.1 405B: approximately $25M (other sources cite $170M including all costs)
- DeepSeek V3: $5.5-5.6 million (noted as one of the most cost-efficient frontier models ever trained)
- Gemini Ultra: approximately $191 million

By Model Size:
- 1B parameters: $2K-$15K
- 7B parameters: $50K-$500K
- 70B parameters: $1.2M-$6M
- 405B+ parameters: $80M-$400M

## Cost Breakdown Components

GPU compute: 70-80% of total costs
Data operations: 10-15%
Engineering personnel: 15-20%
Software tools: 5-10%

## Hardware Requirements

- 70B models require 256x H200 GPUs
- 175B+ models need 2,000+ H200 clusters
- 405B+ models demand 5,000+ B200 GPUs

## Cost Optimization

Fine-tuning costs 1-5% of training from scratch.
7B model fine-tuning: $500-5K vs $50K-500K for full training.
Transfer learning approaches can achieve 80-95% savings.
Hardware efficiency improvements have reduced costs by 45% since 2023.
