# Q1 2026 Shatters Venture Funding Records

## Total Investment & Scale
- Global venture funding: $300 billion across 6,000 startups
- Growth rate: 150%+ increase quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year
- AI sector dominance: $242 billion (80% of total) global venture funding

## Largest Funding Rounds

| Company | Amount | Sector |
|---------|--------|--------|
| OpenAI | $122 billion | Frontier AI |
| Anthropic | $30 billion | Frontier AI |
| xAI | $20 billion | Frontier AI |
| Waymo | $16 billion | Self-driving |

These four companies collectively captured $188 billion, representing 65% of Q1 global funding.

## Regional Breakdown
- United States: $250 billion (83%) of global venture capital
- China: $16.1 billion (second-largest market)
- United Kingdom: $7.4 billion

## Funding Stage Analysis
- Late-stage: $246.6 billion across 584 deals (205% YoY growth)
- Early-stage: $41.3 billion across 1,800 deals (41% YoY increase)
- Seed funding: $12 billion across 3,800 deals (31% YoY growth)

## Exit Activity
- IPOs: 21 venture-backed exits above $1 billion globally
- M&A: $56.6 billion in startup acquisitions (third-highest since 2022)

## Key Trend
February 2026 became the largest single month of startup funding ever recorded—$189 billion globally—driven almost entirely by three companies: OpenAI ($110B), Anthropic ($30B), and Waymo ($16B). Funding is increasingly concentrated in a handful of foundational giants.
