# Claude (language model) - Wikipedia

Claude is a family of large language models developed by Anthropic, first released in March 2023. Named after Claude Shannon, the mathematician who pioneered information theory.

## Model Timeline

- **Claude 1** (March 14, 2023): First public model, available only to selected users
- **Claude 2** (July 11, 2023): First publicly available version
- **Claude 2.1** (November 21, 2023): Doubled context window to 200,000 tokens (~500 pages)
- **Claude 3 family** (March 4, 2024): Introduced three size tiers -- Haiku (smallest/cheapest), Sonnet (mid-tier), Opus (largest/most capable). Knowledge cutoff August 2023.
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (June 20, 2024): Released with new Artifacts feature for code/document generation
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet update** (October 22, 2024): Added "computer use" capability allowing screen navigation
- **Claude Sonnet 4 & Opus 4** (May 22, 2025): Initial Claude 4 generation release
- **Claude Opus 4.1** (August 5, 2025)
- **Claude Sonnet 4.5** (September 29, 2025)
- **Claude Haiku 4.5** (October 15, 2025): Targeting smaller companies needing faster/cheaper assistant
- **Claude Opus 4.5** (November 24, 2025): Improved coding, spreadsheet creation, "Infinite Chats"
- **Claude Opus 4.6** (February 5, 2026): Agent teams, PowerPoint integration, 1M token context, 128K max output
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6** (February 17, 2026): Full upgrade across coding, computer use, long-context reasoning

## Architecture and Training

- Generative pre-trained transformer architecture
- Constitutional AI training methodology using AI feedback (RLAIF)
- Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)

## Constitutional AI Evolution

- 2022: Initial version of the constitution
- 2023: Expanded to 75 guidelines
- 2026: Increased to 23,000 words (from 2,700 in 2023), authored by Amanda Askell with contributions from Joe Carlsmith, Chris Olah, Jared Kaplan, and Holden Karnofsky

## Key Features

- **Web search** (March 2025 for paying users; May 2025 for free users)
- **Artifacts** (June 2024): Interactive code/document generation in separate window
- **Computer use** (October 2024): Screen interpretation, keyboard/mouse simulation
- **Claude Code** (February 2025 preview; May 2025 GA): Agentic CLI tool
- **Claude Cowork** (January 2026): GUI version for non-technical users
- **Persistent memory** (March 2026): Retains user preferences across conversations

## Pricing Tiers

- Free tier: Basic access
- Claude Pro: Standard paid subscription
- Claude Max (April 2025): $100/month (5x usage) or $200/month (20x usage)
- Claude Team and Claude Enterprise (May 2024)

## Notable Benchmarks

- Claude Opus 4.5: 14 hours 30 minutes estimated task-completion time horizon (50% completion) per METR
- Claude Opus 4.6 agents collectively wrote a C compiler in Rust from scratch (~$20,000 cost)

## Military & Government

- November 2024: Partnership with Palantir and AWS for U.S. intelligence/defense
- June 2025: "Claude Gov" model for classified missions
- Anthropic prohibits use for domestic mass surveillance and lethal autonomous weapons
