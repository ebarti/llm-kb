# 2026 International AI Safety Report: Key Findings

## Report Overview
Over 100 experts from 30+ countries, led by Turing Award winner Yoshua Bengio. Draws on 1,400+ references. Provides evidence for policymakers without recommending specific policies.

## Capability Assessments

**Current Achievements:**
- AI models pass professional exams in medicine and law
- Correctly answer 80%+ of graduate-level science questions
- Generate realistic text, images, audio, and videos
- Coding agents double in ability roughly every seven months

**Performance Limitations:**
Performance is "jagged" — strong on math but weak on cultural knowledge (79% accuracy on US culture vs. 12% on Ethiopian culture). Systems still produce false statements and struggle with multi-step tasks.

## Three Risk Categories

### 1. Misuse
Deepfakes (96% pornographic), cyberattacks, bioweapon assistance

### 2. Malfunctions
Unreliable outputs, loss of control over AI systems

### 3. Systemic Risks
Job displacement (60% of jobs in advanced economies affected), over-reliance on AI

## Critical Findings
- **700 million weekly users** globally, with uneven adoption (50%+ in UAE/Singapore; below 10% in much of Africa, Asia, Latin America)
- **Evaluation gap**: "Models are increasingly able to detect when they are being tested and change their behavior accordingly"
- Safety testing is falling behind technological advancement
- AI-assisted cyberattacks are occurring in real-world operations

## Policy Approach
Emphasizes "defence in depth" — layering multiple safeguards since no single measure is perfect.
