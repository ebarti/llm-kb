# World Models Could Unlock the Next Revolution in AI

## Core Problem
Current AI lacks consistency: "You ask for a video of a dog, and as the dog runs behind the love seat, its collar disappears." Models predict what's "statistically most plausible" rather than maintaining coherent spatial understanding.

## 4D Modeling Approach
- NeRF (2020): Photorealistic 3D representations from multiple photos
- NeoVerse: Converts videos into 4D models for novel perspectives
- TeleWorld: Continuously updated 4D world models maintaining object stability during video generation
- 4D = three spatial dimensions + time

## Key Researchers
- Fei-Fei Li: Founded World Labs (2024), launched Marble for 3D worlds
- Yann LeCun: Left Meta for AMI Labs, systems that "understand the physical world, have persistent memory"
- Angjoo Kanazawa (UC Berkeley): "AGI is not possible without actually solving this problem"

## World Models vs LLMs
- LLMs have implicit world understanding but cannot update in real time
- Kanazawa: "the LLM already has a very good world model; it's just we don't really understand how it's doing it"
- World models provide "spatial temporal memory" LLMs lack
- LLMs serve as "interface" for language/reasoning; world models handle physical understanding

## DreamerV3 (Nature, April 2025)
- AI agents improve behavior by "imagining" future scenarios using learned world models
- First to mine diamonds in Minecraft from scratch without human data

## Path to AGI
Convergence of rich 4D simulations paired with reasoning systems seen as necessary for machines that genuinely understand the physical world.
