# Eureka Labs — AI Education Company

## Founding
Announced July 16, 2024 by Andrej Karpathy on X: "Excited to share that I am starting an AI+Education company called Eureka Labs."

## Mission
"We are Eureka Labs and we are building a new kind of school that is AI native."

## Educational Philosophy
The teacher designs course materials, supported and scaled by an AI Teaching Assistant optimized to guide students. This Teacher + AI symbiosis could run an entire curriculum on a common platform.

## LLM101n Course
- Undergraduate-level online course
- Goal: Build a "Storyteller AI" large language model from scratch
- Languages: Python, C, and CUDA
- Culminates in a ChatGPT-like web application for generating and illustrating short stories
- ~17 chapters from foundational language model concepts to multimodal extensions
- "Learn by building" philosophy
- Materials to be openly available online
- Both digital and physical cohorts planned

## Context
Follows Karpathy's pattern of open education:
- CS231n at Stanford (2015-2017)
- Neural Networks: Zero to Hero YouTube series (2022-2024)
- micrograd, nanoGPT, minbpe educational projects
- Eureka Labs represents the formalization of his education mission into a company
