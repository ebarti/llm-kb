# Anthropic - Wikipedia

## Founding and Leadership

Anthropic was established in 2021 by seven former OpenAI employees:
- Dario Amodei (CEO)
- Daniela Amodei (President)
- Jared Kaplan
- Jack Clark
- Chris Olah
- Ben Mann
- Sam McCandlish
- Tom Brown

They left OpenAI in 2020 due to concerns over OpenAI's lack of commitment to safety.

## Corporate Structure

Public benefit corporation headquartered at 500 Howard Street, San Francisco. A "Long-Term Benefit Trust" holds Class T shares enabling board director elections. Approximately 2,500 employees as of 2026.

## Valuation and Funding

- April 2022: $580 million raised (including $500M from FTX)
- September 2023: Amazon invested $1.25B (later $4B total; doubled to $8B by November 2024)
- October 2023: Google invested $500M with $1.5B committed
- March 2025: $3.5B Series E at $61.5B valuation
- September 2025: $13B Series F at $183B valuation
- December 2025: $10B term sheet at $350B valuation
- February 2026: $30B Series G at $380 billion post-money valuation

## Key Products

- Claude language model family (multiple generations)
- Claude Code (AI coding assistant)
- Claude Cowork (graphical interface for non-technical users)

## Research Focus

- Constitutional AI framework
- Interpretability research (dictionary learning, neural activation patterns)
- Responsible Scaling Policy

## Partnerships

- November 2024: Palantir and AWS for U.S. intelligence/defense
- October 2025: Google partnership for TPU access
- November 2025: Nvidia and Microsoft partnership ($15B investment; $30B Azure computing purchase)
- December 2025: $200M Snowflake partnership

## Controversy

February 2026: Pentagon designated Anthropic a "supply chain risk" after the company refused demands to remove contractual restrictions prohibiting domestic surveillance and autonomous weapons use.
