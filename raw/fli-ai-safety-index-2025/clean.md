# AI Safety Index - Summer 2025

## Overall Rankings & Scores

- **Anthropic**: C+ (2.64) — Best overall grade
- **OpenAI**: C (2.10) — Second place
- **Google DeepMind**: C- (1.76)
- **x.AI**: D (1.23)
- **Meta**: D (1.06)
- **Zhipu AI**: F (0.62)
- **DeepSeek**: F (0.37)

## Six Evaluation Domains

1. **Risk Assessment** — Internal/external testing for dangerous capabilities
2. **Current Harms** — Safety benchmarks, robustness, watermarking, privacy
3. **Safety Frameworks** — Risk identification, analysis, treatment, governance
4. **Existential Safety** — Planning for human-level AI systems
5. **Governance & Accountability** — Whistleblowing, company structure, regulatory engagement
6. **Information Sharing** — Technical transparency, voluntary cooperation, incident reporting

## Critical Findings

None of the companies scored above D in Existential Safety planning despite pursuing AGI within a decade. This indicates severe preparedness gaps for advanced AI development.

Anthropic excels through human participant bio-risk trials and privacy protections (non-training on user data). OpenAI distinguishes itself as the sole company publishing whistleblowing policies.

## 33 Evaluation Indicators

**Risk Assessment subdomain**: dangerous capability evaluations, elicitation methodologies, human uplift trials, independent reviews, pre-deployment testing, and bug bounties.

**Current Harms subdomain**: HELM Safety/AIR benchmarks, TrustLLM scoring, Gray Swan adversarial testing, Cisco security evaluations, fine-tuning protections, watermarking, and user privacy practices.

## Safety Benchmarks Referenced

- HarmBench (jailbreak resistance)
- BBQ (social discrimination)
- SimpleSafetyTest
- XSTest (alignment between helpfulness and harmlessness)
- AnthropicRedTeam (resilience to adversarial probing)
- HELM Safety / AIR-Bench

Performance reported as normalized scores ranging from 0 to 1.

## Methodology

Evidence collection spanned March–June 2025 via desk research and targeted company surveys. An independent panel of six AI experts assigned letter grades per domain using fixed performance standards, with final scores calculated through domain averaging.
