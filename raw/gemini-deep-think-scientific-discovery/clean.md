# Gemini Deep Think: Mathematical and Scientific Discovery

## IMO Performance

Gemini Deep Think achieved Gold-medal standard at the International Mathematics Olympiad in summer 2025, scoring 35/42 (5 of 6 problems solved perfectly). Also achieved similar results at the International Collegiate Programming Contest World Finals (September 2025).

## Technical Architecture: Aletheia

Research agent powered by Deep Think that employs:
- Natural language verification to identify flaws in candidate solutions.
- Iterative generation and revision cycles.
- Web browsing and Google Search integration to prevent hallucinations.
- Ability to acknowledge failure, improving efficiency for researchers.

## Mathematical Discoveries

### Open Problems
- Autonomously solved four open questions from Erdos Conjectures database (Erdos-652, 654, 1040, 1051).
- Erdos-1051 led to a generalization published in peer-reviewed research.
- Semi-autonomous assessment of 700 problems in Bloom's Erdos Conjectures collection.

### Performance Metrics
- IMO-ProofBench Advanced: 90% score (January 2026).
- PhD-level exercises (FutureMath Basic): 38%.
- Continued scaling with inference-time compute.

## Physics and Computer Science Breakthroughs

1. **Network optimization**: Solved Max-Cut and Steiner Tree problems using measure theory and topology from unrelated fields.
2. **Online submodular optimization**: Settled a 2015 conjecture by constructing a three-item counterexample.
3. **ML optimization**: Proved why automatic penalty generation works in noise-filtering techniques.
4. **Economic theory**: Extended revelation principle for AI token auctions.
5. **Cosmic strings**: Found novel singularity solutions using Gegenbauer polynomials.

## Human-AI Collaboration: Vibe-Proving

Iterative cycles where humans:
- Guide AI through exploration.
- Employ balanced prompting (requesting simultaneous proof or refutation).
- Utilize code-assisted verification.
