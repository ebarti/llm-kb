# Confluence vs Notion vs SharePoint: Knowledge Platform Comparison 2026

## Confluence (Atlassian)
Structured wiki and documentation platform excelling at technical documentation. "76% of customers ship projects faster when connecting project tracking in Jira to documentation in Confluence." Includes Rovo AI for conversational search with 20+ pre-built agents for documentation tasks: content summarization, meeting note generation, action item extraction, and KB search. AI spots outdated pages and broken links.

## Notion
Flexible, block-based workspace supporting notes, databases, wikis, and projects. Ranked #1 for knowledge bases on G2 with over 100 million users. Features relational databases and autonomous Notion Agent for multi-step task completion. Includes Enterprise Search across connected apps, Research Mode, and Meeting Notes. Expanded beyond docs into AI Agents, Sites, Forms, Calendar, and Mail.

## SharePoint (Microsoft)
Enterprise content management serving 190+ million users across 200,000 organizations. Offers granular permission settings at site, list, and item levels with extensive compliance certifications including HIPAA. Requires Microsoft 365 Copilot at $30/user/month for AI features.

## AI Capabilities Comparison
- Notion provides the most AI functionality in base plans, including autonomous agents
- Confluence includes Rovo AI without per-user add-ons at Premium tier
- SharePoint requires Microsoft 365 Copilot at $30/user/month additional cost

## Pricing
- Notion Plus: $10/user/month
- Confluence Standard: $5.42/user/month
- SharePoint Plan 1: $5.00/user/month (typically requires Microsoft 365 Business Standard at $12.50/user/month)

## Best Fits
- Confluence: Teams using Jira with technical documentation needs
- Notion: Startups valuing flexibility and modern interfaces
- SharePoint: Large enterprises with compliance requirements
