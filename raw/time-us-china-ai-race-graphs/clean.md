# 6 Graphs That Show Who's Really Winning the US-China AI Race

US models have maintained overwhelming market dominance, capturing approximately 93% of global LLM site visits as of August 2025. However, site visits to China-based LLMs increased by 460% in just two months. Chinese LLMs' global market share surged from 3% to 13% in two months, mostly carried by DeepSeek.

Since 2023, Chinese models have trailed U.S. models by seven months on average in model quality. However, China's DeepSeek R1 has shown that competitive alternatives can rapidly erode U.S. market share.

The United States leads China in AI chip production and market control. Yet export rules announced in January by the Trump administration could give Chinese companies access to 890,000 of Nvidia's H200 AI chips—more than double the number of chips that Chinese manufacturers are expected to produce in 2026.

While the United States has the upper hand in access to cutting-edge AI semiconductors, China has a significant advantage in energy for AI data centers.

By 2026, the US-China AI race shows signs of specialization rather than total victory for either side.
