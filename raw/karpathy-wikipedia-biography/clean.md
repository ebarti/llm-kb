# Andrej Karpathy — Wikipedia Biography

## Personal Information
- Born: October 23, 1986, Bratislava, Czechoslovakia (now Slovakia)
- Nationality: Slovak-Canadian
- Moved to Toronto at age 15

## Education
- Bachelor's: Computer Science and Physics, University of Toronto (2009)
- Master's: University of British Columbia (2011), focused on physically simulated figures under Michiel van de Panne
- PhD: Stanford University (2015), supervised by Fei-Fei Li. Dissertation: "Connecting Images and Natural Language"

## Early Content Creation
- YouTube channel "badmephisto" (established 2006) for Rubik's cube tutorials, used by speedcubers including Feliks Zemdegs. Over 9 million views as of June 2025.

## Academic Contributions
- Architect and lead instructor of CS 231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition at Stanford
- Course grew from 150 students (2015) to 750 students (2017), one of Stanford's largest classes
- Lectures, notes, and assignments freely available online; lecture videos garnered 800,000+ views

## Career Timeline

### OpenAI (2015-2017, first stint)
- Founding member of OpenAI's AI research group
- Research Scientist focused on deep learning, generative models, computer vision
- Contributed to work on GANs and deep reinforcement learning

### Tesla, Inc. (June 2017 – July 2022)
- Director of Artificial Intelligence and Autopilot Vision
- Reported directly to Elon Musk
- Led computer vision team for Tesla Autopilot
- Team handled all data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla's custom inference chip
- Briefly involved with Tesla Optimus (humanoid robot)
- Departed July 2022 after several-month sabbatical

### OpenAI (February 2023 – February 2024, second stint)
- Returned to OpenAI, worked on improving GPT-4
- Built new team focused on midtraining and synthetic data generation
- Departed February 13, 2024

### Eureka Labs (July 2024 – Present)
- Founded AI education company
- First product: LLM101n course ("Let's Build A Storyteller")
- Promotes AI teaching assistants alongside human instructors

## Awards and Recognition
- MIT Technology Review Innovators Under 35 (2020)
- TIME 100 Most Influential People in AI (2024)

## Coined Terms
- "Vibe coding" (February 2025) — programming through natural language prompts to LLMs
