# 12 LLM Product Development Practices

## 1. Thin-Slice Your MVP Use Case
Focus on the smallest, most impactful piece of the problem AI can solve. "The tastiest slices are the thinnest-sliced fatty ones." Identify what customers complain about most and determine if AI can solve it.

## 2. Don't Over-Design Tactical UX
Keep initial UI simple. The UX will transform significantly once the product reaches users. Plan for rapid iteration rather than perfection.

## 3. Don't Forget the Vision
Run parallel tracks — one for the thin-slice MVP, another for future capabilities. Execute "two projects" simultaneously to balance shipping speed with strategic direction.

## 4. Use the "Master LLM" Approach
Implement a directing LLM that routes user queries to specialized, task-specific models. Different use cases (explaining, creating dashboards, answering questions) are best handled by purpose-built models.

## 5. Recognize AI Is Fundamentally Different to Ship
Design evolves rapidly even after launch. Seek "provisional approvals" rather than comprehensive commitments. Traditional lengthy up-front validation slows AI projects.

## 6. Let Customers Train Your Model
"Likely the single most important point." The AI becomes an intermediary facilitating collaboration between customers and the trained model. Customer interactions drive continuous improvement.

## 7. First Iteration Focuses on Data Collection
The initial release prioritizes gathering training data over delivering a perfect product. Include prominent feedback mechanisms: verification buttons, thumbs up/down, spaces for expected answers. Include legal disclaimers about AI-generated information.

## 8. Technology Stack Selection Matters
"The quality of the underlying AI model matters much more than anything else." Test multiple models and compare performance. Evaluate response latency critically.

## 9. Prefer Fine-Tuning Over Prompt Engineering
Fine-tuning consistently outperforms prompt engineering. Select platforms supporting effective fine-tuning. Some models resist training — choose ones that accept feedback well.

## 10. Use Positive N-Shot Prompting
Provide examples demonstrating what the model *should* do rather than what to avoid. Like instructing children to "Remember to bring your gloves" rather than "Don't forget your gloves." Provide thousands of positive examples.

## 11. Incentivize AI Development Participation Organizationally
Launch internally first with employees. Gamify contributions to model training. Account executives, support staff, and SMEs can identify training data gaps.

## 12. Know When to Chill Your Model
Temperature controls creativity. For most SaaS: set temperature low for consistency. For regeneration: experiment with higher temperature. Test optimal settings separately.

## Overarching Theme
"UX as a glue for the product development process" — integrating design thinking, technical strategy, and user collaboration into coherent AI product development.
