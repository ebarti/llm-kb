# Research: When Used Correctly, LLMs Can Unlock More Creative Ideas

## Core Thesis

Generative AI's greatest promise extends beyond productivity gains to unlocking "new forms of human creativity that can drive innovation and growth." Intentional deployment matters significantly.

## Key Findings

### Adoption Trends
Between 2024 and 2025, "idea generation and brainstorming" experienced the largest increase in corporate adoption (+12%), jumping to the top five most common AI use cases.

### Ideation Quality
Quality ideation requires balancing two dimensions: originality (deviation from existing ideas) and appropriateness (feasibility). The best concepts break new ground while remaining practically viable.

### Two Pathways LLMs Support
1. **Persistence**: Generating extensive variations on themes exhaustively
2. **Flexibility**: Combining distant concepts in unexpected ways

## Practical Techniques

### For Enhanced Productivity
- Fine-tuning models on specialized content (brand guidelines, customer feedback)
- Few-shot prompting with high-quality examples
- Retrieval-augmented generation accessing domain-specific data

### For Greater Semantic Breadth
- Persona modifiers ("think like Steve Jobs")
- Hybrid prompting with varied parallel prompts
- Chain-of-thought processes for step-by-step generation
- Temperature adjustments for balancing creativity and accuracy

## Proposed AI Ideation Roles

### Lead Ideator
- **The Designer**: Creates personalized variants and multivariate testing
- **The Writer**: Elevates perceived quality and persuasiveness

### Thought Partner
- **The Interviewer**: Asks probing questions revealing blind spots
- **The Actor**: Simulates customer responses (with representation limitations)

## Critical Caveat

While individual users generate more original ideas, group-level diversity can paradoxically narrow when everyone uses similar tools, potentially converging on comparable concepts rather than divergent ones.
