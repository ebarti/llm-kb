# Enterprise RAG: Building an AI Knowledge Base in 2026

## Market Context
Global RAG market valued at USD 1.2 billion in 2024, projections estimate USD 11.0 billion by 2030 (49.1% CAGR). Document retrieval accounts for 32.4% of global RAG revenue. 71% of organizations now use generative AI in at least one business function.

## Core Use Cases
- Intelligent customer support with procedure/FAQ access
- Accelerated employee onboarding through natural language queries
- Compliance and regulatory document management
- R&D knowledge synthesis across dispersed teams
- Technical documentation assistance

## Architecture Approaches
- **Classic RAG**: Vector-based semantic search for straightforward retrieval
- **Agentic RAG**: Breaks complex questions into sub-queries for nuanced answers
- **GraphRAG**: Exploits concept relationships for sophisticated reasoning
- **Hybrid Search**: Combines semantic and keyword matching

## Framework Comparison
| Tool | Strength | Best For |
|------|----------|----------|
| LangChain | Flexible ecosystem | Custom complex projects |
| LlamaIndex | Document optimization | Large document bases |
| Vectara | Turnkey cloud | Rapid deployment |
| Haystack | Open-source modularity | Full control systems |

## Four-Step Implementation
1. **Knowledge Audit**: Inventory internal documentation, identify high-impact domains
2. **Architecture Selection**: Match use cases to RAG patterns
3. **Tool Selection**: Match frameworks to organizational capacity and governance requirements
4. **Deployment & Governance**: Encryption, access controls, reranking, source attribution

## Success Metrics
- Quantitative: Information search time reduction (60-80%), first-contact resolution, productivity gains
- Qualitative: User satisfaction, adoption rates, decision-making quality impact

## Common Pitfalls
- Data Quality Neglect: RAG outputs depend entirely on source material
- Ignoring Business Context: Generic models need organizational customization
- Change Management Underestimation
- Scalability Oversight

## Deployment Roadmap
- Pilot (1-2 months): 100-500 documents, 10-20 users
- Scale (3-6 months): Expand departments, integrate sources, establish governance
- Optimization: Usage analysis, advanced features, multimodal evolution

## Emerging Trends (2026-2027)
- Multimodal RAG (images, videos, audio)
- Advanced Agentic Systems beyond Q&A
- GraphRAG expansion
- Personalized role-based responses
