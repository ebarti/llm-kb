# The State of AI Coding 2025

Greptile's internal data reveals dramatic productivity acceleration across engineering teams using AI tools.

## Engineering Team Velocity

- PR size increased 93% year-over-year, jumping from 57 to 110 lines changed
- Individual developer output nearly tripled, rising from 4,450 to 14,148 lines of code annually
- Medium-sized teams (6-15 developers) saw even steeper gains, escalating from 7,005 to 19,715 lines per developer
- File-level changes grew from 18 to 25 median lines, indicating denser pull requests

## AI Tool Adoption Landscape

### Standardization Trends
- CLAUDE.md appears in 75% of organizations
- AGENTS.md surpassed Cursor Rules
- Teams increasingly standardize on fewer rule formats -- organizations using all three dropped from 17% to 6%

### SDK & Platform Momentum
- Anthropic's SDK reached 124 million monthly downloads by March 2026
- OpenAI Agents grew 5x during Q1 2026, hitting 21 million downloads
- LiteLLM overtook LangSmith in the LLMOps category, achieving 98 million monthly downloads

## Model Provider Competition

- OpenAI maintained 233 million monthly PyPI downloads by March 2026
- Anthropic surged to 83 million
- The competitive gap narrowed substantially -- OpenAI's lead compressed from 3.7:1 to 2.8:1 between December 2025 and March 2026

## Memory & Vector Infrastructure

- mem0 dominates memory packages with 58% market share
- Weaviate extended vector database leadership to 33%, gaining 8 percentage points

## Research Innovations

Recent papers emphasize architectural diversity: quantization-efficient transformers (TurboQuant), recursive execution models, learned memory systems (Titans), multimodal agentic frameworks (Kimi K2.5), and KV-cache-level retrieval (RetroLM).
