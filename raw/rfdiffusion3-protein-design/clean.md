# RFdiffusion3: AI Foundation Model for Protein Biodesign

## Overview

RFdiffusion3 is an open-source foundation model for biodesign capable of generating new proteins that interact with any type of molecule commonly found inside living cells. Released December 2025 from David Baker's lab at the University of Washington Institute for Protein Design.

## Key Capabilities

- Generates protein structures in the context of ligands, nucleic acids, and other non-protein constellations of atoms.
- Treats individual atoms as fundamental units, applying diffusion to create new atomic arrangements.
- Produces intricate chemical interactions with unprecedented precision.
- 10x faster performance than RFdiffusion2 (released earlier in 2025).

## Benchmark Results

Matched or outperformed prior tools on:
- Protein-protein binding
- Protein-DNA binding
- Protein-small molecule binding
- Enzyme design tasks

## Antibody Design (RFdiffusion2)

Using RFdiffusion2, researchers demonstrated de novo generation of:
- Antibody variable heavy chains
- Single-chain variable fragments
- Full antibodies binding to user-specified epitopes with atomic-level precision.

Published in Nature: "Atomically accurate de novo design of antibodies with RFdiffusion."

## Availability

Open-source: training code and model weights available on GitHub through the Rosetta Commons Foundry.
