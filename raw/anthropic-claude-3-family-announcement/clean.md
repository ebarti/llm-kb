# Introducing the Next Generation of Claude - Claude 3 Family

## Release Date
March 4, 2024

## Model Lineup
Three models in ascending capability:
- **Claude 3 Haiku**: Fastest, most compact, near-instant responsiveness
- **Claude 3 Sonnet**: Ideal balance of intelligence and speed for enterprise
- **Claude 3 Opus**: Most intelligent, best-in-market on highly complex tasks

## Benchmarks
Opus delivers industry-leading results on:
- MMLU (undergraduate expert knowledge)
- GPQA (graduate-level expert reasoning)
- GSM8K (basic mathematics)
- HumanEval (coding)

Claude 3 Opus was the first model to surpass GPT-4 on several benchmarks.

## Vision Capabilities
All three models process a wide range of visual formats: photos, charts, graphs, technical diagrams. Particularly valuable for enterprise knowledge bases encoded in PDFs, flowcharts, and presentation slides.

## Context Window
- Initial launch: 200K token context windows
- Select use cases: capable of inputs exceeding 1 million tokens
- Opus achieved near-perfect recall (>99% accuracy) on Needle In A Haystack evaluation

## Speed
- Sonnet: 2x faster than Claude 2/2.1 with higher intelligence
- Haiku: Processes ~10K token research papers in under 3 seconds

## Pricing
- Opus: $15/$75 per million tokens (input/output)
- Sonnet: $3/$15 per million tokens
- Haiku: $0.25/$1.25 per million tokens

## Key Improvements
- Better accuracy and reduced hallucinations
- Fewer unnecessary refusals compared to previous generations
- Sophisticated visual processing on par with leading models
