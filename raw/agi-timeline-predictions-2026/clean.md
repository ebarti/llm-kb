# AGI Timeline Predictions: Expert Forecasts as of 2026

## Timeline Compression Trend

Every expert group has significantly shortened AGI arrival estimates in recent years. In 2020, the median expert estimate was roughly 2060-2070. By 2024, it had moved to 2040-2050 in broad researcher surveys. By 2026, forecasters on Metaculus average 25% chance by 2029 and 50% by 2033.

Metaculus' median forecast dropped from 50 years (2020) to approximately 5 years (2026) — the most dramatic shift.

## Definitional Disagreement

Google DeepMind framework: Five capability tiers (Emerging through Superhuman), separately evaluates autonomy levels.
Anthropic: Avoids "AGI" terminology, prefers "systems with intellectual capabilities matching Nobel Prize winners."
OpenAI: Internal five-level model from Chatbots through Organizations.
LeCun: Disputes whether genuine general intelligence emerges from language models at all.

## Forecaster Groups

### AI Company Leaders (Jan 2025)
- 25% chance by 2026
- Strength: Direct visibility into next-gen systems
- Weakness: Selection bias, financial incentives to oversell

### Published AI Researchers (2023)
- ~25% chance by 2032 for "high-level machine intelligence"
- Median shortened 13 years between 2022-2023
- Some forecasts already falsified (Python coding prediction off by 2+ years)

### Metaculus (Feb 2026)
- 25% by 2029; 50% by 2033
- Strength: Strong track record on near-term events
- Caveat: Definition includes robotic capabilities

### Superforecasters (XPT 2022)
- 25% chance by 2047
- Predates ChatGPT — likely outdated

### Samotsvety (2023)
- ~28% by 2030; AI 2027 project forecast ~25% for "superhuman coders" by 2027

## Critical Observation

"Expert opinion can neither rule out nor rule in AGI soon." All methodologies contain limitations — definitional ambiguities, selection effects, incentive misalignment.
