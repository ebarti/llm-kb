# The DeepSeek Revolution

## DeepSeek V3.2
- 685 billion parameters, MIT licensing
- Gold-medal performance on International Math Olympiad
- Surpassed OpenAI's GPT-5-High on certain math benchmarks
- DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA): reduces inference costs by ~70%
- GPT-4 level performance at fraction of cost with full control

## Cost Revolution
- Competitive capability no longer requires proprietary APIs or prohibitive expenses
- Open models deliver frontier performance on modest hardware through quantization

## Distillation & Accessibility
- 32B distilled variant captures step-by-step reasoning abilities
- Enables advanced capabilities on modest hardware
- Expands ecosystem beyond GPU-rich organizations

## Hardware Independence
- Training DeepSeek V4 on Huawei Ascend chips rather than NVIDIA
- V4 targets 1 trillion total parameters with 32B active
- Native multimodality, 1M-token context window

## Landscape Transformation
- Chinese organizations released 1,500+ open LLMs by mid-2025
- Triggered competitive responses from Western firms
- Five independent open model families (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, Mistral) simultaneously reached frontier quality
- Shift toward efficiency, multilingual capability, and specialization over pure scale
