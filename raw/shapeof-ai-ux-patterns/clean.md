# The Shape of AI — UX Patterns for Artificial Intelligence Design

A comprehensive catalog of 57 AI UX design patterns organized into six categories. This is the most complete open taxonomy of interaction patterns for AI products available.

## WAYFINDERS — Helping users construct initial prompts and get started

1. **Gallery** — Share sample generations, prompts, and parameters to educate and inspire users.
2. **Follow up** — Get more information from the user when the initial prompt isn't sufficiently clear.
3. **Initial CTA** — Large, open-ended input inviting the user to start their first interaction with the AI.
4. **Nudges** — Alert users to actions they can take to use AI, especially if they are just getting started.
5. **Prompt details** — Show users what is actually happening behind the scenes.
6. **Randomize** — Kickstart the prompting experience with a low bar and fun results.
7. **Suggestions** — Solves the blank canvas dilemma with clues for how to prompt.
8. **Templates** — Structured templates that can be filled by the user or pre-filled by the AI.

## PROMPT ACTIONS — Different actions users direct AI to complete

9. **Auto-fill** — Extend a prompt to multiple fields or inputs at once.
10. **Chained action** — Sequential workflow execution.
11. **Describe** — Decomposes the course into its fundamental tokens and suggested prompt.
12. **Expand** — Lengthen the underlying content or add depth and details.
13. **Inline Action** — Ask or interact with AI contextually based on something already available on the page.
14. **Inpainting** — Target specific areas of the AI's result to regenerate or remix.
15. **Madlibs** — Repeatedly run generative tasks without compromising on the format or accuracy.
16. **Open input** — Open ended prompt inputs for AI conversations and natural language prompting.
17. **Regenerate** — Have the AI reproduce its response to the prompt without additional input.
18. **Restructure** — Use existing content as the starting point for prompting.
19. **Restyle** — Transfer styles without changing the underlying structure of a generation.
20. **Summary** — Have AI distill a topic or resource down to its essence.
21. **Synthesis** — Distill or reorganize complicated information into simple structure.
22. **Transform** — Use AI to change the modality of content.

## TUNERS — Adjusting contextual data and prompt refinement

23. **Attachments** — Give the AI a specific reference to anchor its response.
24. **Connectors** — Allow AI to reference external data and context.
25. **Filters** — Constrain the inputs or the outputs of the AI by source, type, modality, etc.
26. **Model management** — Let users specify what model to use for their prompts.
27. **Modes** — Adjust the underlying training, constraints, and persona to a specific context of use.
28. **Parameters** — Include constraints with your prompt for the AI to reference when generating its result.
29. **Preset styles** — Provide default options to change the texture, aesthetic, or tone of generative media.
30. **Prompt enhancer** — Improve prompt quality automatically.
31. **Saved styles** — Allow users to define their own style presets for re-use.
32. **Voice and tone** — Ensure outputs match your voice, tone, and preferences in a consistent way.

## GOVERNORS — Human-in-the-loop features maintaining oversight

33. **Action plan** — Have the AI show the steps it will take to respond before it executes its response.
34. **Branches** — Support iterative prompting while retaining footprints and visibility back to the original.
35. **Citations** — Lets the AI give inline annotations to cite its sources.
36. **Controls** — Manage the flow of information or pause a request mid-stream to adjust the prompt.
37. **Cost estimates** — Help users proactively modulate compute power through transparent cost estimates for actions.
38. **Draft mode** — Support exploration and iterative prompting while reducing compute costs until a final form is ready.
39. **Memory** — Control what details the AI knows about you.
40. **References** — See and manage what additional sources the AI references to generate its response.
41. **Sample response** — Confirm the user's intent for complicated prompts.
42. **Shared vision** — Live visibility into the AI's action in a shared canvas or workspace.
43. **Stream of Thought** — Reveals the AI's logic thought process, tool use, and decisions for oversight and auditability.
44. **Variations** — Trace through multiple variations of a result to choose the one that works best.
45. **Verification** — Allow users to confirm AI decisions and actions before proceeding.

## TRUST BUILDERS — Building confidence in ethical, accurate results

46. **Caveat** — Inform users about shortcomings or risks in the model or the technology overall.
47. **Consent** — Only capture data from others with their knowledge and permission.
48. **Data ownership** — Control how the model remembers and uses your data.
49. **Disclosure** — Clearly mark content and interactions guided or delivered by AI.
50. **Footprints** — Let users trace the AI's steps from prompt to result.
51. **Incognito Mode** — Interact with the AI outside of its memory.
52. **Watermark** — Identifiers on AI Generative content that humans, software, or programs can read.

## IDENTIFIERS — Distinct AI qualities at brand/model level

53. **Avatar** — Visual identifier of the AI itself to help it be recognizable, memorable, and on-brand.
54. **Color** — Visual cues to help users identify AI features or content.
55. **Iconography** — Images that represent AI-powered actions throughout the interface.
56. **Name** — How the AI is referred to through the product and across the overall customer experience.
57. **Personality** — Characteristics that distinguish the AI's personality and vibe.
