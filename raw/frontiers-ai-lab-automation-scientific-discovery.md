# AI, Agentic Models, and Lab Automation for Scientific Discovery

## Overview

Review article by Thomas Hartung examining the transformative impact of AI on scientific research, emphasizing the transition from AI as a "co-pilot" interpreting knowledge to a "lab-pilot" that actively conducts experiments.

## Key Domains of AI Impact

### Literature Synthesis and Meta-Analysis
AI systems now process vast research datasets at unprecedented scales. Platforms like SysRev have enabled over 16,000 systematic review projects in the last 5 years. These tools democratize access to cutting-edge research across geographical and institutional boundaries, though challenges remain regarding data quality and algorithmic bias.

### Hypothesis Generation
Language models integrate diverse data sources to propose novel research directions. DeepMind's AlphaFold-2 exemplifies this capability by predicting protein structures with near-experimental accuracy, earning a Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Researchers used deep neural networks to identify "halicin," a novel antibiotic scaffold, rejuvenating drug discovery pipelines.

### Autonomous Experimentation
GPT-4-driven agents now design, execute, and interpret multi-step chemical reactions independently. Insilico Medicine's AI-invented compound ISM001-055 advanced to Phase II clinical trials in 2024, marking the first AI-generated small molecule reaching this stage.

## Key Milestones

- AlphaFold-2: Nobel Prize in Chemistry for protein structure prediction
- Halicin: Novel antibiotic discovered by deep neural networks
- ISM001-055: First AI-generated drug to reach Phase II clinical trials
- A-Lab: Autonomous laboratory for solid-state synthesis of inorganic powders
- GNoME: DeepMind engine predicting stability of 380,000 unknown crystals

## Governance and Reproducibility

Alignment with emerging standards including the EU AI Act and ISO 42001. Critical concerns include:
- Reproducibility and auditability of AI-driven results
- Equitable access to advanced technologies
- Validation of AI-generated hypotheses
- Mitigation of algorithmic biases in training data

## Scientific Transition

The field is transitioning toward integrated systems where AI orchestrates both cloud software and physical laboratory hardware, fundamentally reshaping how research questions are formulated and answered.
