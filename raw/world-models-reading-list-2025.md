# World Models Reading List (2025)

## Foundational Papers
1. Sutton (1990) — Integrated Architectures for Learning, Planning, and Reacting: separating real vs imagined experience
2. Ha & Schmidhuber (2018) — World Models: VAE + recurrent dynamics + evolution strategies, training in latent representations
3. Ha & Schmidhuber (NeurIPS 2018) — Recurrent World Models: policy evolution in compact state representations

## Model-Based RL Breakthroughs
4. Hafner et al. (ICLR 2020) — Dream to Control (Dreamer): data-efficient imagined trajectory rollouts
5. Schrittwieser et al. (Nature 2020) — MuZero: plans via implicit learned dynamics without observation reconstruction
6. LeCun (2022) — A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence: proposes JEPA, representation-space prediction

## Contemporary Systems (2025)
7. Hafner et al. (Nature 2025) — DreamerV3: single algorithm, 150+ diverse tasks, Minecraft diamond from scratch
8. Assran et al. (2025) — V-JEPA 2: web-scale video pretraining → actionable world models, robotics transfer with minimal data

## Foundation Models (2025)
9. DeepMind — Genie 3: real-time interactive simulation, controllability
10. NVIDIA — Cosmos: physical alignment evaluation, dataset composition
11. OpenAI — Sora/Sora 2 (2024-2025): world simulators, physics understanding, improved controllability

## Domain Applications
12. Wayve — GAIA-2 (2025): autonomous driving simulation, scenario controllability, rare-event catalogs
13. Wayve — LINGO-2 (2024): natural language + control policies, safety evaluation

## Chronological Arc
Sutton (1990) → Ha/Schmidhuber (2018) → Dreamer series (2020-2025) → Foundation-scale video models (2024-2025)
Increasing emphasis on physical alignment, scalability, and real-world deployment.
