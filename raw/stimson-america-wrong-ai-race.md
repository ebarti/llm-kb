# America is Running the Wrong AI Race

## Core Thesis
Success in the AI race will not be determined by speed, but through public trust and safety. The article challenges the prevailing American approach to AI competition.

## Main Critiques

Rather than viewing AI development primarily as a speed competition against other nations, the author argues this framework misses crucial dimensions of what actually determines technological leadership and national advantage.

The framing of AI development as a "race" to be won fastest creates perverse incentives that conflict with building robust, trustworthy systems. Nations that prioritize responsible development and maintain public confidence may ultimately achieve greater strategic advantage than those pursuing pure speed metrics.

## Alternative Approach

The recommended path emphasizes:
- Public trust as a foundational element of sustainable AI development
- Safety considerations as central to long-term competitiveness rather than secondary concerns
- Recognition that rushing to deploy AI without adequate safeguards may undermine rather than enhance American technological dominance
- The need to reframe the competitive dynamic from pure speed to responsible capability building
