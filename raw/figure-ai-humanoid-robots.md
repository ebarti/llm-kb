# Figure AI

Founded 2022 by Brett Adcock (prev. Archer Aviation, Vettery). HQ: San Jose, CA. ~180 employees.

## Funding

- May 2023: $70M seed (Adcock invested $20M, Parkway Venture Capital)
- Feb 2024: $675M (Jeff Bezos, Microsoft, Nvidia, Intel, Amazon, OpenAI). Valuation: $2.6B
- Sep 2025: Series C >$1B. Valuation: $39B. (Brookfield, Macquarie, Qualcomm, Salesforce, T-Mobile)

## Robot Generations

**Figure 01** (2022): Bipedal for manual labor, logistics/warehousing, external cabling.

**Figure 02** (Aug 2024): 35 DOF, five-fingered hands with 16 DOF each, 25 kg carry capacity, 6 RGB cameras, onboard VLA model, dual GPUs (Nvidia), 3x computing power vs 01, microphones/speakers.

**Figure 03** (Oct 2025): Camera: doubled frame rate, 1/4 latency, 60% wider FOV. Hand-mounted cameras. Tactile sensors detecting forces as small as 3 grams. Soft materials, wireless inductive charging.

## OpenAI Partnership & Dissolution

Partnered Feb 2024; OpenAI developed specialized AI for language processing. Ended ~1 year later. Adcock: "large language models had become a smaller problem compared to those allowing for 'high rate robot control.'"

## Helix VLA System

- **Helix 01-02**: Controls up to 2 robots simultaneously. Enables interaction without extensive manual training. Figure 02 can "pick up nearly any small household object."
- **Helix 02** (Jan 2026): Full-body autonomy. Demonstrated loading/unloading dishwashers using motion-capture data + simulation ML.

## BotQ Manufacturing

Facility aiming to produce 12,000 humanoids per year with help of its own humanoid robots.

## Demonstrated Capabilities (2026)

Loading washing machines, sorting packages, folding laundry, loading/unloading dishwashers.

## Safety Concerns

Nov 2025: Former head of product safety sued Figure AI, alleging termination for raising concerns about robots "strong enough to fracture a human skull."
