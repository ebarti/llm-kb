# Nine AI Breakthroughs at UC San Diego

## Health Breakthroughs

1. **Alzheimer's**: PHGDH gene identified as a cause (not just biomarker) using AI 3D protein structure modeling.
2. **Tuberculosis**: MycoBCP tool accelerates TB drug discovery using deep learning + bacterial cytological profiling. First ML application of this kind to bacteria.
3. **Heart Monitoring**: Non-invasive monitoring of heart cell activity using AI reconstruction of intracellular signals from extracellular recordings.
4. **Breast Cancer**: Deep learning improves radiotherapy planning speed and reduces radiation dose errors to critical organs.
5. **Muscle Monitoring**: Wireless ultrasound patch + AI algorithm controls robotic arm via muscle signals.
6. **Ophthalmology**: Computer vision tools for eye care diagnostics.

## Climate Breakthroughs

7. **Climate Forecasting**: Spherical DYffusion model projects 100 years of climate patterns in 25 hours — 25x faster than current methods.
8. **Wildfire Detection**: ALERTCalifornia AI-powered camera network for fire detection, tracking, and predictive spread modeling (FIRIS system).

## Art

9. **Generative AI Art**: "Superradiance. Embodying Earth" multiscreen installation exploring AI as creative collaborator.
