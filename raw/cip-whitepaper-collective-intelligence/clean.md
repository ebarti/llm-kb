# The Collective Intelligence Project Whitepaper

**Authors:** Divya Siddarth, Saffron Huang
**Organization:** The Collective Intelligence Project (CIP)

## Organization & Purpose

CIP is an incubator for governance models addressing transformative technology through collective intelligence capabilities — decision-making systems that expand groups' capacity to achieve shared goals.

## Core Problem: The Transformative Technology Trilemma

CIP identifies an impossible choice facing technology governance:

### Three Failed Paths

1. **Capitalist Acceleration**: Prioritizes progress via markets; sacrifices safety and public oversight. Risk includes unchecked biotech/AI development.
2. **Authoritarian Technocracy**: Ensures safety through centralized control; sacrifices participation and democratic input. Example: surveillance-based pandemic response.
3. **Shared Stagnation**: Emphasizes participation and local control; sacrifices necessary technological progress and large-scale coordination.

## Proposed Solution: Collective Intelligence R&D

CIP proposes a fourth path encompassing progress, participation, AND safety through institutional innovation across two domains:

### I. Value Elicitation

Mechanisms for surfacing and aggregating diverse values:
- Quadratic voting and liquid democracy
- Deliberative platforms (Pol.is, SpeakEasy)
- AI-assisted tools for summarizing consensus across viewpoints
- Citizens' assemblies using digital platforms
- Prediction markets for collective forecasting

### II. Remaking Technology Institutions

Alternative organizational structures for technology development:
- "CI Corporation" model with capped returns
- Benefit corporations and cooperatives
- Decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs)
- Data intermediary institutions
- Standards-setting and auditing organizations

## Key Insight

"Humans created our current CI systems to help achieve collective goals. We can remake them."

## Implementation Strategy

CIP is piloting:
- Citizens' assemblies for AI alignment decisions
- Liquid democracy experiments for recommender systems
- Alternative funding models beyond venture capital
- Collaborative projects with aligned organizations (RadicalXChange, New Public, Consilience Project)

## Notable Projects

- **Collective Constitutional AI**: Partnership with Anthropic — representative sample of U.S. public fine-tuned Claude on collectively-defined principles
- **Alignment Assemblies**: Since February 2023, involving public in defining "good" for AI labs and governments
- **Global Dialogues**: 1,000 participants from 70+ countries every two months for conversations about AI's societal role

## Conclusion

CIP positions itself within an emerging ecosystem addressing governance innovation, emphasizing that institutional evolution is necessary to match technological capability with collective wisdom and democratic legitimacy.
