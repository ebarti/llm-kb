# AI Music Generation 2026

## Suno v5
Dominant platform with ~100M users, $2.4B+ valuation. V5 model: significant leap in vocal clarity, arrangement sophistication, and genre accuracy. Suno Studio: timeline-based editor with stem separation (up to 12 tracks), MIDI export for DAW integration. Output at 44.1kHz. Pricing: Free (10 songs/day), Pro ($10/mo, 500 songs), Premier ($30/mo, 2000 songs). Strength: natural vocal synthesis, excellent for pop, rock, R&B with complex lyrical phrasing.

## Udio
Founded by ex-Google DeepMind researchers. Launched April 2024. Standout: inpainting tool to select and regenerate specific song sections. Settled with UMG (Oct 2025) and WMG (Nov 2025). Pricing mirrors Suno ($10-30/mo). Disabled download functionality Oct 2025, not restored as of March 2026. Audio fidelity rated "almost indistinguishable from real recordings."

## ElevenLabs Music
Launched August 2025, leveraging $11B parent company's voice synthesis expertise. Vocals described as "unsettling in their realism" with superior breath, vibrato, emotional inflection. Songs up to 4 minutes, multiple languages. Fewer editing tools than competitors.

## Specialized Platforms
- AIVA: 15-49 EUR/mo, orchestral/cinematic, built-in MIDI editor, first AI composer recognized by France's SACEM. No vocals.
- Boomy: $10-30/mo, distribution to 40+ streaming platforms, 20% royalty cut. Quality step below leaders.
- Soundraw: $10-50/mo, trains exclusively on in-house productions (zero copyright risk), instrumental only.
- Meta MusicGen: Fully open-source, self-hosted, instrumental only.
- Google MusicFX: Free via Google Labs, 70-second limit, real-time DJ mode.

## Legal Landscape
Warner settled with Suno, UMG settled with Udio. Both forming partnerships with major labels, legitimizing AI music generation.
