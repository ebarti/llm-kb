# AI Arms Race Has Real Numbers: Pentagon vs China 2026

## Key Military AI Systems & Spending

**Pentagon Infrastructure:**
- **Project Maven**: $480 million Army contract (May 2024) for Maven Smart System deployed across five combatant commands
- **GenAI.mil**: Pentagon-wide platform accessible to all military and civilian DoD employees; xAI's Grok models integrated by December 2025 at classified information levels
- **Operational Partners**: Palantir (primary contractor), OpenAI (recent procurement shift), xAI

## Operational Performance: Operation Epic Fury (Feb 28 - Mar 5, 2026)
- 900 strikes executed in 12 hours (previously required days/weeks of planning)
- 24 of Iran's 31 provinces struck
- Iranian countermeasures: 165 ballistic missiles, 541 drones, 2 cruise missiles intercepted by UAE
- 6 American soldiers killed; airports and Amazon data centers damaged

## Pentagon vs China Comparison

**U.S. Advantages:**
- Years of classified deployment infrastructure and operational doctrine refined through live-fire conflicts (Ukraine, Gaza, Iran)
- Integrated systems (Maven, GenAI.mil) across unified command structures
- Operational feedback loop strengthening systems continuously

**China's Position:**
- Georgetown CSET review found PLA building AI decision-support systems to compensate for perceived command weaknesses
- DeepSeek efficiency advantage: capable reasoning models requiring significantly less compute
- Huawei domestically-produced chip integration ("algorithmic sovereignty")
- December 2025 Pentagon report: "performance gap had narrowed"
- **Critical Gap**: Zero combat testing; PLA's last conflict was 1979

## AI Weapons Systems Identified

**U.S. Deployed Systems:**
- **Gospel**: Generates target building lists
- **Lavender**: Identifies individual combatants across hierarchy levels
- **"Where's Daddy"**: Tracks targets' phones to locations
- Maven Smart System: Fuses satellite imagery, geolocation, communications intercepts

**Chinese Development:**
- PLA-linked researchers (March 2025) described "fully autonomous execution of combat decisions" as development goal

## Strategic Implications

The U.S. advantage is "real" but "not permanent." Anthropic's Pentagon removal during active strikes exposed infrastructure fragility when ethical constraints clash with military operational needs. Compared to Britain's Chain Home radar (1940)—advantage lay in integrated system, not isolated technology, requiring years to replicate.
