# AI-Discovered Drugs Reach Phase III

## Current Pipeline Status

Over 173 AI-discovered drug programs are in clinical development as of early 2026, with 15-20 expected to enter pivotal Phase III trials during the year.

## Leading Companies & Their Approaches

### Insilico Medicine
- Developing rentosertib (ISM001-055) for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
- Uses generative chemistry to identify both disease targets and molecular compounds.
- Achieved 30-month timeline from target to Phase I (versus traditional 6-8 years).
- Phase IIa results: lung function improvement of 98.4 mL vs placebo decline of 62.3 mL.

### Schrödinger
- Developing zasocitinib (TAK-279) for psoriasis and inflammatory conditions.
- Employs physics-based AI combining quantum mechanics with machine learning.
- Already in Phase III trials; results expected 2026.

### Recursion-Exscientia (merged July 2025)
- Combined pipeline includes 10+ clinical and preclinical programs.
- Recursion uses phenomics (high-throughput biological imaging: 2.2 million experiments weekly).
- Near-term 2026 readouts: REC-394 (C. difficile infection) and REC-1245 (lymphoma degrader).
- Notable failure: REC-994 discontinued May 2025 after disappointing efficacy data.

## Clinical Evidence & Failures

- AI-discovered compounds show Phase I success rates of 80-90%, significantly higher than the 52% historical average.
- Setbacks: compounds failed in 2023 trials for atopic dermatitis, schizophrenia, and cancer.
- First AI-designed drug approval projected for 2026-2027 with approximately 60% probability.

## Regulatory Framework

- FDA published draft guidance in January 2025 establishing risk-based credibility assessment for AI in drug development.
- Joint FDA-EMA guiding principles released January 2026.
- Final guidance expected 2026.
