# 2025 LLM Year in Review

Published December 31, 2025 by Andrej Karpathy on Bear Blog.

## Major Paradigm Shifts Identified

### 1. Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)
New dominant training stage replacing SFT+RLHF formula. Models develop emergent reasoning strategies by optimizing against objective reward functions in math/coding environments. Enables "test time compute" scaling through longer reasoning traces.

### 2. Ghosts vs. Animals / Jagged Intelligence
LLMs should be understood as "summoning ghosts" rather than evolving biological intelligence. Models display dramatically uneven capabilities — genius in some domains, grade-school level in others. This jagged profile reflects unique optimization pressures (text imitation, puzzle rewards, arena upvotes) vs. human survival-based evolution.

### 3. Cursor as New LLM App Layer
Demonstrates emerging middleware that bundles LLM orchestration, context engineering, multi-call DAGs, domain-specific UIs, and autonomy sliders. Karpathy predicts LLM labs graduate generalists while apps specialize them into "deployed professionals."

### 4. Claude Code: Localhost-First Agents
LLM agents operating on personal computers with direct access to private data and environment. Contrasts favorably against cloud-based approaches — emphasizes low-latency local interaction over centralized infrastructure.

### 5. Vibe Coding
Programming accessible to non-professionals through English descriptions. Democratizes software creation while empowering experts to build ephemeral applications.

### 6. Nano Banana / LLM GUI Evolution
Future LLM interface should communicate visually through images, infographics, and spatial layouts rather than text-based 1980s command-line paradigm.

## Critical Observations
- **Benchmark Skepticism**: "General apathy and loss of trust in benchmarks" — verifiable environments immediately susceptible to RLVR optimization
- **Capability Paradox**: Simultaneously rapid progress AND substantial remaining work
- **Unrealized Potential**: Industry has realized "nowhere near 10% of their potential even at present capability"
