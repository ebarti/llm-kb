# PKM Systems Comparison: Obsidian vs Logseq vs Notion (2026)

## Architecture Overview

- **Obsidian**: Local-first markdown model prioritizing user control and offline access
- **Logseq**: Open-source, block-based architecture for hierarchical note structuring
- **Notion**: Cloud-based all-in-one workspace designed for team collaboration

## Core Feature Comparison

| Feature | Obsidian | Logseq | Notion |
|---------|----------|--------|--------|
| Data Storage | Local files | Local files | Cloud-based |
| Syncing | Manual (3rd-party tools) | Manual (Git/Dropbox) | Automatic/real-time |
| Zettelkasten Support | Native bidirectional linking | Limited block-based approach | No support |
| Query System | Plugin-dependent (Dataview) | Advanced with metadata | Database filters only |
| Collaboration | Not supported natively | Not supported | Real-time enabled |
| AI Integration | Plugin-based (100+ AI extensions) | Limited | Notion AI (Business tier) |
| Privacy Level | High (local control) | High (local control) | Moderate (cloud-dependent) |

## Performance Metrics

- Obsidian: 95% responsiveness, under 200ms latency with 20,000 notes
- Logseq: 90% responsiveness, 250ms latency
- Notion: 98% responsiveness for real-time syncing, 70% responsiveness offline

## Key 2025-2026 Updates

**Obsidian:**
- Introduced Bases (November 2025): native database feature with table and list views
- Removed commercial license requirement in early 2025 — free for business use
- 2,700+ community plugins, 100+ AI-related extensions
- 1.5 million active users with 22% YoY growth

**Notion:**
- Removed AI add-on, moved AI to Business tier ($20/mo) in May 2025
- Released Notion Agents (September 2025): autonomous multi-step actions
- Plus plan ($10/mo) only gives 20 AI trials

**Logseq:**
- v1.5.0: improved query systems and refined knowledge graph visualization
- AGPL licensing ensures community forks can survive

## Core Philosophies

- Obsidian: permanence in Markdown files, extensibility via plugins
- Notion: collaboration, compliance, and conversational AI
- Logseq: openness, graphs, and survival beyond the company

## Use Case Recommendations

- Obsidian: Individual scholars, academics, deep knowledge curators, solo knowledge workers
- Logseq: Developers and knowledge workers requiring structured outlining with visual graphs
- Notion: Teams needing centralized collaboration, project management, real-time sync

## Pricing

- Obsidian: Free for personal AND business use. Optional Sync ($5/mo), Publish ($10/mo)
- Notion: Free tier, Plus ($10/mo), Business ($20/mo with AI)
- Logseq: Free and open-source
