# AI Economics: Market Size, Investment, and GDP Impact (2026)

## Market Size

Gartner forecasts worldwide AI spending at $2.52 trillion in 2026, a 44% year-over-year increase. Rising to $3.3 trillion by 2029.
UN Trade and Development projects the global AI market growing from $189B (2023) to $4.8 trillion by 2033 — a 25x increase in a decade.
Hardware and infrastructure accounts for ~59% of total AI spending.

## Capital Investment / Infrastructure

Goldman Sachs estimates AI companies will invest $527B+ in CapEx for 2026 (up from $465B at start of Q3 2025 earnings).
Amazon, Google, Meta, and Microsoft combined: ~$700B on AI infrastructure in 2026.
Morgan Stanley: Nearly $3 trillion of AI-related infrastructure investment will flow through global economy by 2028, with 80%+ still ahead.

## GDP and Productivity Impact

St. Louis Fed: AI has surpassed IT's contribution to real GDP growth during the dot-com boom.
Net AI-related investment contribution: 40-50 basis points, or 20-25% of real GDP growth (Q1-Q3 2025).
Dallas Fed: AI could boost US productivity by 1.5% annually over the next decade, with measurable GDP impact starting 2027.
Long-term estimates: +1.5% GDP by 2035, +3% by 2055, +3.7% by 2075.
80% of increase in final private domestic demand (H1 2025) attributable to data centers and high-tech spending.

## Energy Demand

IEA: Data centers consumed ~415 TWh in 2024 (1.5% of global electricity).
Projected to double to ~945 TWh by 2030 (3% of global electricity).
AI-driven accelerated servers growing at 30% annually.
US data centers: 183 TWh in 2024 (4%+ of US electricity), projected 426 TWh by 2030 (133% growth).
AI data centers could need 68 GW of power capacity by 2027 — near California's entire current capacity.
Goldman Sachs: Data center-driven electricity demand will boost core inflation by 0.1% in both 2026 and 2027.

## GPU and Chip Supply

Data-center GPUs sold out: lead times 36-52 weeks.
NVIDIA Rubin entering full production, volume shipments H2 2026.
Production ceiling: ~200,000-300,000 Rubin GPUs for 2026.
Memory chip shortage: AI consumes 70% of DRAM, prices up 20%.
HBM chips nearly sold out until 2026.

## The Revenue Gap Question

Despite massive investment, the question of whether AI revenue will justify CapEx remains open. CNBC noted that consumption, not AI investment, was the biggest driver of US GDP growth in 2025. The ratio of AI spending to AI revenue generated remains a key concern for investors.

## Post-Training Scaling Paradigm

The meaning of "scaling" has shifted from training scale to inference scale (test-time compute). Models like OpenAI o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1 spend more compute at generation time. The field increasingly asks: "Which scaling metrics translate into durable economic utility?" rather than "does scaling reduce loss?"
