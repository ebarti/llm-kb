# Small Law Firm AI Guide: Using LLMs in 2026

## Overview

Practical guide addressing how small law firms can leverage LLMs to enhance efficiency, save time, reduce costs, and minimize human error.

## Three Primary Use Cases

### 1. Document Summarization and Classification
LLMs automate traditionally paralegal-heavy tasks. Tools like Everlaw and Luminance can rapidly and accurately summarize large volumes of documents, enabling lawyers to identify critical information quickly. Documents are automatically categorized for streamlined organization and retrieval.

### 2. Document Drafting
AI legal assistants such as Gavel Exec help create initial drafts of legal documents including pleadings. These tools operate directly within Word, offering a risk-free way to draft, redline, and analyze documents. Attorneys focus on refinement rather than initial creation.

### 3. Legal Research and Case Identification
Platforms like Casetext and Westlaw Edge access extensive legal databases to identify relevant cases, statutes, and opinions. Small firms gain access to current legal research for building stronger arguments.

## Adoption Context

A 2024 Clio study revealed AI adoption among legal professionals surged from 19% in 2023 to 79% in 2024. LLMs are emerging as supplementary tools supporting efficiency and enabling practitioners to dedicate more time to analysis, strategic advice, and client-focused work.

## Key Tools
- Gavel Exec: contract review and drafting
- Everlaw: document analysis and e-discovery
- Luminance: AI-powered legal document review
- Casetext: legal research and case identification
- Westlaw Edge: legal research with AI insights

## Ethical Considerations

LLMs serve as supplementary tools rather than replacements. Ethical concerns include systemic bias, hallucination, and misinformation, with real-world incidents demonstrating dangers of unsupervised reliance on these tools.
