# AI Pioneers Win 2024 Nobel Prizes

## Nobel Prize in Physics 2024

**John J. Hopfield** and **Geoffrey E. Hinton** were awarded the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics for developing machine learning technology using artificial neural networks.

- Hopfield: Hopfield networks (1982), associative memory model based on spin glass physics.
- Hinton: Boltzmann machines, backpropagation popularization, deep learning foundations. Known as the "Godfather of AI."

## Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2024

Awarded in two halves:
1. **David Baker** (University of Washington): Protein design using computational methods for over 20 years. Created Rosetta software suite, RFdiffusion.
2. **Demis Hassabis** and **John M. Jumper** (Google DeepMind): Developed AlphaFold 2, solving the 50-year protein structure prediction challenge.

## Significance

- First time AI work has been recognized by Nobel Prizes in both Physics and Chemistry in the same year.
- Demonstrates AI's fundamental contribution to scientific understanding.
- Hassabis' and Jumper's AlphaFold 2 was made possible by Hopfield's and Hinton's foundational work on neural networks.
- Sparked debate about whether AI is physics or chemistry, reflecting how deeply AI has penetrated the sciences.

## Impact

- AlphaFold has been used by 3+ million researchers in 190+ countries.
- Baker's protein design work led to RFdiffusion, RFdiffusion3, enabling de novo protein design.
- These innovations are transforming drug discovery, materials science, and fundamental biology.
