# Generative AI Enhances Individual Creativity but Reduces Collective Diversity

## Core Finding

Access to generative AI ideas causes stories to be evaluated as more creative, better written, and more enjoyable, especially among less creative writers. However, generative AI-enabled stories are more similar to each other than stories by humans alone.

## The Creativity Paradox

With generative AI, writers are individually better off, but collectively a narrower scope of novel content is produced. This represents a fundamental tension: AI amplifies individual output quality while homogenizing collective output.

## Key Implications

### For Individual Creators
- Less creative writers benefit disproportionately from AI assistance
- Stories written with AI are rated as higher quality by evaluators
- AI acts as an equalizer, raising the floor of creative output

### For Collective Culture
- AI-assisted content converges on similar patterns and styles
- Diversity of ideas and expression narrows when everyone uses similar tools
- The cultural ecosystem may become less varied even as individual pieces improve

### For Knowledge Work
- Same dynamic applies beyond creative writing to research, analysis, and ideation
- Organizations using AI for brainstorming may generate better individual ideas but less diverse portfolios
- Deliberate strategies needed to maintain intellectual diversity

## Relevance to LLM Applications Beyond Code

This research directly addresses the expanding frontier of LLM creative applications. The finding that LLMs are individually empowering but collectively homogenizing has implications for every domain where LLMs are deployed for knowledge manipulation: research synthesis, educational content, legal analysis, and scientific writing.
