# AI Video Market After Sora (2026)

## Sora's Shutdown
OpenAI discontinued Sora on March 24, 2026 due to unsustainable economics: ~$15M/day compute costs vs $2.1M lifetime revenue. Downloads fell 67% Nov 2025 to Feb 2026. Active users below 500K. Per-clip cost ~$1.30. Disney withdrew planned $1B partnership. OpenAI pivoting to enterprise productivity ahead of 2026-2027 IPO.

## Four-Tier Market Structure

### Quality-First: Runway Gen-4
- Character persistence via reference imagery
- Act-Two motion capture for realistic character animation
- In-video editing via Aleph interface
- 1080p, 10-second clips, 5 credits/second (Turbo)

### Cost-Efficiency: Kling 3.0 (ByteDance)
- $0.07/second -- 65% cheaper than Sora
- Multi-shot sequences (2-6 scenes) with automatic transitions
- Native audio with dialogue and lip sync
- Character lock consistency across scenes
- 720p-1080p, up to 15 seconds

### Ecosystem: Google Veo 3
- Native synchronized audio generation (effects, dialogue, ambient)
- Veo 3.1 Lite: $0.05/second
- Full Veo 3: $0.35-0.50/second
- 4K output potential, YouTube and Vertex AI integration

### Creative: Pika 2.5
- Pikaformance converts voice/image into performances
- 1080p, 10-second clips
- Optimized for TikTok/Reels viral content
