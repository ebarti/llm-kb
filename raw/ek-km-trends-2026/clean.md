# Top Knowledge Management Trends for 2026

Zach Wahl, CEO of Enterprise Knowledge, presents the annual KM trends analysis based on client interviews, RFP analysis, conference attendance, and industry expert input. Eight major trends shaping knowledge management in 2026.

## 1. KM and Semantic Layers Powering Enterprise AI

Organizations are moving beyond stalled AI pilots by implementing semantic layers paired with knowledge management expertise. Rather than generic AI fixes, companies now seek specific semantic layer implementations. KM professionals should occupy central roles in AI initiatives, addressing knowledge gaps and detecting AI hallucinations.

## 2. Boxed AI Versus Built AI

Organizations face competing approaches: quick-deploy "plug-and-play" black-box solutions versus customized, context-aware AI systems. Comparative evaluations increasingly favor built solutions, which offer superior organizational context, advanced reasoning capabilities, and explainable results.

## 3. Scaling AI-Ready Knowledge Assets

AI enables automated standardization and enhancement of legacy content and data at enterprise scale. Rather than requiring thousands of manual hours, AI can now accomplish metadata enrichment and quality improvement in minutes, benefiting both AI systems and existing legacy platforms.

## 4. Enterprise-Level Tacit Knowledge Capture

AI note-taking tools, automated transcription, and digital meeting platforms now support enterprise-wide experiential knowledge programs. KM professionals guide dialogue structure and output validation while automation handles bulk work, with semantic layers identifying knowledge gaps and measuring impact.

## 5. From Enterprise Search to Conversational AI

Traditional search transitions toward conversational interfaces delivering integrated answers across multiple knowledge assets. User training from ChatGPT and similar tools creates expectations for plain-language question-and-answer interactions rather than indexed search results.

## 6. Knowledge Generation and Sharing Flattening

As AI automates report generation and analysis, senior leaders gain direct access to insights but lose expert analysis layering. Organizations must identify at-risk human expertise and embed it within semantic layers and AI solutions.

## 7. Data Adopting KM Principles

Structured data professionals increasingly recognize ontologies, controlled vocabularies, and metadata value — concepts long central to KM. This convergence supports the "knowledge assets" framework encompassing both structured and unstructured information.

## 8. Organizational Change from Enterprise AI

AI deployment drives structural changes including reduced entry-level staffing, emergence of AI Governance departments, and consolidation of traditional management groups into knowledge asset product teams. KM professionals positioned to demonstrate value claim key governance roles.

## Key Insights

The article emphasizes that KM trends from previous years (2019's knowledge graphs, 2020's taxonomy importance, 2024's semantic layers) have proven accurate and influential industry-wide.
