# Microsoft Research: What Will Shape AI in 2026?

## Overview

Microsoft Research identifies 20 major research frontiers shaping AI in 2026, moving from incremental gains to transformative scale. The emphasis is on reconstructing core computing principles rather than grafting AI onto existing frameworks.

## Key Research Areas

### Scientific Discovery
Peter Lee envisions that "every research scientist has AI lab assistants that suggest and run parts of experiments." AI will generate hypotheses, control experiments, and collaborate with human colleagues.

### Biological AI
Ava Amini's team treats biology as a language using generative models. Key projects:
- EvoDiff: designing proteins never seen in nature from billions of sequences
- Project Ex Vivo: bridging computation and experimentation for cancer cell targeting

### Precision Healthcare
Hoifung Poon aims to decode "the language of patients" through multimodal AI integrating radiology, pathology, and genomics. Goal: creating "virtual patients" — digital twins simulating disease progression and treatment responses.

### Advanced Reasoning
Jianfeng Gao describes shifting from encoding world knowledge via language models to developing reasoning through environmental interaction. Future systems include simulation (world models) and mentalizing (understanding human mental states).

### Inclusive Innovation
Tanuja Ganu focuses on designing systems serving underserved populations — rural teachers, farmers, health workers — through multilingual support and context-aware interfaces.

### Interactive Storytelling
Katja Hofmann envisions interactive storytelling where audiences co-create narratives with AI, moving beyond passive consumption toward "shared spaces where creativity and collaboration flourish."

### Agentic Media
Yan Lu proposes agentic media — interactive communication channels that sustain context and adapt to different audiences, transforming static documents into dynamic environments.

### Autonomous Economic Systems
Saleema Amershi explores agent-to-agent negotiation in digital markets via the Magentic Marketplace simulation framework.

### Spatial & Physical Intelligence
Baining Guo identifies four trends: scalable 3D datasets, foundation models for spatial reasoning, embodied AI, and world models. Ashley Llorens emphasizes "vision-language-action" models for robots.

### Psychological Well-being
Jina Suh advocates embedding psychological stewardship into AI design, mitigating risks of dependency and social fragmentation.

### AI Companionship
Xing Xie treats AI as trusted companions incorporating psychometric evaluation. Projects like Value Compass explore cultural value diversity.

### Clinical Agentic Systems
Xinxing Xu describes multimodal foundation models integrating clinical notes, medical images, and genomics for context-rich reasoning with clinician-in-the-loop validation.

## Unifying Themes

All research emphasizes collaboration over replacement, responsible scaling, measuring societal impact, and bridging capability gaps for underserved populations.
