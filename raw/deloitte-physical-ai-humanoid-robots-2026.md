# Physical AI and Humanoid Robots — Deloitte Tech Trends 2026

Physical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems enabling machines to autonomously perceive, understand, reason about, and interact with the physical world in real time. Unlike traditional robots following preprogrammed instructions, physical AI systems perceive their environment, learn from experience, and adapt behavior based on real-time data.

## Technology Enablers

**Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models**: Integrate computer vision, NLP, and motor control. Help robots interpret surroundings and select appropriate actions. Adapt training methods from LLMs while incorporating physical world data.

**Onboard Computing**: Neural processing units enable low-latency, energy-efficient real-time AI processing directly on robots without cloud dependency. Essential for autonomous vehicles, industrial robotics, and remote surgery.

**Training Approaches**:
- Reinforcement learning: trial-and-error with rewards/penalties
- Imitation learning: mimicking expert demonstrations
- Blended: simulation-based RL + targeted physical fine-tuning

## Implementation Barriers

1. **Sim-to-real gap**: "A robot might learn to grab something in simulation, but when it enters physical space, it's not a one-to-one match."
2. **AI Safety**: Smallest error rates can cascade across production runs; hallucinations in physical world
3. **Regulatory**: Overlapping, sometimes contradictory requirements across jurisdictions
4. **Human Acceptance**: Worker concerns about job displacement
5. **Cybersecurity**: New attack surfaces bridging digital and physical domains
6. **Fleet Orchestration**: Heterogeneous robot fleets from multiple vendors
